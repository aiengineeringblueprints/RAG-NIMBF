## Relationship history

Rosen Pharma ApS is a pharmaceuticals company based in Denmark. The relationship started with a small pilot installation and has expanded to several plants since then.

During the first years Rosen Pharma ApS mainly bought standard products. Later projects added integration services and customer training.

Rosen Pharma ApS uses Kestrel equipment mainly for monitoring and quality assurance in its pharmaceuticals operations.

Rosen Pharma ApS values short response times and local support, which is why the account is served by a dedicated account manager.

## Support contract

In 2023 Rosen Pharma ApS upgraded its support contract from the Bronze tier to the Silver tier. Before the upgrade, the SLA response time was 48 hours; it is now 24 hours.
