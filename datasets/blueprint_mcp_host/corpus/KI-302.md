## Relationship history

Lundby Bygg Inc. uses Kestrel equipment mainly for monitoring and quality assurance in its construction operations.

Lundby Bygg Inc. is a construction company based in United States. The relationship started with a small pilot installation and has expanded to several plants since then.

Over the years Lundby Bygg Inc. has standardised several measuring points on Kestrel products, which simplifies spare parts and training on their side.

## Contract history

In 2024 Lundby Bygg Inc. upgraded its support contract from the Bronze tier to the Silver tier. Before the upgrade, the SLA response time was 48 hours; it is now 24 hours.
