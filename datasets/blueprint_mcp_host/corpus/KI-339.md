Falkberg Foods GmbH uses Kestrel equipment mainly for monitoring and quality assurance in its food processing operations.

Falkberg Foods GmbH values short response times and local support, which is why the account is served by a dedicated account manager.

## Contract history

In 2021 Falkberg Foods GmbH upgraded its support contract from the Bronze tier to the Gold tier. Before the upgrade, the SLA response time was 48 hours; it is now 8 hours.
