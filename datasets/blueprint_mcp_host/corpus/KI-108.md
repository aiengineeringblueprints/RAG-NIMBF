# Incident Response Procedure

Kestrel Instruments, internal document.

## Purpose of this policy

The Incident Response Procedure turns legal requirements and company values into practical rules. Where local law sets stricter requirements, local law takes precedence.

## Terms used

- **Manager**: an employee with formal responsibility for a team or cost center
- **Working day**: a weekday that is not a public holiday at the employee's site
- **Employee**: a person with an employment contract with Kestrel Instruments
- **Site**: any office, plant, lab or warehouse operated by Kestrel Instruments
- **Contractor**: a person working for Kestrel through a service agreement
- **Intranet**: the internal information portal of Kestrel Instruments

## Scope

Temporary staff and interns follow the Incident Response Procedure in the same way as permanent employees.

## Requirements

1. Severity 1 incidents must receive a first response within 15 minutes.
2. Severity 2 incidents must receive a first response within 4 hours.
3. A written postmortem is due within 5 working days after a Severity 1 incident.
