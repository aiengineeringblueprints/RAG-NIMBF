# Incident Report INC-2026-020

Kestrel Instruments. Internal use only.

## Incident summary

This report documents incident INC-2026-020 at Ostrand Foods AB. It follows the blameless postmortem format and focuses on causes and improvements, not on individuals.

Incident INC-2026-020 interrupted measurements at Ostrand Foods AB and was handled by customer support together with engineering.

Incident INC-2026-020 occurred on 19 July 2026 at Ostrand Foods AB. The affected product was the Kestrel KD2 and the incident was classified as severity 3.

## How the incident was detected

Monitoring at Ostrand Foods AB raised a communication alarm, which led to the investigation of incident INC-2026-020.

Incident INC-2026-020 was detected when Ostrand Foods AB noticed missing values in the control system and opened a support case.

## Root cause analysis

Root cause analysis identified a clock synchronisation error.

## Customer communication

Ostrand Foods AB received status updates from customer support throughout incident INC-2026-020.

After incident INC-2026-020 the account manager held a review meeting with Ostrand Foods AB to explain causes and actions.

A written summary of incident INC-2026-020 was sent to Ostrand Foods AB together with this report.

## Other factors

- The fault was first mistaken for a network problem
- The affected installation was not on the latest firmware
- Monitoring did not alert on the first symptoms
- Documentation of the installation was incomplete

## Actions

| Action | Owner | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Ship the fix with firmware 4.0.8 | Engineering | done |
| Roll out the fix to all affected installations | Engineering | done |
| Review spare part stock for key customers | Logistics | in progress |

The fix shipped with firmware 4.0.8.

## Follow-up

Similar installations at other customers were checked for the conditions that led to incident INC-2026-020.

The knowledge base was updated with the diagnosis steps used during incident INC-2026-020.

The effectiveness of the corrective actions for incident INC-2026-020 will be checked in the next quality review.

## Lessons learned

- Remote diagnostics access was essential
- Checklists need regular review after incidents
- Early escalation shortened the investigation
