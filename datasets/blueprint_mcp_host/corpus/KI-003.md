# Kestrel KH7 Datasheet

Kestrel Instruments. Internal use only.

## Introduction

The Kestrel KH7 is a humidity sensor sold under SKU KS-1003. It was launched in 2023.

Every Kestrel KH7 leaves the factory with an individual test report. The report documents the final functional test and the configuration loaded before shipment, which simplifies acceptance testing on site.

Its operating temperature range is -30 °C to 100 °C; the supply current is 22 mA.

## Transport and storage

Transport the Kestrel KH7 only in packaging that protects it against shocks; damage caused by unsuitable packaging is not covered by the warranty.

## Mechanical installation

Orient the cable entries of the Kestrel KH7 downwards when installing outdoors, so that condensation cannot collect in the terminal compartment.

## Electrical connection

The terminals of the Kestrel KH7 accept solid and stranded conductors. Use ferrules for stranded wire and tighten the terminal screws with a suitable screwdriver.

## Care and maintenance

Recalibrate the Kestrel KH7 according to the calibration procedure of Kestrel Instruments or send it to an accredited calibration lab.

## FAQ

**Q:** Can the Kestrel KH7 be mounted next to a frequency converter?
**A:** Keep as much distance as possible and use shielded cables to limit electromagnetic interference.

**Q:** Can I replace the sensing element of the Kestrel KH7 myself?
**A:** Only trained service staff should replace it, because the device must be recalibrated afterwards.
