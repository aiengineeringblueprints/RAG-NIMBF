# Training Course: Firmware Update Workshop

Kestrel Instruments, internal document.

## Description

Firmware Update Workshop: 3 days, trainer Tuva Mortensen.

The course Firmware Update Workshop gives participants the practical knowledge they need in daily work with Kestrel products.

Participants of Firmware Update Workshop work in small groups, so trainers can respond to individual questions.

It accepts at most 11 participants and costs 1850 EUR per person.

The course has no prerequisites.

## What you will learn

- Diagnose typical faults
- Configure devices with the Kestrel Configurator
- Apply the relevant safety rules
- Plan maintenance tasks

## Course materials

Participants of Firmware Update Workshop receive the course slides and exercise descriptions in digital form.

Demo devices and tools for Firmware Update Workshop are provided by Kestrel; participants do not need to bring equipment apart from a laptop.

## Target audience

Firmware Update Workshop is aimed at technicians and engineers who install, configure or maintain Kestrel products.

Participants of Firmware Update Workshop should be familiar with basic measurement technology.

Customers, partners and Kestrel employees can register for Firmware Update Workshop.

## Certification

Participants who do not pass the assessment of Firmware Update Workshop can repeat it at a later date free of charge.
