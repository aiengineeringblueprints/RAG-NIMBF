# Project Brief PRJ-217: Birch Metering Retrofit

Kestrel Instruments, internal document.

## Introduction

Project PRJ-217, Birch Metering Retrofit, is delivered for Halland Energi AS.

Project PRJ-217 replaces manual readings with continuous monitoring, so that Halland Energi AS can react to deviations faster.

Halland Energi AS commissioned project PRJ-217 to modernise measurement and monitoring at one of its sites. The existing equipment had reached the end of its service life.

## Scope

The scope of project PRJ-217 covers delivery, installation and commissioning of Kestrel KQ1 devices and their integration into the customer's control system.

Civil works and cabling outside the measuring points are not part of project PRJ-217 and are provided by Halland Energi AS.
