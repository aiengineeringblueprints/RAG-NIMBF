# Oslo Headquarters

Kestrel Instruments. Internal use only.

## About the site

The Oslo Headquarters in Oslo, Norway, is headed by Emilie Moe, Chief Executive Officer.

Teams at the Oslo Headquarters in Oslo collaborate daily with colleagues at other sites through shared tools and regular video meetings.

The Oslo Headquarters is part of the Kestrel Instruments network of sites and works closely with the headquarters and the other locations.

The Oslo Headquarters has grown steadily since it opened and is an important part of the company's presence in Norway.

## Facts and figures

- The headquarters is located at Fjordveien 18 in Oslo.
- About 240 employees work at the Oslo headquarters.
- The Oslo headquarters hosts finance, product management and IT.

## Facilities

- Quiet rooms for focused work
- Meeting rooms with video conferencing equipment
- Secure storage for test equipment
- Bicycle parking and showers
- Staff canteen and coffee corners

## Getting there

The Oslo Headquarters can be reached by public transport from the city center of Oslo. Parking for visitors is available next to the main entrance.

## History

Over the years the Oslo Headquarters has taken on additional responsibilities and now supports colleagues and customers across the region.

The Oslo Headquarters was renovated recently to provide modern workplaces and better meeting rooms.

## Sustainability

Energy consumption of the Oslo Headquarters is monitored with Kestrel devices and reviewed every year.

Employees at the Oslo Headquarters are encouraged to commute by public transport or bicycle.

The Oslo Headquarters uses renewable electricity and separates waste for recycling.

## Emergency information

Contractors working at the Oslo Headquarters receive a site-specific safety briefing before they start.

First aiders and fire wardens are appointed at the Oslo Headquarters; their names are posted on the notice boards.

Emergency exits and assembly points of the Oslo Headquarters are marked on the floor plans next to every staircase.

## Who works here

- Facilities
- IT support
- Management and administration
- Quality assurance
- Engineering and testing
- Customer support

## Visiting the site

Visitors to the Oslo Headquarters register at the reception and receive a badge that must be worn at all times. Safety instructions are handed out during registration.

Guests of the Oslo Headquarters are collected at the reception by their host and accompanied in production and laboratory areas.

Deliveries for the Oslo Headquarters are accepted at the goods entrance and registered by the facilities team.
