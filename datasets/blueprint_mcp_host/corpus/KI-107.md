# Returns Policy

Owner: Kestrel Instruments. Classification: internal.

## Purpose

The Returns Policy protects employees, customers and the company by setting clear and fair expectations. It applies equally at every Kestrel site.

## Terms used

- **Site**: any office, plant, lab or warehouse operated by Kestrel Instruments
- **Employee**: a person with an employment contract with Kestrel Instruments
- **Contractor**: a person working for Kestrel through a service agreement
- **Working day**: a weekday that is not a public holiday at the employee's site

## Applicability

Managers are responsible for making sure that their teams know the Returns Policy and apply it.

## Policy

1. Unused products can be returned within 30 days of delivery.
2. Returned products are subject to a restocking fee of 15 %.
3. Every return requires an RMA number issued by customer support.

## Roles and responsibilities

- The document owner of the Returns Policy reviews it regularly and publishes changes on the intranet.
- HR maintains the Returns Policy, answers questions about it and reports on its use to the management team.
- Employees follow the Returns Policy and ask their manager or HR when something is unclear.

## Frequently asked questions

**Q:** Who answers questions about the Returns Policy?
**A:** Your manager is the first contact; HR helps with questions the manager cannot answer.

**Q:** What happens if the Returns Policy conflicts with local law?
**A:** Local law always takes precedence, and HR documents the deviation.

## Questions and contacts

Questions about the Returns Policy go to your manager first. HR business partners support managers and employees with the interpretation.
