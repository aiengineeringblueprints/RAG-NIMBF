# Kestrel KL3 Installation and Operating Manual

Kestrel Instruments, internal document.

## Overview

The Kestrel KL3 is a level sensor sold under SKU KS-1026. It was launched in 2018.

Every Kestrel KL3 leaves the factory with an individual test report. The report documents the final functional test and the configuration loaded before shipment, which simplifies acceptance testing on site.

Typical applications of the Kestrel KL3 include process monitoring, building automation and environmental control. The device integrates into existing control systems and does not need proprietary software for basic operation.

The Kestrel KL3 is designed for continuous operation in demanding industrial environments. It combines a robust enclosure with a measurement core that Kestrel Instruments develops and calibrates in-house, so readings stay stable over long service intervals.

The Kestrel KL3 belongs to the Kestrel level sensor range and shares accessories, mounting kits and configuration tools with the other models of its family. Spare parts remain available for the whole support period of the product.

## Safety instructions

Do not operate the Kestrel KL3 outside the conditions stated in the specification table. Operation outside these limits can damage the device and void the warranty.

Modifications to the Kestrel KL3 that are not described in this document are not permitted. Use only original spare parts and accessories supplied by Kestrel Instruments.

Disconnect the supply voltage before opening the terminal compartment of the Kestrel KL3. Work on live circuits is permitted only where national regulations allow it and the necessary protective measures are in place.

Only qualified personnel may install, commission or service the Kestrel KL3. Read this document completely before working on the device and keep it available at the place of installation.

If the housing of the Kestrel KL3 is visibly damaged, take the device out of service and contact customer support before reconnecting it.

## Mechanical installation

Mount the Kestrel KL3 on a vibration-free surface away from direct sunlight and heat sources. Leave enough clearance around the housing for cable entry and later maintenance work.

The Kestrel KL3 can be mounted on a wall bracket, a pipe clamp or a standard mounting rail. Choose the mounting kit that matches the installation and tighten the screws evenly.

After mechanical installation, check that the Kestrel KL3 is firmly seated and that the housing cover closes without force.

Where the Kestrel KL3 is installed in a process line, use the supplied adapter and check the sealing surfaces for damage before tightening the process connection.

Orient the cable entries of the Kestrel KL3 downwards when installing outdoors, so that condensation cannot collect in the terminal compartment.

## Electrical connection

Connect the protective earth of the Kestrel KL3 before connecting the supply. Check the polarity of all terminals against the wiring diagram on the inside of the cover.

Route signal cables to the Kestrel KL3 separately from power cables and connect the cable shield on the controller side only, to avoid ground loops.

The terminals of the Kestrel KL3 accept solid and stranded conductors. Use ferrules for stranded wire and tighten the terminal screws with a suitable screwdriver.

Install a suitable fuse in the supply line of the Kestrel KL3 and protect the signal lines against surges where long cable runs leave the building.

Use shielded twisted-pair cable for the communication line of the Kestrel KL3 and terminate the bus at both ends according to the rules of the fieldbus in use.

## Commissioning

Compare the first readings of the Kestrel KL3 with a reference instrument and document the result in the commissioning report.

Once commissioning is complete, close the housing of the Kestrel KL3, seal unused cable entries and record the configuration in the asset management system.

Switch on the supply and wait until the status LED of the Kestrel KL3 indicates normal operation. The device runs a self-test after every start.

Set the communication address of the Kestrel KL3 before connecting it to a bus with other devices, otherwise address conflicts can interrupt communication.

## Configuration

A factory reset restores the delivery configuration of the Kestrel KL3. The calibration data is kept, but communication settings must be entered again.

Configuration profiles of the Kestrel KL3 can be exported and imported, which speeds up the commissioning of many identical measuring points.

Access to the configuration of the Kestrel KL3 can be protected with a user code. Without the code, settings are visible but cannot be changed.

The Kestrel KL3 is configured with the Kestrel Configurator, either locally through the service interface or remotely over the fieldbus.

Alarm limits, filter settings and engineering units of the Kestrel KL3 can be changed during operation. Changes take effect immediately and are logged in the device event list.

## Normal operation

Readings of the Kestrel KL3 can be logged in the control system or in a Kestrel data logger for later analysis.

The status LED of the Kestrel KL3 shows normal operation, maintenance requests and faults with different colours and blink patterns.

Diagnostic messages of the Kestrel KL3 follow the NAMUR recommendation and distinguish between failure, function check, out of specification and maintenance required.

During normal operation the Kestrel KL3 needs no user intervention. Measured values and diagnostic information are available continuously through the communication interface.

## Maintenance

Check the cable glands and seals of the Kestrel KL3 for wear during each inspection and replace them if they have become brittle.

Recalibrate the Kestrel KL3 according to the calibration procedure of Kestrel Instruments or send it to an accredited calibration lab.

Firmware updates for the Kestrel KL3 are distributed through the Kestrel service portal. Read the release notes before updating devices in the field.

Do not use solvents or high-pressure cleaners on the Kestrel KL3; they can damage the seals and the sensing element.

Inspect the Kestrel KL3 during scheduled plant shutdowns, clean the housing with a dry cloth, and record every intervention in the asset log.

## Fault finding

| Symptom | Possible cause | Remedy |
|---|---|---|
| Configuration rejected | User code active | Enter the user code and retry |
| Reading drifts slowly | Sensing element contaminated | Clean or recalibrate the device |
| Status LED shows a fault | Internal self-test failed | Restart; contact support if it persists |
| Condensation in housing | Cable entries facing upwards | Remount with entries facing down |
| Values frozen | Device in function-check mode | Leave service mode in the configurator |
| Intermittent communication | Missing bus termination | Terminate the bus at both ends |
| No reading | No supply voltage | Check fuse, wiring and supply polarity |
| No communication | Wrong bus address | Set a unique address and restart |

## Questions and answers

**Q:** Do I need special software for the Kestrel KL3?
**A:** No. The device works with standard control systems; the Kestrel Configurator is only needed for advanced settings.

**Q:** How do I find the firmware version of my Kestrel KL3?
**A:** The configurator shows it on the device information page; the current release is listed in the firmware release notes.

**Q:** Can the Kestrel KL3 be used outdoors?
**A:** Yes, provided the enclosure rating stated in the specification table matches the installation conditions and the cable entries face downwards.

**Q:** How is the Kestrel KL3 protected against unauthorised changes?
**A:** A user code can lock the configuration; without it settings can be read but not changed.

**Q:** Is the Kestrel KL3 suitable for hazardous areas?
**A:** Only variants with an explicit hazardous area certification may be used there; check the nameplate before installation.
