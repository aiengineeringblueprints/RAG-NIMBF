# Incident Report INC-2026-180

Owner: Kestrel Instruments. Classification: internal.

## Summary

This report documents incident INC-2026-180 at Kvist Bygg AB. It follows the blameless postmortem format and focuses on causes and improvements, not on individuals.

Incident INC-2026-180 interrupted measurements at Kvist Bygg AB and was handled by customer support together with engineering.

Incident INC-2026-180 occurred on 22 October 2025 at Kvist Bygg AB. The affected product was the Kestrel KH5 and the incident was classified as severity 2.

## Impact

The customer had to fall back on manual readings while incident INC-2026-180 was open.

Measurements at Kvist Bygg AB were unavailable or unreliable during the incident. No safety functions were affected.

Data gaps caused by incident INC-2026-180 were documented and shared with the customer.

The final total downtime was 74 minutes.

## How the incident was detected

Monitoring at Kvist Bygg AB raised a communication alarm, which led to the investigation of incident INC-2026-180.

A technician of Kvist Bygg AB reported unusual readings during a routine inspection, which started incident INC-2026-180.

## Cause

The root cause was radio interference from a nearby transmitter.

## Other factors

- The affected installation was not on the latest firmware
- Spare devices were not available on site
- The fault was first mistaken for a network problem

## Communication

After incident INC-2026-180 the account manager held a review meeting with Kvist Bygg AB to explain causes and actions.

Kvist Bygg AB received status updates from customer support throughout incident INC-2026-180.

A written summary of incident INC-2026-180 was sent to Kvist Bygg AB together with this report.

## Corrective actions

| Action | Owner | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Ship the fix with firmware 1.6.9 | Engineering | done |
| Roll out the fix to all affected installations | Engineering | in progress |
| Review spare part stock for key customers | Logistics | done |
| Share lessons learned in the engineering forum | Engineering | done |
| Add an alert for the observed symptom | Customer Support | open |

The fix shipped with firmware 1.6.9.

## Next steps

Similar installations at other customers were checked for the conditions that led to incident INC-2026-180.

The effectiveness of the corrective actions for incident INC-2026-180 will be checked in the next quality review.

The knowledge base was updated with the diagnosis steps used during incident INC-2026-180.

## What we learned

- Clear customer communication reduced pressure on the team
- Checklists need regular review after incidents
