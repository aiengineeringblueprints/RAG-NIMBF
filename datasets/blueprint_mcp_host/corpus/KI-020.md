## Mechanical installation

The Kestrel KG9 can be mounted on a wall bracket, a pipe clamp or a standard mounting rail. Choose the mounting kit that matches the installation and tighten the screws evenly.

Orient the cable entries of the Kestrel KG9 downwards when installing outdoors, so that condensation cannot collect in the terminal compartment.

## Electrical connection

Connect the protective earth of the Kestrel KG9 before connecting the supply. Check the polarity of all terminals against the wiring diagram on the inside of the cover.

Route signal cables to the Kestrel KG9 separately from power cables and connect the cable shield on the controller side only, to avoid ground loops.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Possible cause | Remedy |
|---|---|---|
| Condensation in housing | Cable entries facing upwards | Remount with entries facing down |
| Status LED shows a fault | Internal self-test failed | Restart; contact support if it persists |
| Intermittent communication | Missing bus termination | Terminate the bus at both ends |
| Values frozen | Device in function-check mode | Leave service mode in the configurator |
