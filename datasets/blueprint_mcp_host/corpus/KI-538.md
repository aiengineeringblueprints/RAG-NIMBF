# Incident Report INC-2026-334

Kestrel Instruments. Internal use only.

## Summary

Incident INC-2026-334 interrupted measurements at Bergvik Marine Oy and was handled by customer support together with engineering.

This report documents incident INC-2026-334 at Bergvik Marine Oy. It follows the blameless postmortem format and focuses on causes and improvements, not on individuals.

Incident INC-2026-334 occurred on 24 June 2026 at Bergvik Marine Oy. The affected product was the Kestrel KV9 and the incident was classified as severity 2.

## Customer impact

Measurements at Bergvik Marine Oy were unavailable or unreliable during the incident. No safety functions were affected.

Data gaps caused by incident INC-2026-334 were documented and shared with the customer.

The customer had to fall back on manual readings while incident INC-2026-334 was open.

The final total downtime was 435 minutes.

## How the incident was detected

Incident INC-2026-334 was detected when Bergvik Marine Oy noticed missing values in the control system and opened a support case.

Monitoring at Bergvik Marine Oy raised a communication alarm, which led to the investigation of incident INC-2026-334.

## Root cause analysis

Root cause analysis identified a misconfigured Modbus address.

## Other factors

- The affected installation was not on the latest firmware
- Monitoring did not alert on the first symptoms
- Spare devices were not available on site
- Documentation of the installation was incomplete

## Customer communication

A written summary of incident INC-2026-334 was sent to Bergvik Marine Oy together with this report.

Bergvik Marine Oy received status updates from customer support throughout incident INC-2026-334.

After incident INC-2026-334 the account manager held a review meeting with Bergvik Marine Oy to explain causes and actions.

## Actions

| Action | Owner | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Ship the fix with firmware 3.7.9 | Engineering | done |
| Share lessons learned in the engineering forum | Engineering | open |
| Review spare part stock for key customers | Logistics | open |
| Add an alert for the observed symptom | Customer Support | in progress |

The fix shipped with firmware 3.7.9.

## Lessons learned

- Early escalation shortened the investigation
- Clear customer communication reduced pressure on the team
- Remote diagnostics access was essential

## Follow-up

The effectiveness of the corrective actions for incident INC-2026-334 will be checked in the next quality review.

Similar installations at other customers were checked for the conditions that led to incident INC-2026-334.
