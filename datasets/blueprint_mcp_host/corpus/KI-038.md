# Kestrel KQ2 Datasheet

Kestrel Instruments, internal document.

## Introduction

Product overview: the Kestrel KQ2 (power quality meter) is ordered with SKU KS-1038 and has been on the market since it was launched in 2021.

The Kestrel KQ2 belongs to the Kestrel power quality meter range and shares accessories, mounting kits and configuration tools with the other models of its family. Spare parts remain available for the whole support period of the product.

Typical applications of the Kestrel KQ2 include process monitoring, building automation and environmental control. The device integrates into existing control systems and does not need proprietary software for basic operation.

## Mounting

Mount the Kestrel KQ2 on a vibration-free surface away from direct sunlight and heat sources. Leave enough clearance around the housing for cable entry and later maintenance work.

Where the Kestrel KQ2 is installed in a process line, use the supplied adapter and check the sealing surfaces for damage before tightening the process connection.

## Electrical connection

Install a suitable fuse in the supply line of the Kestrel KQ2 and protect the signal lines against surges where long cable runs leave the building.

## Maintenance

Check the cable glands and seals of the Kestrel KQ2 for wear during each inspection and replace them if they have become brittle.

Recalibrate the Kestrel KQ2 according to the calibration procedure of Kestrel Instruments or send it to an accredited calibration lab.
