# Leave Policy

Kestrel Instruments, internal document.

## Purpose of this policy

The Leave Policy turns legal requirements and company values into practical rules. Where local law sets stricter requirements, local law takes precedence.

The Leave Policy protects employees, customers and the company by setting clear and fair expectations. It applies equally at every Kestrel site.

This document describes the rules of the Leave Policy at Kestrel Instruments and explains why they exist. It gives employees and managers a common reference for daily decisions.

## Background

The Leave Policy is reviewed together with employee representatives, so that changes are understood and accepted.

Feedback from employees and managers shaped the current Leave Policy; it aims to be simple to apply and fair for everybody.

The Leave Policy was introduced to make expectations consistent across all Kestrel sites after the company grew beyond its original headquarters.

## Who this applies to

Managers are responsible for making sure that their teams know the Leave Policy and apply it.

Temporary staff and interns follow the Leave Policy in the same way as permanent employees.

The Leave Policy applies to all employees and contractors of Kestrel Instruments unless a local works agreement states otherwise.

## Definitions

- **Contractor**: a person working for Kestrel through a service agreement
- **Manager**: an employee with formal responsibility for a team or cost center
- **Intranet**: the internal information portal of Kestrel Instruments
- **Site**: any office, plant, lab or warehouse operated by Kestrel Instruments
- **Working day**: a weekday that is not a public holiday at the employee's site

## Policy statements

Employees must give at least 14 days of notice before taking planned leave. Unplanned leave due to illness is reported to the team lead on the first day of absence. At most 5 unused leave days can be carried over into the next calendar year.

## Frequently asked questions

**Q:** Where can I find the current version of the Leave Policy?
**A:** The current version is always published on the intranet; printed copies may be outdated.

**Q:** Are earlier versions of the Leave Policy still valid?
**A:** No. Earlier versions are archived for audit purposes only and must not be applied.

**Q:** What happens if the Leave Policy conflicts with local law?
**A:** Local law always takes precedence, and HR documents the deviation.

## Procedure

1. Keep the confirmation for your records
2. Read the relevant section of the Leave Policy before starting the process
3. Wait for the confirmation before acting on the request
4. Submit the request through the HR portal

## Questions and contacts

The intranet page of the Leave Policy lists the responsible HR contact for each site.

Questions about the Leave Policy go to your manager first. HR business partners support managers and employees with the interpretation.

## Responsibilities

- Managers apply the Leave Policy consistently and document exceptions they approve.
- HR maintains the Leave Policy, answers questions about it and reports on its use to the management team.
- Employees follow the Leave Policy and ask their manager or HR when something is unclear.

## Exceptions

Repeated or deliberate violations of the Leave Policy can lead to disciplinary measures.

Approved exceptions to the Leave Policy are documented and reviewed when the policy is updated.

Exceptions to the Leave Policy require written approval from the responsible manager and HR.
