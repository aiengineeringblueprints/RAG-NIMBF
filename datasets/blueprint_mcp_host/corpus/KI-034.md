# Kestrel KW7 Installation and Operating Manual

Kestrel Instruments, internal document.

## Overview

The Kestrel KW7 is a wireless gateway sold under SKU KS-1034. It was launched in 2023.

Every Kestrel KW7 leaves the factory with an individual test report. The report documents the final functional test and the configuration loaded before shipment, which simplifies acceptance testing on site.

The Kestrel KW7 is designed for continuous operation in demanding industrial environments. It combines a robust enclosure with a measurement core that Kestrel Instruments develops and calibrates in-house, so readings stay stable over long service intervals.

The Kestrel KW7 belongs to the Kestrel wireless gateway range and shares accessories, mounting kits and configuration tools with the other models of its family. Spare parts remain available for the whole support period of the product.

Typical applications of the Kestrel KW7 include process monitoring, building automation and environmental control. The device integrates into existing control systems and does not need proprietary software for basic operation.

## For your safety

Disconnect the supply voltage before opening the terminal compartment of the Kestrel KW7. Work on live circuits is permitted only where national regulations allow it and the necessary protective measures are in place.

If the housing of the Kestrel KW7 is visibly damaged, take the device out of service and contact customer support before reconnecting it.

Do not operate the Kestrel KW7 outside the conditions stated in the specification table. Operation outside these limits can damage the device and void the warranty.

Modifications to the Kestrel KW7 that are not described in this document are not permitted. Use only original spare parts and accessories supplied by Kestrel Instruments.

Only qualified personnel may install, commission or service the Kestrel KW7. Read this document completely before working on the device and keep it available at the place of installation.

## Specifications

The Kestrel KW7 operates between -40 °C and 80 °C and communicates via HART.

## Mounting

Orient the cable entries of the Kestrel KW7 downwards when installing outdoors, so that condensation cannot collect in the terminal compartment.

The Kestrel KW7 can be mounted on a wall bracket, a pipe clamp or a standard mounting rail. Choose the mounting kit that matches the installation and tighten the screws evenly.

After mechanical installation, check that the Kestrel KW7 is firmly seated and that the housing cover closes without force.

Where the Kestrel KW7 is installed in a process line, use the supplied adapter and check the sealing surfaces for damage before tightening the process connection.

Mount the Kestrel KW7 on a vibration-free surface away from direct sunlight and heat sources. Leave enough clearance around the housing for cable entry and later maintenance work.

## Wiring

Install a suitable fuse in the supply line of the Kestrel KW7 and protect the signal lines against surges where long cable runs leave the building.

The terminals of the Kestrel KW7 accept solid and stranded conductors. Use ferrules for stranded wire and tighten the terminal screws with a suitable screwdriver.

Use shielded twisted-pair cable for the communication line of the Kestrel KW7 and terminate the bus at both ends according to the rules of the fieldbus in use.

Route signal cables to the Kestrel KW7 separately from power cables and connect the cable shield on the controller side only, to avoid ground loops.

Connect the protective earth of the Kestrel KW7 before connecting the supply. Check the polarity of all terminals against the wiring diagram on the inside of the cover.

## Commissioning

Once commissioning is complete, close the housing of the Kestrel KW7, seal unused cable entries and record the configuration in the asset management system.

Switch on the supply and wait until the status LED of the Kestrel KW7 indicates normal operation. The device runs a self-test after every start.

Set the communication address of the Kestrel KW7 before connecting it to a bus with other devices, otherwise address conflicts can interrupt communication.

Compare the first readings of the Kestrel KW7 with a reference instrument and document the result in the commissioning report.

## Device configuration

Access to the configuration of the Kestrel KW7 can be protected with a user code. Without the code, settings are visible but cannot be changed.

The Kestrel KW7 is configured with the Kestrel Configurator, either locally through the service interface or remotely over the fieldbus.

Alarm limits, filter settings and engineering units of the Kestrel KW7 can be changed during operation. Changes take effect immediately and are logged in the device event list.

Configuration profiles of the Kestrel KW7 can be exported and imported, which speeds up the commissioning of many identical measuring points.

A factory reset restores the delivery configuration of the Kestrel KW7. The calibration data is kept, but communication settings must be entered again.

## Operation

Readings of the Kestrel KW7 can be logged in the control system or in a Kestrel data logger for later analysis.

Diagnostic messages of the Kestrel KW7 follow the NAMUR recommendation and distinguish between failure, function check, out of specification and maintenance required.

The status LED of the Kestrel KW7 shows normal operation, maintenance requests and faults with different colours and blink patterns.

During normal operation the Kestrel KW7 needs no user intervention. Measured values and diagnostic information are available continuously through the communication interface.

## Maintenance

Check the cable glands and seals of the Kestrel KW7 for wear during each inspection and replace them if they have become brittle.

Inspect the Kestrel KW7 during scheduled plant shutdowns, clean the housing with a dry cloth, and record every intervention in the asset log.

Firmware updates for the Kestrel KW7 are distributed through the Kestrel service portal. Read the release notes before updating devices in the field.

Do not use solvents or high-pressure cleaners on the Kestrel KW7; they can damage the seals and the sensing element.

Recalibrate the Kestrel KW7 according to the calibration procedure of Kestrel Instruments or send it to an accredited calibration lab.

## Diagnostics and troubleshooting

| Symptom | Possible cause | Remedy |
|---|---|---|
| Values frozen | Device in function-check mode | Leave service mode in the configurator |
| Reading drifts slowly | Sensing element contaminated | Clean or recalibrate the device |
| No communication | Wrong bus address | Set a unique address and restart |
| Condensation in housing | Cable entries facing upwards | Remount with entries facing down |
| Intermittent communication | Missing bus termination | Terminate the bus at both ends |
| Alarm without cause | Alarm limits too tight | Review limits and hysteresis |

## Questions and answers

**Q:** Can I replace the sensing element of the Kestrel KW7 myself?
**A:** Only trained service staff should replace it, because the device must be recalibrated afterwards.

**Q:** Where do I order spare parts for the Kestrel KW7?
**A:** Spare parts are ordered through the Kestrel sales office responsible for your country.

**Q:** Can the Kestrel KW7 be mounted next to a frequency converter?
**A:** Keep as much distance as possible and use shielded cables to limit electromagnetic interference.

**Q:** Can the Kestrel KW7 be used outdoors?
**A:** Yes, provided the enclosure rating stated in the specification table matches the installation conditions and the cable entries face downwards.
