Kestrel Instruments, internal document.

## Introduction

The Kestrel KQ5 is a power quality meter sold under SKU KS-1040. It was launched in 2017.

The Kestrel KQ5 belongs to the Kestrel power quality meter range and shares accessories, mounting kits and configuration tools with the other models of its family. Spare parts remain available for the whole support period of the product.

## Mounting

Orient the cable entries of the Kestrel KQ5 downwards when installing outdoors, so that condensation cannot collect in the terminal compartment.

## Wiring

Route signal cables to the Kestrel KQ5 separately from power cables and connect the cable shield on the controller side only, to avoid ground loops.

## Maintenance

Inspect the Kestrel KQ5 during scheduled plant shutdowns, clean the housing with a dry cloth, and record every intervention in the asset log.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Possible cause | Remedy |
|---|---|---|
| Reading drifts slowly | Sensing element contaminated | Clean or recalibrate the device |
| Configuration rejected | User code active | Enter the user code and retry |
| Intermittent communication | Missing bus termination | Terminate the bus at both ends |
| No communication | Wrong bus address | Set a unique address and restart |
| Unstable reading | Electrical interference | Check shielding and cable routing |
