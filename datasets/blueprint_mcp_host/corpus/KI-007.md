# Kestrel KT8 Datasheet

Kestrel Instruments. Internal use only.

## Product overview

Product overview: the Kestrel KT8 (temperature probe) is ordered with SKU KS-1007 and has been on the market since it was launched in 2017.

Every Kestrel KT8 leaves the factory with an individual test report. The report documents the final functional test and the configuration loaded before shipment, which simplifies acceptance testing on site.

The Kestrel KT8 belongs to the Kestrel temperature probe range and shares accessories, mounting kits and configuration tools with the other models of its family. Spare parts remain available for the whole support period of the product.

## Technical specifications

Minimum operating temperature: -40 °C. Maximum operating temperature: 105 °C. Supply current: 31 mA. Communication interface: Modbus RTU. Accuracy: ±0.2 K. Enclosure rating: IP67. Weight (current revision): 570 g.

## Storage and transport

Store the Kestrel KT8 in its original packaging in a dry place, protected from dust, direct sunlight and condensation.

Transport the Kestrel KT8 only in packaging that protects it against shocks; damage caused by unsuitable packaging is not covered by the warranty.

After long storage, check the seals of the Kestrel KT8 before installation and replace them if they have hardened.

## Mechanical installation

The Kestrel KT8 can be mounted on a wall bracket, a pipe clamp or a standard mounting rail. Choose the mounting kit that matches the installation and tighten the screws evenly.

Orient the cable entries of the Kestrel KT8 downwards when installing outdoors, so that condensation cannot collect in the terminal compartment.

## Electrical connection

Use shielded twisted-pair cable for the communication line of the Kestrel KT8 and terminate the bus at both ends according to the rules of the fieldbus in use.

Install a suitable fuse in the supply line of the Kestrel KT8 and protect the signal lines against surges where long cable runs leave the building.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Possible cause | Remedy |
|---|---|---|
| Values frozen | Device in function-check mode | Leave service mode in the configurator |
| No reading | No supply voltage | Check fuse, wiring and supply polarity |
| Alarm without cause | Alarm limits too tight | Review limits and hysteresis |
| Reading drifts slowly | Sensing element contaminated | Clean or recalibrate the device |
