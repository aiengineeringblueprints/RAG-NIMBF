# Training Course: Project Management Basics

Kestrel Instruments, internal document.

## About the course

Project Management Basics: 1 days, trainer Live Myhre.

Project Management Basics combines short theory blocks with hands-on exercises on real devices.

The course Project Management Basics gives participants the practical knowledge they need in daily work with Kestrel products.

Participants of Project Management Basics work in small groups, so trainers can respond to individual questions.

It accepts at most 18 participants and costs 1750 EUR per person.

The course has no prerequisites.

## Who should attend

Participants of Project Management Basics should be familiar with basic measurement technology.

Customers, partners and Kestrel employees can register for Project Management Basics.

Project Management Basics is aimed at technicians and engineers who install, configure or maintain Kestrel products.

## Course materials

Demo devices and tools for Project Management Basics are provided by Kestrel; participants do not need to bring equipment apart from a laptop.

After Project Management Basics participants keep access to the recorded theory blocks for later reference.

Participants of Project Management Basics receive the course slides and exercise descriptions in digital form.

## What you will learn

- Plan maintenance tasks
- Document work according to company standards
- Configure devices with the Kestrel Configurator
- Apply the relevant safety rules
- Use the service portal efficiently

## Assessment

Project Management Basics ends with a short practical assessment on a demo device.

Participants who do not pass the assessment of Project Management Basics can repeat it at a later date free of charge.

## Agenda

### Day 1

- Final assessment
- Review and questions
- Troubleshooting lab
