# Kestrel KG7 Datasheet

Owner: Kestrel Instruments. Classification: internal.

## Introduction

The Kestrel KG7 is a gas sensor sold under SKU KS-1018. It was launched in 2017.

Every Kestrel KG7 leaves the factory with an individual test report. The report documents the final functional test and the configuration loaded before shipment, which simplifies acceptance testing on site.

## Specifications

- Minimum operating temperature: -10 °C
- Maximum operating temperature: 60 °C
- Supply current: 43 mA
- Communication interface: PROFINET
- Accuracy: ±30 ppm
- Enclosure rating: IP65
- Weight (current revision): 525 g

Its operating temperature range is -10 °C to 60 °C; the supply current is 43 mA.

## Storage and transport

Transport the Kestrel KG7 only in packaging that protects it against shocks; damage caused by unsuitable packaging is not covered by the warranty.

## Mounting

Where the Kestrel KG7 is installed in a process line, use the supplied adapter and check the sealing surfaces for damage before tightening the process connection.

## Electrical connection

Install a suitable fuse in the supply line of the Kestrel KG7 and protect the signal lines against surges where long cable runs leave the building.

## Care and maintenance

Check the cable glands and seals of the Kestrel KG7 for wear during each inspection and replace them if they have become brittle.
