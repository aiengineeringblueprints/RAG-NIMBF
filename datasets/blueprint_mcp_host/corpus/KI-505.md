# Incident Report INC-2026-047

Owner: Kestrel Instruments. Classification: internal.

## Overview

Incident INC-2026-047 interrupted measurements at Vikholm Marine Inc. and was handled by customer support together with engineering.

This report documents incident INC-2026-047 at Vikholm Marine Inc. It follows the blameless postmortem format and focuses on causes and improvements, not on individuals.

Incident INC-2026-047 occurred on 12 August 2026 at Vikholm Marine Inc. The affected product was the Kestrel KQ4 and the incident was classified as severity 3.

## Customer impact

The customer had to fall back on manual readings while incident INC-2026-047 was open.

## Root cause analysis

Root cause analysis identified a clock synchronisation error.

## Contributing factors

- The fault was first mistaken for a network problem
- Spare devices were not available on site
- Monitoring did not alert on the first symptoms
