# Incident Report INC-2026-328

Owner: Kestrel Instruments. Classification: internal.

## Incident summary

Incident INC-2026-328 interrupted measurements at Elvebakk Data AS and was handled by customer support together with engineering.

This report documents incident INC-2026-328 at Elvebakk Data AS. It follows the blameless postmortem format and focuses on causes and improvements, not on individuals.

Incident INC-2026-328 occurred on 13 October 2025 at Elvebakk Data AS. The affected product was the Kestrel KQ5 and the incident was classified as severity 2.

## Detection

Monitoring at Elvebakk Data AS raised a communication alarm, which led to the investigation of incident INC-2026-328.

Incident INC-2026-328 was detected when Elvebakk Data AS noticed missing values in the control system and opened a support case.

## Chronology

|---|---|
| 11:00 | Customer reported missing readings |
| 11:48 | Remote diagnosis started |

| 13:37 | Workaround applied on site |

## Impact

The customer had to fall back on manual readings while incident INC-2026-328 was open.

Data gaps caused by incident INC-2026-328 were documented and shared with the customer.

Measurements at Elvebakk Data AS were unavailable or unreliable during the incident. No safety functions were affected.

The final total downtime was 317 minutes.

## Cause

The root cause was radio interference from a nearby transmitter.

## Contributing factors

- Monitoring did not alert on the first symptoms
- Documentation of the installation was incomplete
- Spare devices were not available on site

## Customer communication

A written summary of incident INC-2026-328 was sent to Elvebakk Data AS together with this report.

After incident INC-2026-328 the account manager held a review meeting with Elvebakk Data AS to explain causes and actions.

Elvebakk Data AS received status updates from customer support throughout incident INC-2026-328.

## Measures

| Action | Owner | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Ship the fix with firmware 2.4.8 | Engineering | done |
| Update the installation checklist | Quality | open |
| Roll out the fix to all affected installations | Engineering | done |

The fix shipped with firmware 2.4.8.

## Follow-up

Similar installations at other customers were checked for the conditions that led to incident INC-2026-328.

The effectiveness of the corrective actions for incident INC-2026-328 will be checked in the next quality review.

The knowledge base was updated with the diagnosis steps used during incident INC-2026-328.

## Lessons learned

- Early escalation shortened the investigation
- Checklists need regular review after incidents
- Remote diagnostics access was essential
