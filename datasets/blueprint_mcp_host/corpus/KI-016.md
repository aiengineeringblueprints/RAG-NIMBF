# Kestrel KF9 Datasheet

Owner: Kestrel Instruments. Classification: internal.

## Overview

The Kestrel KF9 is a flow meter sold under SKU KS-1016. It was launched in 2023.

Every Kestrel KF9 leaves the factory with an individual test report. The report documents the final functional test and the configuration loaded before shipment, which simplifies acceptance testing on site.

Typical applications of the Kestrel KF9 include process monitoring, building automation and environmental control. The device integrates into existing control systems and does not need proprietary software for basic operation.

## Applications

In water treatment plants the Kestrel KF9 monitors process conditions continuously and feeds its readings into the plant control system, where operators set alarms for deviations.

Building operators use the Kestrel KF9 to keep indoor conditions within comfort and energy targets, often as part of a larger building management system.

## Storage and transport

Transport the Kestrel KF9 only in packaging that protects it against shocks; damage caused by unsuitable packaging is not covered by the warranty.

Store the Kestrel KF9 in its original packaging in a dry place, protected from dust, direct sunlight and condensation.

After long storage, check the seals of the Kestrel KF9 before installation and replace them if they have hardened.

## Approvals

The Kestrel KF9 carries the CE marking and complies with the applicable European directives for electromagnetic compatibility and electrical safety.

Materials of the Kestrel KF9 comply with the RoHS directive, and a declaration of conformity is available on request.

## Mounting

After mechanical installation, check that the Kestrel KF9 is firmly seated and that the housing cover closes without force.

Where the Kestrel KF9 is installed in a process line, use the supplied adapter and check the sealing surfaces for damage before tightening the process connection.

## Wiring

Connect the protective earth of the Kestrel KF9 before connecting the supply. Check the polarity of all terminals against the wiring diagram on the inside of the cover.

The terminals of the Kestrel KF9 accept solid and stranded conductors. Use ferrules for stranded wire and tighten the terminal screws with a suitable screwdriver.

## Care and maintenance

Inspect the Kestrel KF9 during scheduled plant shutdowns, clean the housing with a dry cloth, and record every intervention in the asset log.

Check the cable glands and seals of the Kestrel KF9 for wear during each inspection and replace them if they have become brittle.
