# Environmental Policy

Kestrel Instruments. Internal use only.

## Purpose of this policy

This document describes the rules of the Environmental Policy at Kestrel Instruments and explains why they exist. It gives employees and managers a common reference for daily decisions.

## Policy

Kestrel aims to cut its scope 1 and 2 emissions by 50 % by 2030. All sites have used renewable electricity since 2023. Returned devices are refurbished or recycled through a certified partner.

## Procedure

1. Submit the request through the HR portal
2. Inform your team about any absence or change that affects them
3. Keep the confirmation for your records

## Frequently asked questions

**Q:** Are earlier versions of the Environmental Policy still valid?
**A:** No. Earlier versions are archived for audit purposes only and must not be applied.

**Q:** Where can I find the current version of the Environmental Policy?
**A:** The current version is always published on the intranet; printed copies may be outdated.

**Q:** Who answers questions about the Environmental Policy?
**A:** Your manager is the first contact; HR helps with questions the manager cannot answer.

**Q:** What happens if the Environmental Policy conflicts with local law?
**A:** Local law always takes precedence, and HR documents the deviation.
