# Incident Report INC-2026-231

Kestrel Instruments. Internal use only.

## Overview

This report documents incident INC-2026-231 at Fjellheim Data Oy. It follows the blameless postmortem format and focuses on causes and improvements, not on individuals.

Incident INC-2026-231 interrupted measurements at Fjellheim Data Oy and was handled by customer support together with engineering.

Incident INC-2026-231 occurred on 8 May 2026 at Fjellheim Data Oy. The affected product was the Kestrel KW3 and the incident was classified as severity 2.

## Detection

Incident INC-2026-231 was detected when Fjellheim Data Oy noticed missing values in the control system and opened a support case.

Monitoring at Fjellheim Data Oy raised a communication alarm, which led to the investigation of incident INC-2026-231.

## Impact

Data gaps caused by incident INC-2026-231 were documented and shared with the customer.

The customer had to fall back on manual readings while incident INC-2026-231 was open.

Measurements at Fjellheim Data Oy were unavailable or unreliable during the incident. No safety functions were affected.

The final total downtime was 452 minutes.

## Root cause

Root cause analysis identified radio interference from a nearby transmitter.

## Other factors

- The affected installation was not on the latest firmware
- Spare devices were not available on site
- The fault was first mistaken for a network problem
- Monitoring did not alert on the first symptoms

## Customer communication

Fjellheim Data Oy received status updates from customer support throughout incident INC-2026-231.

After incident INC-2026-231 the account manager held a review meeting with Fjellheim Data Oy to explain causes and actions.

A written summary of incident INC-2026-231 was sent to Fjellheim Data Oy together with this report.

## Actions

| Action | Owner | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Ship the fix with firmware 1.7.8 | Engineering | done |
| Update the installation checklist | Quality | open |

The fix shipped with firmware 1.7.8.

## Lessons learned

- Checklists need regular review after incidents
- Early escalation shortened the investigation
- Clear customer communication reduced pressure on the team
- Remote diagnostics access was essential

## Follow-up

The knowledge base was updated with the diagnosis steps used during incident INC-2026-231.

Similar installations at other customers were checked for the conditions that led to incident INC-2026-231.

The effectiveness of the corrective actions for incident INC-2026-231 will be checked in the next quality review.
