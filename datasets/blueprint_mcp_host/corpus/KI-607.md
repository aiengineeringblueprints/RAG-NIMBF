# Supplier Profile: Vega Components

Kestrel Instruments, internal document.

## Introduction

Kestrel buys cable glands from Vega Components. Vega Components is based in Taiwan.

Kestrel sources cable glands from Vega Components because of consistent quality and good technical support.

Vega Components is a long-standing supplier of Kestrel Instruments and delivers cable glands for several product families.

Vega Components produces at its own facilities in Taiwan and holds the usual quality certifications for its industry.

## Supplier data

- Component: cable glands
- Country: Taiwan
- Current standard lead time: 3 weeks
- Framework contract until: 2028

## Delivery performance

During the component shortage the lead time temporarily rose to 7 weeks. The current standard lead time is 3 weeks. The current framework contract runs until 2028.

## Contract terms

Payment terms with Vega Components follow the Kestrel standard terms unless the contract states otherwise.

The framework contract with Vega Components defines prices, delivery terms and quality requirements for cable glands.

Price changes from Vega Components must be announced in advance and are reviewed by the purchasing board.

## Audit history

Vega Components has implemented all corrective actions from previous audits on time.

Kestrel quality engineers audit Vega Components regularly; the last audit found no major deviations.

Audit reports for Vega Components are stored in the quality system and shared with purchasing.

## Quality

Supplier performance of Vega Components is reviewed every quarter by purchasing and quality.

Vega Components shares test reports with every delivery of cable glands, which simplifies incoming inspection.

Incoming inspection at Kestrel checks samples of every delivery from Vega Components. Deviations are handled through supplier corrective action requests.

## Responsible sourcing

Packaging from Vega Components is reusable or recyclable wherever possible.

Vega Components has signed the Kestrel supplier code of conduct.

Vega Components reports on energy use and waste reduction as part of the annual supplier review.

## Logistics

Vega Components offers consignment stock for cable glands, which reduces the risk of shortages.

Deliveries from Vega Components go to the Aarhus plant and the Stavanger warehouse. Delivery schedules and open purchase orders are tracked in the ERP system.

Forecasts for cable glands are shared with Vega Components monthly to support their capacity planning.
