# Kestrel KH3 Datasheet

Owner: Kestrel Instruments. Classification: internal.

## Product overview

The Kestrel KH3 is a humidity sensor sold under SKU KS-1001. It was launched in 2021.

Every Kestrel KH3 leaves the factory with an individual test report. The report documents the final functional test and the configuration loaded before shipment, which simplifies acceptance testing on site.

The Kestrel KH3 belongs to the Kestrel humidity sensor range and shares accessories, mounting kits and configuration tools with the other models of its family. Spare parts remain available for the whole support period of the product.

## Transport and storage

After long storage, check the seals of the Kestrel KH3 before installation and replace them if they have hardened.

Store the Kestrel KH3 in its original packaging in a dry place, protected from dust, direct sunlight and condensation.

Transport the Kestrel KH3 only in packaging that protects it against shocks; damage caused by unsuitable packaging is not covered by the warranty.

## Applications

Utilities deploy the Kestrel KH3 at remote stations, where reliable unattended operation matters more than frequent manual maintenance.

## Mounting

Orient the cable entries of the Kestrel KH3 downwards when installing outdoors, so that condensation cannot collect in the terminal compartment.

Mount the Kestrel KH3 on a vibration-free surface away from direct sunlight and heat sources. Leave enough clearance around the housing for cable entry and later maintenance work.

## Electrical connection

Use shielded twisted-pair cable for the communication line of the Kestrel KH3 and terminate the bus at both ends according to the rules of the fieldbus in use.

Install a suitable fuse in the supply line of the Kestrel KH3 and protect the signal lines against surges where long cable runs leave the building.

## Maintenance

Inspect the Kestrel KH3 during scheduled plant shutdowns, clean the housing with a dry cloth, and record every intervention in the asset log.

## Questions and answers

**Q:** Can the Kestrel KH3 be used outdoors?
**A:** Yes, provided the enclosure rating stated in the specification table matches the installation conditions and the cable entries face downwards.

**Q:** Is the Kestrel KH3 suitable for hazardous areas?
**A:** Only variants with an explicit hazardous area certification may be used there; check the nameplate before installation.

**Q:** Can the Kestrel KH3 be mounted next to a frequency converter?
**A:** Keep as much distance as possible and use shielded cables to limit electromagnetic interference.
