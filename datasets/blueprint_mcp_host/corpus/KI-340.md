## Background

Ahlberg Marine Oy uses Kestrel equipment mainly for monitoring and quality assurance in its shipping operations.

During the first years Ahlberg Marine Oy mainly bought standard products. Later projects added integration services and customer training.

Ahlberg Marine Oy took part in a beta programme for new firmware and gave valuable feedback to product management.

Ahlberg Marine Oy values short response times and local support, which is why the account is served by a dedicated account manager.

## Support contract

In 2024 Ahlberg Marine Oy upgraded its support contract from the Bronze tier to the Silver tier. Before the upgrade, the SLA response time was 48 hours; it is now 24 hours.
