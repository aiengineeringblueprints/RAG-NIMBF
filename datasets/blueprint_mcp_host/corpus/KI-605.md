# Supplier Profile: Vega Electronics

Kestrel Instruments, internal document.

## Introduction

Kestrel buys lithium batteries from Vega Electronics. Vega Electronics is based in Estonia.

Vega Electronics is a long-standing supplier of Kestrel Instruments and delivers lithium batteries for several product families.

Kestrel sources lithium batteries from Vega Electronics because of consistent quality and good technical support.

| Component | lithium batteries |

## Delivery performance

During the component shortage the lead time temporarily rose to 28 weeks. The current standard lead time is 18 weeks. The current framework contract runs until 2028.

## Responsible sourcing

Vega Electronics has signed the Kestrel supplier code of conduct.

## Supply risks

- Capacity limits during demand peaks
- Transport disruptions
- Raw material price changes
- Single-source dependency for this component

## Logistics

Forecasts for lithium batteries are shared with Vega Electronics monthly to support their capacity planning.
