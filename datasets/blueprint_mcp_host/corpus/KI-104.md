# Remote Work Policy

Kestrel Instruments. Internal use only.

## Purpose

The Remote Work Policy turns legal requirements and company values into practical rules. Where local law sets stricter requirements, local law takes precedence.

The Remote Work Policy protects employees, customers and the company by setting clear and fair expectations. It applies equally at every Kestrel site.

This document describes the rules of the Remote Work Policy at Kestrel Instruments and explains why they exist. It gives employees and managers a common reference for daily decisions.

## Background

The Remote Work Policy was introduced to make expectations consistent across all Kestrel sites after the company grew beyond its original headquarters.

Feedback from employees and managers shaped the current Remote Work Policy; it aims to be simple to apply and fair for everybody.

The Remote Work Policy is reviewed together with employee representatives, so that changes are understood and accepted.

## Applicability

The Remote Work Policy applies to all employees and contractors of Kestrel Instruments unless a local works agreement states otherwise.

Temporary staff and interns follow the Remote Work Policy in the same way as permanent employees.

Managers are responsible for making sure that their teams know the Remote Work Policy and apply it.

## Terms used

- **Intranet**: the internal information portal of Kestrel Instruments
- **Contractor**: a person working for Kestrel through a service agreement
- **Site**: any office, plant, lab or warehouse operated by Kestrel Instruments
- **Manager**: an employee with formal responsibility for a team or cost center

## Rules

1. Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week.
2. Core hours, during which everyone is reachable, are 10:00 to 15:00.
3. Each remote employee receives a one-time equipment allowance of 300 EUR.

## Frequently asked questions

**Q:** Where can I find the current version of the Remote Work Policy?
**A:** The current version is always published on the intranet; printed copies may be outdated.

**Q:** What happens if the Remote Work Policy conflicts with local law?
**A:** Local law always takes precedence, and HR documents the deviation.

**Q:** Are earlier versions of the Remote Work Policy still valid?
**A:** No. Earlier versions are archived for audit purposes only and must not be applied.

## Process

1. Read the relevant section of the Remote Work Policy before starting the process
2. Attach the required documents
3. Inform your team about any absence or change that affects them
4. Discuss open questions with your manager

## Deviations

Repeated or deliberate violations of the Remote Work Policy can lead to disciplinary measures.

Approved exceptions to the Remote Work Policy are documented and reviewed when the policy is updated.

Exceptions to the Remote Work Policy require written approval from the responsible manager and HR.

## Responsibilities

Managers apply the Remote Work Policy consistently and document exceptions they approve.

HR maintains the Remote Work Policy, answers questions about it and reports on its use to the management team.

The document owner of the Remote Work Policy reviews it regularly and publishes changes on the intranet.

Employees follow the Remote Work Policy and ask their manager or HR when something is unclear.

## Contacts

Questions about the Remote Work Policy go to your manager first. HR business partners support managers and employees with the interpretation.

The intranet page of the Remote Work Policy lists the responsible HR contact for each site.
