# Training Course: Advanced Calibration

Kestrel Instruments, internal document.

## Description

The course Advanced Calibration lasts 3 days and is taught by Eirik Rognes.

Advanced Calibration combines short theory blocks with hands-on exercises on real devices.

Participants of Advanced Calibration work in small groups, so trainers can respond to individual questions.

It accepts at most 20 participants and costs 750 EUR per person.

Participants must first complete Sensor Calibration Basics.

## Assessment

Participants who do not pass the assessment of Advanced Calibration can repeat it at a later date free of charge.

## Materials

Participants of Advanced Calibration receive the course slides and exercise descriptions in digital form.

## Target audience

Advanced Calibration is aimed at technicians and engineers who install, configure or maintain Kestrel products.

## Course agenda

### Day 1

- Group work on case studies
- Review and questions
- Theory block
- Introduction and safety briefing

### Day 2

- Troubleshooting lab
- Group work on case studies
- Hands-on exercise on demo devices
- Introduction and safety briefing

### Day 3

- Hands-on exercise on demo devices
- Introduction and safety briefing
- Review and questions
- Troubleshooting lab
- Theory block

## FAQ

**Q:** Is Advanced Calibration also offered online?
**A:** Selected dates are offered online; hands-on exercises then use remote demo devices.

**Q:** Will I receive a certificate for Advanced Calibration?
**A:** Yes, after completing the final assessment.

**Q:** Do I need to bring a laptop to Advanced Calibration?
**A:** Yes, please bring a laptop with the Kestrel Configurator installed.
