# Kestrel KF8 Datasheet

Owner: Kestrel Instruments. Classification: internal.

## Product overview

The Kestrel KF8 is a flow meter sold under SKU KS-1015. It was launched in 2023.

The Kestrel KF8 is designed for continuous operation in demanding industrial environments. It combines a robust enclosure with a measurement core that Kestrel Instruments develops and calibrates in-house, so readings stay stable over long service intervals.

Every Kestrel KF8 leaves the factory with an individual test report. The report documents the final functional test and the configuration loaded before shipment, which simplifies acceptance testing on site.

## Storage and transport

Store the Kestrel KF8 in its original packaging in a dry place, protected from dust, direct sunlight and condensation.

After long storage, check the seals of the Kestrel KF8 before installation and replace them if they have hardened.

Transport the Kestrel KF8 only in packaging that protects it against shocks; damage caused by unsuitable packaging is not covered by the warranty.

## Typical use cases

Data center operators install the Kestrel KF8 in server rooms to detect unfavourable conditions before they affect equipment availability.

In food processing the Kestrel KF8 supports quality assurance, because continuous records replace manual spot checks and make audits easier.

## Mounting

After mechanical installation, check that the Kestrel KF8 is firmly seated and that the housing cover closes without force.

Mount the Kestrel KF8 on a vibration-free surface away from direct sunlight and heat sources. Leave enough clearance around the housing for cable entry and later maintenance work.

## Wiring

The terminals of the Kestrel KF8 accept solid and stranded conductors. Use ferrules for stranded wire and tighten the terminal screws with a suitable screwdriver.

Connect the protective earth of the Kestrel KF8 before connecting the supply. Check the polarity of all terminals against the wiring diagram on the inside of the cover.

## Maintenance

Recalibrate the Kestrel KF8 according to the calibration procedure of Kestrel Instruments or send it to an accredited calibration lab.

Check the cable glands and seals of the Kestrel KF8 for wear during each inspection and replace them if they have become brittle.

## Diagnostics and troubleshooting

| Symptom | Possible cause | Remedy |
|---|---|---|
| Alarm without cause | Alarm limits too tight | Review limits and hysteresis |
| Reading drifts slowly | Sensing element contaminated | Clean or recalibrate the device |
| Configuration rejected | User code active | Enter the user code and retry |
| Condensation in housing | Cable entries facing upwards | Remount with entries facing down |
| Intermittent communication | Missing bus termination | Terminate the bus at both ends |
