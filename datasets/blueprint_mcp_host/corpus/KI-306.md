## History

Over the years Halland Energi AS has standardised several measuring points on Kestrel products, which simplifies spare parts and training on their side.

During the first years Halland Energi AS mainly bought standard products. Later projects added integration services and customer training.

Halland Energi AS is an energy company based in Norway. The relationship started with a small pilot installation and has expanded to several plants since then.

The installed base at Halland Energi AS is documented in the CRM, including installation sites and firmware versions.
