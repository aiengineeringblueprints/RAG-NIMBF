# Incident Report INC-2026-070

Kestrel Instruments, internal document.

## Summary

This report documents incident INC-2026-070 at Bergvik Marine Oy. It follows the blameless postmortem format and focuses on causes and improvements, not on individuals.

Incident INC-2026-070 occurred on 22 September 2025 at Bergvik Marine Oy. The affected product was the Kestrel KQ4 and the incident was classified as severity 3.

## Root cause

Root cause analysis identified an expired TLS certificate on the gateway.

## Other factors

- The affected installation was not on the latest firmware
- Documentation of the installation was incomplete
- The fault was first mistaken for a network problem
- Monitoring did not alert on the first symptoms

## Customer communication

Bergvik Marine Oy received status updates from customer support throughout incident INC-2026-070.

After incident INC-2026-070 the account manager held a review meeting with Bergvik Marine Oy to explain causes and actions.

A written summary of incident INC-2026-070 was sent to Bergvik Marine Oy together with this report.
