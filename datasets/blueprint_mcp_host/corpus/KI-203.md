# Stavanger Warehouse

Kestrel Instruments, internal document.

## Overview

The Stavanger Warehouse in Stavanger, Norway, is headed by Erik Lindqvist, Warehouse Manager.

The Stavanger Warehouse is part of the Kestrel Instruments network of sites and works closely with the headquarters and the other locations.

The Stavanger Warehouse has grown steadily since it opened and is an important part of the company's presence in Norway.

Teams at the Stavanger Warehouse in Stavanger collaborate daily with colleagues at other sites through shared tools and regular video meetings.

## Key facts

The Stavanger warehouse is open from 06:00 to 22:00 on working days. Orders placed before the carrier cutoff at 14:00 leave Stavanger the same day. All customer shipments leave from the Stavanger warehouse.

## Facilities

- Charging points for electric vehicles
- Visitor reception and badge office
- Secure storage for test equipment
- Quiet rooms for focused work
- Staff canteen and coffee corners

## Safety and emergencies

Contractors working at the Stavanger Warehouse receive a site-specific safety briefing before they start.

First aiders and fire wardens are appointed at the Stavanger Warehouse; their names are posted on the notice boards.

Emergency exits and assembly points of the Stavanger Warehouse are marked on the floor plans next to every staircase.

## Directions

Detailed directions to the Stavanger Warehouse are sent with every meeting invitation. Taxis can be ordered at the reception.

The Stavanger Warehouse can be reached by public transport from the city center of Stavanger. Parking for visitors is available next to the main entrance.

## Site history

Over the years the Stavanger Warehouse has taken on additional responsibilities and now supports colleagues and customers across the region.

The Stavanger Warehouse started as a small team and has expanded several times since then. The current building was chosen for its good transport connections in Stavanger.

## Visiting the site

Guests of the Stavanger Warehouse are collected at the reception by their host and accompanied in production and laboratory areas.

Deliveries for the Stavanger Warehouse are accepted at the goods entrance and registered by the facilities team.
