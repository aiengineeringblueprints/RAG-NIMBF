# Incident Report INC-2026-220

Kestrel Instruments, internal document.

## Overview

This report documents incident INC-2026-220 at Brekke Data AB. It follows the blameless postmortem format and focuses on causes and improvements, not on individuals.

Incident INC-2026-220 interrupted measurements at Brekke Data AB and was handled by customer support together with engineering.

Incident INC-2026-220 occurred on 26 March 2026 at Brekke Data AB. The affected product was the Kestrel KD2 and the incident was classified as severity 2.

## Customer impact

Data gaps caused by incident INC-2026-220 were documented and shared with the customer.

Measurements at Brekke Data AB were unavailable or unreliable during the incident. No safety functions were affected.

The final total downtime was 171 minutes.

## How the incident was detected

Incident INC-2026-220 was detected when Brekke Data AB noticed missing values in the control system and opened a support case.

A technician of Brekke Data AB reported unusual readings during a routine inspection, which started incident INC-2026-220.

## Cause

Root cause analysis identified lightning damage to the field wiring.

## Contributing factors

- Spare devices were not available on site
- Documentation of the installation was incomplete
- The affected installation was not on the latest firmware
- Monitoring did not alert on the first symptoms

## Communication

After incident INC-2026-220 the account manager held a review meeting with Brekke Data AB to explain causes and actions.

Brekke Data AB received status updates from customer support throughout incident INC-2026-220.

A written summary of incident INC-2026-220 was sent to Brekke Data AB together with this report.

## Corrective actions

| Action | Owner | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Ship the fix with firmware 1.2.2 | Engineering | done |
| Roll out the fix to all affected installations | Engineering | done |
| Add an alert for the observed symptom | Customer Support | open |
| Review spare part stock for key customers | Logistics | in progress |
| Update the installation checklist | Quality | in progress |

The fix shipped with firmware 1.2.2.

## Lessons learned

- Checklists need regular review after incidents
- Early escalation shortened the investigation
- Remote diagnostics access was essential
