# Kestrel KD2 Installation and Operating Manual

Kestrel Instruments, internal document.

## Product overview

Product overview: the Kestrel KD2 (data logger) is ordered with SKU KS-1030 and has been on the market since it was launched in 2020.

The Kestrel KD2 belongs to the Kestrel data logger range and shares accessories, mounting kits and configuration tools with the other models of its family. Spare parts remain available for the whole support period of the product.

Typical applications of the Kestrel KD2 include process monitoring, building automation and environmental control. The device integrates into existing control systems and does not need proprietary software for basic operation.

Every Kestrel KD2 leaves the factory with an individual test report. The report documents the final functional test and the configuration loaded before shipment, which simplifies acceptance testing on site.

The Kestrel KD2 is designed for continuous operation in demanding industrial environments. It combines a robust enclosure with a measurement core that Kestrel Instruments develops and calibrates in-house, so readings stay stable over long service intervals.

## Safety instructions

Only qualified personnel may install, commission or service the Kestrel KD2. Read this document completely before working on the device and keep it available at the place of installation.

Disconnect the supply voltage before opening the terminal compartment of the Kestrel KD2. Work on live circuits is permitted only where national regulations allow it and the necessary protective measures are in place.

Do not operate the Kestrel KD2 outside the conditions stated in the specification table. Operation outside these limits can damage the device and void the warranty.

If the housing of the Kestrel KD2 is visibly damaged, take the device out of service and contact customer support before reconnecting it.

Modifications to the Kestrel KD2 that are not described in this document are not permitted. Use only original spare parts and accessories supplied by Kestrel Instruments.

## Mounting

After mechanical installation, check that the Kestrel KD2 is firmly seated and that the housing cover closes without force.

Where the Kestrel KD2 is installed in a process line, use the supplied adapter and check the sealing surfaces for damage before tightening the process connection.

The Kestrel KD2 can be mounted on a wall bracket, a pipe clamp or a standard mounting rail. Choose the mounting kit that matches the installation and tighten the screws evenly.

Orient the cable entries of the Kestrel KD2 downwards when installing outdoors, so that condensation cannot collect in the terminal compartment.

Mount the Kestrel KD2 on a vibration-free surface away from direct sunlight and heat sources. Leave enough clearance around the housing for cable entry and later maintenance work.

## Electrical connection

Route signal cables to the Kestrel KD2 separately from power cables and connect the cable shield on the controller side only, to avoid ground loops.

Use shielded twisted-pair cable for the communication line of the Kestrel KD2 and terminate the bus at both ends according to the rules of the fieldbus in use.

Connect the protective earth of the Kestrel KD2 before connecting the supply. Check the polarity of all terminals against the wiring diagram on the inside of the cover.

The terminals of the Kestrel KD2 accept solid and stranded conductors. Use ferrules for stranded wire and tighten the terminal screws with a suitable screwdriver.

Install a suitable fuse in the supply line of the Kestrel KD2 and protect the signal lines against surges where long cable runs leave the building.

## Commissioning

Set the communication address of the Kestrel KD2 before connecting it to a bus with other devices, otherwise address conflicts can interrupt communication.

Compare the first readings of the Kestrel KD2 with a reference instrument and document the result in the commissioning report.

Switch on the supply and wait until the status LED of the Kestrel KD2 indicates normal operation. The device runs a self-test after every start.

Once commissioning is complete, close the housing of the Kestrel KD2, seal unused cable entries and record the configuration in the asset management system.

## Configuration

A factory reset restores the delivery configuration of the Kestrel KD2. The calibration data is kept, but communication settings must be entered again.

Alarm limits, filter settings and engineering units of the Kestrel KD2 can be changed during operation. Changes take effect immediately and are logged in the device event list.

Configuration profiles of the Kestrel KD2 can be exported and imported, which speeds up the commissioning of many identical measuring points.

Access to the configuration of the Kestrel KD2 can be protected with a user code. Without the code, settings are visible but cannot be changed.

The Kestrel KD2 is configured with the Kestrel Configurator, either locally through the service interface or remotely over the fieldbus.

## Normal operation

The status LED of the Kestrel KD2 shows normal operation, maintenance requests and faults with different colours and blink patterns.

Diagnostic messages of the Kestrel KD2 follow the NAMUR recommendation and distinguish between failure, function check, out of specification and maintenance required.

During normal operation the Kestrel KD2 needs no user intervention. Measured values and diagnostic information are available continuously through the communication interface.

Readings of the Kestrel KD2 can be logged in the control system or in a Kestrel data logger for later analysis.

## Care and maintenance

Recalibrate the Kestrel KD2 according to the calibration procedure of Kestrel Instruments or send it to an accredited calibration lab.

Firmware updates for the Kestrel KD2 are distributed through the Kestrel service portal. Read the release notes before updating devices in the field.

Do not use solvents or high-pressure cleaners on the Kestrel KD2; they can damage the seals and the sensing element.

Check the cable glands and seals of the Kestrel KD2 for wear during each inspection and replace them if they have become brittle.

Inspect the Kestrel KD2 during scheduled plant shutdowns, clean the housing with a dry cloth, and record every intervention in the asset log.

## Diagnostics and troubleshooting

| Symptom | Possible cause | Remedy |
|---|---|---|
| No reading | No supply voltage | Check fuse, wiring and supply polarity |
| Reading drifts slowly | Sensing element contaminated | Clean or recalibrate the device |
| Configuration rejected | User code active | Enter the user code and retry |
| Unstable reading | Electrical interference | Check shielding and cable routing |
| Status LED shows a fault | Internal self-test failed | Restart; contact support if it persists |
| Intermittent communication | Missing bus termination | Terminate the bus at both ends |

## FAQ

**Q:** How do I find the firmware version of my Kestrel KD2?
**A:** The configurator shows it on the device information page; the current release is listed in the firmware release notes.

**Q:** Is the Kestrel KD2 suitable for hazardous areas?
**A:** Only variants with an explicit hazardous area certification may be used there; check the nameplate before installation.

**Q:** Do I need special software for the Kestrel KD2?
**A:** No. The device works with standard control systems; the Kestrel Configurator is only needed for advanced settings.

**Q:** How is the Kestrel KD2 protected against unauthorised changes?
**A:** A user code can lock the configuration; without it settings can be read but not changed.

**Q:** Can the Kestrel KD2 be mounted next to a frequency converter?
**A:** Keep as much distance as possible and use shielded cables to limit electromagnetic interference.

**Q:** Can I replace the sensing element of the Kestrel KD2 myself?
**A:** Only trained service staff should replace it, because the device must be recalibrated afterwards.

**Q:** Can the Kestrel KD2 be used outdoors?
**A:** Yes, provided the enclosure rating stated in the specification table matches the installation conditions and the cable entries face downwards.

**Q:** Where do I order spare parts for the Kestrel KD2?
**A:** Spare parts are ordered through the Kestrel sales office responsible for your country.
