# Incident Report INC-2026-068

Owner: Kestrel Instruments. Classification: internal.

## Summary

This report documents incident INC-2026-068 at Hedberg Foods GmbH. It follows the blameless postmortem format and focuses on causes and improvements, not on individuals.

Incident INC-2026-068 interrupted measurements at Hedberg Foods GmbH and was handled by customer support together with engineering.

Incident INC-2026-068 occurred on 19 August 2026 at Hedberg Foods GmbH. The affected product was the Kestrel KD2 and the incident was classified as severity 2.

## Detection

A technician of Hedberg Foods GmbH reported unusual readings during a routine inspection, which started incident INC-2026-068.

Incident INC-2026-068 was detected when Hedberg Foods GmbH noticed missing values in the control system and opened a support case.

## Customer impact

Measurements at Hedberg Foods GmbH were unavailable or unreliable during the incident. No safety functions were affected.

The customer had to fall back on manual readings while incident INC-2026-068 was open.

Data gaps caused by incident INC-2026-068 were documented and shared with the customer.

The final total downtime was 259 minutes.

## Cause

Root cause analysis identified lightning damage to the field wiring.

## Customer communication

A written summary of incident INC-2026-068 was sent to Hedberg Foods GmbH together with this report.

After incident INC-2026-068 the account manager held a review meeting with Hedberg Foods GmbH to explain causes and actions.

Hedberg Foods GmbH received status updates from customer support throughout incident INC-2026-068.

## Other factors

- Monitoring did not alert on the first symptoms
- Spare devices were not available on site

## Corrective actions

| Action | Owner | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Ship the fix with firmware 2.1.6 | Engineering | done |
| Add an alert for the observed symptom | Customer Support | done |
| Share lessons learned in the engineering forum | Engineering | open |

The fix shipped with firmware 2.1.6.

## Follow-up

Similar installations at other customers were checked for the conditions that led to incident INC-2026-068.

The effectiveness of the corrective actions for incident INC-2026-068 will be checked in the next quality review.

The knowledge base was updated with the diagnosis steps used during incident INC-2026-068.

## Lessons learned

- Early escalation shortened the investigation
- Checklists need regular review after incidents
