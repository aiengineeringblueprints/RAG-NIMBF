# Incident Report INC-2026-206

Kestrel Instruments, internal document.

## Overview

Incident INC-2026-206 interrupted measurements at Torvik Kraft AS and was handled by customer support together with engineering.

This report documents incident INC-2026-206 at Torvik Kraft AS. It follows the blameless postmortem format and focuses on causes and improvements, not on individuals.

Incident INC-2026-206 occurred on 22 February 2026 at Torvik Kraft AS. The affected product was the Kestrel KQ5 and the incident was classified as severity 2.

## How the incident was detected

Incident INC-2026-206 was detected when Torvik Kraft AS noticed missing values in the control system and opened a support case.

Monitoring at Torvik Kraft AS raised a communication alarm, which led to the investigation of incident INC-2026-206.

## Customer impact

Data gaps caused by incident INC-2026-206 were documented and shared with the customer.

The customer had to fall back on manual readings while incident INC-2026-206 was open.

Measurements at Torvik Kraft AS were unavailable or unreliable during the incident. No safety functions were affected.

The final total downtime was 673 minutes.

## Cause

Root cause analysis identified a clock synchronisation error.

## Contributing factors

- The fault was first mistaken for a network problem
- The affected installation was not on the latest firmware

## Customer communication

Torvik Kraft AS received status updates from customer support throughout incident INC-2026-206.

A written summary of incident INC-2026-206 was sent to Torvik Kraft AS together with this report.

After incident INC-2026-206 the account manager held a review meeting with Torvik Kraft AS to explain causes and actions.

## Corrective actions

| Action | Owner | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Ship the fix with firmware 2.3.0 | Engineering | done |
| Roll out the fix to all affected installations | Engineering | in progress |
| Update the installation checklist | Quality | done |
| Share lessons learned in the engineering forum | Engineering | in progress |

The fix shipped with firmware 2.3.0.

## Lessons learned

- Checklists need regular review after incidents
- Clear customer communication reduced pressure on the team
- Early escalation shortened the investigation

## Next steps

The effectiveness of the corrective actions for incident INC-2026-206 will be checked in the next quality review.

Similar installations at other customers were checked for the conditions that led to incident INC-2026-206.

The knowledge base was updated with the diagnosis steps used during incident INC-2026-206.
