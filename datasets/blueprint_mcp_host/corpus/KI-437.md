# Project Brief PRJ-237: Glacier Diagnostics Upgrade

Kestrel Instruments. Internal use only.

## Context

Ostrand Foods AB commissioned project PRJ-237 (Glacier Diagnostics Upgrade).

The goal of project PRJ-237 is to improve data quality and reduce maintenance effort for Ostrand Foods AB.

Project PRJ-237 replaces manual readings with continuous monitoring, so that Ostrand Foods AB can react to deviations faster.

Ostrand Foods AB commissioned project PRJ-237 to modernise measurement and monitoring at one of its sites. The existing equipment had reached the end of its service life.

## Goals

- Replace legacy instruments with current Kestrel devices
- Provide remote diagnostics for the service team
- Document the installation for later audits
- Reduce manual inspection rounds
- Train the customer's technicians

## Communication

Weekly calls between the project teams of Ostrand Foods AB and Kestrel keep project PRJ-237 on track between steering committee meetings.

Decisions in project PRJ-237 are documented in the decision log and confirmed by email.

Project PRJ-237 uses a shared workspace for documents, meeting minutes and open points.

## Scope of delivery

Project PRJ-237 includes training for the customer's maintenance staff and a documentation package for audits.

Civil works and cabling outside the measuring points are not part of project PRJ-237 and are provided by Ostrand Foods AB.

The scope of project PRJ-237 covers delivery, installation and commissioning of Kestrel KH3 devices and their integration into the customer's control system.

Optional services in project PRJ-237, such as extended support, are offered separately.
