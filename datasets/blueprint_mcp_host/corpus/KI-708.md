# Training Course: Flow Measurement in Practice

Kestrel Instruments. Internal use only.

## Description

Flow Measurement in Practice: 2 days, trainer Sofia Ostby.

Participants of Flow Measurement in Practice work in small groups, so trainers can respond to individual questions.

Flow Measurement in Practice combines short theory blocks with hands-on exercises on real devices.

The course Flow Measurement in Practice gives participants the practical knowledge they need in daily work with Kestrel products.

It accepts at most 7 participants and costs 1650 EUR per person.

The course has no prerequisites.

## Materials

Demo devices and tools for Flow Measurement in Practice are provided by Kestrel; participants do not need to bring equipment apart from a laptop.

Participants of Flow Measurement in Practice receive the course slides and exercise descriptions in digital form.

After Flow Measurement in Practice participants keep access to the recorded theory blocks for later reference.

## Target audience

Flow Measurement in Practice is aimed at technicians and engineers who install, configure or maintain Kestrel products.

Customers, partners and Kestrel employees can register for Flow Measurement in Practice.

Participants of Flow Measurement in Practice should be familiar with basic measurement technology.

## Learning objectives

- Document work according to company standards
- Diagnose typical faults
- Use the service portal efficiently
- Plan maintenance tasks
- Configure devices with the Kestrel Configurator

## Certification

Flow Measurement in Practice ends with a short practical assessment on a demo device.

Participants who do not pass the assessment of Flow Measurement in Practice can repeat it at a later date free of charge.

## Course agenda

### Day 1

- Hands-on exercise on demo devices
- Theory block
- Final assessment
- Introduction and safety briefing
- Group work on case studies

### Day 2

- Review and questions
- Hands-on exercise on demo devices
- Introduction and safety briefing
