# Warranty Policy

Kestrel Instruments, internal document.

## Purpose of this policy

This document describes the rules of the Warranty Policy at Kestrel Instruments and explains why they exist. It gives employees and managers a common reference for daily decisions.

The Warranty Policy protects employees, customers and the company by setting clear and fair expectations. It applies equally at every Kestrel site.

The Warranty Policy turns legal requirements and company values into practical rules. Where local law sets stricter requirements, local law takes precedence.

## Background

The Warranty Policy is reviewed together with employee representatives, so that changes are understood and accepted.

The Warranty Policy was introduced to make expectations consistent across all Kestrel sites after the company grew beyond its original headquarters.

Feedback from employees and managers shaped the current Warranty Policy; it aims to be simple to apply and fair for everybody.

## Definitions

- **Employee**: a person with an employment contract with Kestrel Instruments
- **Intranet**: the internal information portal of Kestrel Instruments
- **Manager**: an employee with formal responsibility for a team or cost center
- **Contractor**: a person working for Kestrel through a service agreement
- **Site**: any office, plant, lab or warehouse operated by Kestrel Instruments

## Applicability

Temporary staff and interns follow the Warranty Policy in the same way as permanent employees.

The Warranty Policy applies to all employees and contractors of Kestrel Instruments unless a local works agreement states otherwise.

Managers are responsible for making sure that their teams know the Warranty Policy and apply it.

## Requirements

The warranty period of each product is stated in its datasheet. Warranty claims must be filed within 60 days after a defect is discovered. Repairs under warranty are completed within 10 working days.

## Responsibilities

HR maintains the Warranty Policy, answers questions about it and reports on its use to the management team.

Employees follow the Warranty Policy and ask their manager or HR when something is unclear.

The document owner of the Warranty Policy reviews it regularly and publishes changes on the intranet.

Managers apply the Warranty Policy consistently and document exceptions they approve.

## Exceptions

Exceptions to the Warranty Policy require written approval from the responsible manager and HR.

Repeated or deliberate violations of the Warranty Policy can lead to disciplinary measures.

Approved exceptions to the Warranty Policy are documented and reviewed when the policy is updated.

## FAQ

**Q:** Where can I find the current version of the Warranty Policy?
**A:** The current version is always published on the intranet; printed copies may be outdated.

**Q:** Are earlier versions of the Warranty Policy still valid?
**A:** No. Earlier versions are archived for audit purposes only and must not be applied.

**Q:** Who answers questions about the Warranty Policy?
**A:** Your manager is the first contact; HR helps with questions the manager cannot answer.

## Process

1. Wait for the confirmation before acting on the request
2. Read the relevant section of the Warranty Policy before starting the process
3. Discuss open questions with your manager

## Contacts

The intranet page of the Warranty Policy lists the responsible HR contact for each site.

Questions about the Warranty Policy go to your manager first. HR business partners support managers and employees with the interpretation.
