# Kestrel KW8 Datasheet

Kestrel Instruments, internal document.

## Overview

Product overview: the Kestrel KW8 (wireless gateway) is ordered with SKU KS-1035 and has been on the market since it was launched in 2018.

The Kestrel KW8 belongs to the Kestrel wireless gateway range and shares accessories, mounting kits and configuration tools with the other models of its family. Spare parts remain available for the whole support period of the product.

The Kestrel KW8 is designed for continuous operation in demanding industrial environments. It combines a robust enclosure with a measurement core that Kestrel Instruments develops and calibrates in-house, so readings stay stable over long service intervals.

## Transport and storage

Transport the Kestrel KW8 only in packaging that protects it against shocks; damage caused by unsuitable packaging is not covered by the warranty.

Store the Kestrel KW8 in its original packaging in a dry place, protected from dust, direct sunlight and condensation.

After long storage, check the seals of the Kestrel KW8 before installation and replace them if they have hardened.

## Mechanical installation

Orient the cable entries of the Kestrel KW8 downwards when installing outdoors, so that condensation cannot collect in the terminal compartment.

Where the Kestrel KW8 is installed in a process line, use the supplied adapter and check the sealing surfaces for damage before tightening the process connection.

## Electrical connection

The terminals of the Kestrel KW8 accept solid and stranded conductors. Use ferrules for stranded wire and tighten the terminal screws with a suitable screwdriver.

Route signal cables to the Kestrel KW8 separately from power cables and connect the cable shield on the controller side only, to avoid ground loops.

## Care and maintenance

Do not use solvents or high-pressure cleaners on the Kestrel KW8; they can damage the seals and the sensing element.

Check the cable glands and seals of the Kestrel KW8 for wear during each inspection and replace them if they have become brittle.

## Frequently asked questions

**Q:** Is the Kestrel KW8 suitable for hazardous areas?
**A:** Only variants with an explicit hazardous area certification may be used there; check the nameplate before installation.

**Q:** How is the Kestrel KW8 protected against unauthorised changes?
**A:** A user code can lock the configuration; without it settings can be read but not changed.

**Q:** Can I replace the sensing element of the Kestrel KW8 myself?
**A:** Only trained service staff should replace it, because the device must be recalibrated afterwards.

**Q:** Do I need special software for the Kestrel KW8?
**A:** No. The device works with standard control systems; the Kestrel Configurator is only needed for advanced settings.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Possible cause | Remedy |
|---|---|---|
| Alarm without cause | Alarm limits too tight | Review limits and hysteresis |
| Values frozen | Device in function-check mode | Leave service mode in the configurator |
| Configuration rejected | User code active | Enter the user code and retry |
| Reading drifts slowly | Sensing element contaminated | Clean or recalibrate the device |
| No communication | Wrong bus address | Set a unique address and restart |
