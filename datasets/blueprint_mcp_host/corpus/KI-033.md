# Kestrel KW3 Datasheet

Kestrel Instruments, internal document.

## Product overview

Product overview: the Kestrel KW3 (wireless gateway) is ordered with SKU KS-1033 and has been on the market since it was launched in 2018.

Every Kestrel KW3 leaves the factory with an individual test report. The report documents the final functional test and the configuration loaded before shipment, which simplifies acceptance testing on site.

## Mounting

After mechanical installation, check that the Kestrel KW3 is firmly seated and that the housing cover closes without force.

## Wiring

Connect the protective earth of the Kestrel KW3 before connecting the supply. Check the polarity of all terminals against the wiring diagram on the inside of the cover.

## Care and maintenance

Recalibrate the Kestrel KW3 according to the calibration procedure of Kestrel Instruments or send it to an accredited calibration lab.
