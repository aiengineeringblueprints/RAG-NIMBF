# Kestrel KD8 Installation and Operating Manual

Kestrel Instruments, internal document.

## Overview

The Kestrel KD8 is a data logger sold under SKU KS-1032. It was launched in 2020.

The Kestrel KD8 belongs to the Kestrel data logger range and shares accessories, mounting kits and configuration tools with the other models of its family. Spare parts remain available for the whole support period of the product.

Every Kestrel KD8 leaves the factory with an individual test report. The report documents the final functional test and the configuration loaded before shipment, which simplifies acceptance testing on site.

Typical applications of the Kestrel KD8 include process monitoring, building automation and environmental control. The device integrates into existing control systems and does not need proprietary software for basic operation.

The Kestrel KD8 is designed for continuous operation in demanding industrial environments. It combines a robust enclosure with a measurement core that Kestrel Instruments develops and calibrates in-house, so readings stay stable over long service intervals.

## For your safety

Modifications to the Kestrel KD8 that are not described in this document are not permitted. Use only original spare parts and accessories supplied by Kestrel Instruments.

Disconnect the supply voltage before opening the terminal compartment of the Kestrel KD8. Work on live circuits is permitted only where national regulations allow it and the necessary protective measures are in place.

Do not operate the Kestrel KD8 outside the conditions stated in the specification table. Operation outside these limits can damage the device and void the warranty.

If the housing of the Kestrel KD8 is visibly damaged, take the device out of service and contact customer support before reconnecting it.

Only qualified personnel may install, commission or service the Kestrel KD8. Read this document completely before working on the device and keep it available at the place of installation.

## Mechanical installation

Where the Kestrel KD8 is installed in a process line, use the supplied adapter and check the sealing surfaces for damage before tightening the process connection.

Orient the cable entries of the Kestrel KD8 downwards when installing outdoors, so that condensation cannot collect in the terminal compartment.

The Kestrel KD8 can be mounted on a wall bracket, a pipe clamp or a standard mounting rail. Choose the mounting kit that matches the installation and tighten the screws evenly.

After mechanical installation, check that the Kestrel KD8 is firmly seated and that the housing cover closes without force.

Mount the Kestrel KD8 on a vibration-free surface away from direct sunlight and heat sources. Leave enough clearance around the housing for cable entry and later maintenance work.

## Electrical connection

Use shielded twisted-pair cable for the communication line of the Kestrel KD8 and terminate the bus at both ends according to the rules of the fieldbus in use.

The terminals of the Kestrel KD8 accept solid and stranded conductors. Use ferrules for stranded wire and tighten the terminal screws with a suitable screwdriver.

Connect the protective earth of the Kestrel KD8 before connecting the supply. Check the polarity of all terminals against the wiring diagram on the inside of the cover.

Route signal cables to the Kestrel KD8 separately from power cables and connect the cable shield on the controller side only, to avoid ground loops.

Install a suitable fuse in the supply line of the Kestrel KD8 and protect the signal lines against surges where long cable runs leave the building.

## Start-up

Set the communication address of the Kestrel KD8 before connecting it to a bus with other devices, otherwise address conflicts can interrupt communication.

Switch on the supply and wait until the status LED of the Kestrel KD8 indicates normal operation. The device runs a self-test after every start.

Compare the first readings of the Kestrel KD8 with a reference instrument and document the result in the commissioning report.

Once commissioning is complete, close the housing of the Kestrel KD8, seal unused cable entries and record the configuration in the asset management system.

## Configuration

The Kestrel KD8 is configured with the Kestrel Configurator, either locally through the service interface or remotely over the fieldbus.

A factory reset restores the delivery configuration of the Kestrel KD8. The calibration data is kept, but communication settings must be entered again.

Access to the configuration of the Kestrel KD8 can be protected with a user code. Without the code, settings are visible but cannot be changed.

Alarm limits, filter settings and engineering units of the Kestrel KD8 can be changed during operation. Changes take effect immediately and are logged in the device event list.

Configuration profiles of the Kestrel KD8 can be exported and imported, which speeds up the commissioning of many identical measuring points.

## Operation

During normal operation the Kestrel KD8 needs no user intervention. Measured values and diagnostic information are available continuously through the communication interface.

Readings of the Kestrel KD8 can be logged in the control system or in a Kestrel data logger for later analysis.

The status LED of the Kestrel KD8 shows normal operation, maintenance requests and faults with different colours and blink patterns.

Diagnostic messages of the Kestrel KD8 follow the NAMUR recommendation and distinguish between failure, function check, out of specification and maintenance required.

## Service and maintenance

Firmware updates for the Kestrel KD8 are distributed through the Kestrel service portal. Read the release notes before updating devices in the field.

Check the cable glands and seals of the Kestrel KD8 for wear during each inspection and replace them if they have become brittle.

Do not use solvents or high-pressure cleaners on the Kestrel KD8; they can damage the seals and the sensing element.

Recalibrate the Kestrel KD8 according to the calibration procedure of Kestrel Instruments or send it to an accredited calibration lab.

Inspect the Kestrel KD8 during scheduled plant shutdowns, clean the housing with a dry cloth, and record every intervention in the asset log.

## Diagnostics and troubleshooting

| Symptom | Possible cause | Remedy |
|---|---|---|
| Unstable reading | Electrical interference | Check shielding and cable routing |
| Status LED shows a fault | Internal self-test failed | Restart; contact support if it persists |
| No reading | No supply voltage | Check fuse, wiring and supply polarity |
| Values frozen | Device in function-check mode | Leave service mode in the configurator |
| Condensation in housing | Cable entries facing upwards | Remount with entries facing down |
| Configuration rejected | User code active | Enter the user code and retry |
| No communication | Wrong bus address | Set a unique address and restart |

## Questions and answers

**Q:** Can the Kestrel KD8 be used outdoors?
**A:** Yes, provided the enclosure rating stated in the specification table matches the installation conditions and the cable entries face downwards.

**Q:** Where do I order spare parts for the Kestrel KD8?
**A:** Spare parts are ordered through the Kestrel sales office responsible for your country.

**Q:** Do I need special software for the Kestrel KD8?
**A:** No. The device works with standard control systems; the Kestrel Configurator is only needed for advanced settings.

**Q:** How is the Kestrel KD8 protected against unauthorised changes?
**A:** A user code can lock the configuration; without it settings can be read but not changed.

**Q:** Can the Kestrel KD8 be mounted next to a frequency converter?
**A:** Keep as much distance as possible and use shielded cables to limit electromagnetic interference.

**Q:** Can I replace the sensing element of the Kestrel KD8 myself?
**A:** Only trained service staff should replace it, because the device must be recalibrated afterwards.

**Q:** How do I find the firmware version of my Kestrel KD8?
**A:** The configurator shows it on the device information page; the current release is listed in the firmware release notes.
