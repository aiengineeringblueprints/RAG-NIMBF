# Incident Report INC-2026-225

Kestrel Instruments, internal document.

## Overview

Incident INC-2026-225 interrupted measurements at Halland Marine GmbH and was handled by customer support together with engineering.

This report documents incident INC-2026-225 at Halland Marine GmbH. It follows the blameless postmortem format and focuses on causes and improvements, not on individuals.

Incident INC-2026-225 occurred on 19 November 2025 at Halland Marine GmbH. The affected product was the Kestrel KV4 and the incident was classified as severity 3.

## Impact

The customer had to fall back on manual readings while incident INC-2026-225 was open.

Measurements at Halland Marine GmbH were unavailable or unreliable during the incident. No safety functions were affected.

Data gaps caused by incident INC-2026-225 were documented and shared with the customer.

The final total downtime was 326 minutes.

## Detection

Monitoring at Halland Marine GmbH raised a communication alarm, which led to the investigation of incident INC-2026-225.

A technician of Halland Marine GmbH reported unusual readings during a routine inspection, which started incident INC-2026-225.

## Root cause

Root cause analysis identified a corroded connector.

## Communication

Halland Marine GmbH received status updates from customer support throughout incident INC-2026-225.

After incident INC-2026-225 the account manager held a review meeting with Halland Marine GmbH to explain causes and actions.

A written summary of incident INC-2026-225 was sent to Halland Marine GmbH together with this report.

## Other factors

- Spare devices were not available on site
- Monitoring did not alert on the first symptoms

## Corrective actions

| Action | Owner | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Ship the fix with firmware 5.1.4 | Engineering | done |
| Share lessons learned in the engineering forum | Engineering | open |
| Roll out the fix to all affected installations | Engineering | open |

The fix shipped with firmware 5.1.4.

## Lessons learned

- Early escalation shortened the investigation
- Remote diagnostics access was essential

## Follow-up

The knowledge base was updated with the diagnosis steps used during incident INC-2026-225.

The effectiveness of the corrective actions for incident INC-2026-225 will be checked in the next quality review.

Similar installations at other customers were checked for the conditions that led to incident INC-2026-225.
