# Kestrel KH5 Datasheet

Kestrel Instruments, internal document.

## Introduction

The Kestrel KH5 is a humidity sensor sold under SKU KS-1002. It was launched in 2024.

Every Kestrel KH5 leaves the factory with an individual test report. The report documents the final functional test and the configuration loaded before shipment, which simplifies acceptance testing on site.

Typical applications of the Kestrel KH5 include process monitoring, building automation and environmental control. The device integrates into existing control systems and does not need proprietary software for basic operation.

## Transport and storage

Transport the Kestrel KH5 only in packaging that protects it against shocks; damage caused by unsuitable packaging is not covered by the warranty.

After long storage, check the seals of the Kestrel KH5 before installation and replace them if they have hardened.

Store the Kestrel KH5 in its original packaging in a dry place, protected from dust, direct sunlight and condensation.

## Mounting

Mount the Kestrel KH5 on a vibration-free surface away from direct sunlight and heat sources. Leave enough clearance around the housing for cable entry and later maintenance work.

The Kestrel KH5 can be mounted on a wall bracket, a pipe clamp or a standard mounting rail. Choose the mounting kit that matches the installation and tighten the screws evenly.

## Wiring

Use shielded twisted-pair cable for the communication line of the Kestrel KH5 and terminate the bus at both ends according to the rules of the fieldbus in use.

Connect the protective earth of the Kestrel KH5 before connecting the supply. Check the polarity of all terminals against the wiring diagram on the inside of the cover.

## Service and maintenance

Recalibrate the Kestrel KH5 according to the calibration procedure of Kestrel Instruments or send it to an accredited calibration lab.

Firmware updates for the Kestrel KH5 are distributed through the Kestrel service portal. Read the release notes before updating devices in the field.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Possible cause | Remedy |
|---|---|---|
| Condensation in housing | Cable entries facing upwards | Remount with entries facing down |
| Reading drifts slowly | Sensing element contaminated | Clean or recalibrate the device |
| Status LED shows a fault | Internal self-test failed | Restart; contact support if it persists |
| No communication | Wrong bus address | Set a unique address and restart |
| Configuration rejected | User code active | Enter the user code and retry |
