# Kestrel KP9 Datasheet

Kestrel Instruments, internal document.

## Product overview

Product overview: the Kestrel KP9 (pressure transmitter) is ordered with SKU KS-1012 and has been on the market since it was launched in 2015.

The Kestrel KP9 belongs to the Kestrel pressure transmitter range and shares accessories, mounting kits and configuration tools with the other models of its family. Spare parts remain available for the whole support period of the product.

Typical applications of the Kestrel KP9 include process monitoring, building automation and environmental control. The device integrates into existing control systems and does not need proprietary software for basic operation.

## Technical data

Transport the Kestrel KP9 only in packaging that protects it against shocks; damage caused by unsuitable packaging is not covered by the warranty.

## Mounting

After mechanical installation, check that the Kestrel KP9 is firmly seated and that the housing cover closes without force.

The Kestrel KP9 can be mounted on a wall bracket, a pipe clamp or a standard mounting rail. Choose the mounting kit that matches the installation and tighten the screws evenly.

## Wiring

The terminals of the Kestrel KP9 accept solid and stranded conductors. Use ferrules for stranded wire and tighten the terminal screws with a suitable screwdriver.

Route signal cables to the Kestrel KP9 separately from power cables and connect the cable shield on the controller side only, to avoid ground loops.

## Service and maintenance

Check the cable glands and seals of the Kestrel KP9 for wear during each inspection and replace them if they have become brittle.

Firmware updates for the Kestrel KP9 are distributed through the Kestrel service portal. Read the release notes before updating devices in the field.

## Diagnostics and troubleshooting

| Symptom | Possible cause | Remedy |
|---|---|---|
| Intermittent communication | Missing bus termination | Terminate the bus at both ends |
| Values frozen | Device in function-check mode | Leave service mode in the configurator |
| Condensation in housing | Cable entries facing upwards | Remount with entries facing down |
| Alarm without cause | Alarm limits too tight | Review limits and hysteresis |

## FAQ

**Q:** How is the Kestrel KP9 protected against unauthorised changes?
**A:** A user code can lock the configuration; without it settings can be read but not changed.

**Q:** Can the Kestrel KP9 be used outdoors?
**A:** Yes, provided the enclosure rating stated in the specification table matches the installation conditions and the cable entries face downwards.

**Q:** Can I replace the sensing element of the Kestrel KP9 myself?
**A:** Only trained service staff should replace it, because the device must be recalibrated afterwards.
