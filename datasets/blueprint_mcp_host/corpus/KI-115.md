# Training Budget Policy

Kestrel Instruments, internal document.

## Purpose

The Training Budget Policy turns legal requirements and company values into practical rules. Where local law sets stricter requirements, local law takes precedence.

The Training Budget Policy protects employees, customers and the company by setting clear and fair expectations. It applies equally at every Kestrel site.

This document describes the rules of the Training Budget Policy at Kestrel Instruments and explains why they exist. It gives employees and managers a common reference for daily decisions.

## Scope

Managers are responsible for making sure that their teams know the Training Budget Policy and apply it.

Temporary staff and interns follow the Training Budget Policy in the same way as permanent employees.

The Training Budget Policy applies to all employees and contractors of Kestrel Instruments unless a local works agreement states otherwise.

## Background

Feedback from employees and managers shaped the current Training Budget Policy; it aims to be simple to apply and fair for everybody.

Audits and legal requirements also influenced the Training Budget Policy, which therefore uses clear and verifiable rules.

The Training Budget Policy was introduced to make expectations consistent across all Kestrel sites after the company grew beyond its original headquarters.

## Terms used

- **Intranet**: the internal information portal of Kestrel Instruments
- **Contractor**: a person working for Kestrel through a service agreement
- **Site**: any office, plant, lab or warehouse operated by Kestrel Instruments

## Rules

Each employee has an annual training budget of 1200 EUR. External training requires approval from the line manager. Unused training budget does not carry over to the next year.

## FAQ

**Q:** Are earlier versions of the Training Budget Policy still valid?
**A:** No. Earlier versions are archived for audit purposes only and must not be applied.

**Q:** What happens if the Training Budget Policy conflicts with local law?
**A:** Local law always takes precedence, and HR documents the deviation.

**Q:** Who answers questions about the Training Budget Policy?
**A:** Your manager is the first contact; HR helps with questions the manager cannot answer.

**Q:** Where can I find the current version of the Training Budget Policy?
**A:** The current version is always published on the intranet; printed copies may be outdated.

## Process

1. Keep the confirmation for your records
2. Attach the required documents
3. Submit the request through the HR portal
4. Wait for the confirmation before acting on the request
5. Read the relevant section of the Training Budget Policy before starting the process
6. Discuss open questions with your manager

## Exceptions

Repeated or deliberate violations of the Training Budget Policy can lead to disciplinary measures.

Approved exceptions to the Training Budget Policy are documented and reviewed when the policy is updated.

Exceptions to the Training Budget Policy require written approval from the responsible manager and HR.

## Responsibilities

Employees follow the Training Budget Policy and ask their manager or HR when something is unclear.

The document owner of the Training Budget Policy reviews it regularly and publishes changes on the intranet.

Managers apply the Training Budget Policy consistently and document exceptions they approve.

HR maintains the Training Budget Policy, answers questions about it and reports on its use to the management team.

## Questions and contacts

The intranet page of the Training Budget Policy lists the responsible HR contact for each site.

For urgent questions about the Training Budget Policy outside office hours, contact the duty manager of your site.
