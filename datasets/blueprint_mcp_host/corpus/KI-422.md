# Project Brief PRJ-222: Aurora Monitoring Expansion

Kestrel Instruments. Internal use only.

## Introduction

Project PRJ-222, Aurora Monitoring Expansion, is delivered for Nessby Kraft GmbH.

Project PRJ-222 replaces manual readings with continuous monitoring, so that Nessby Kraft GmbH can react to deviations faster.

Nessby Kraft GmbH commissioned project PRJ-222 to modernise measurement and monitoring at one of its sites. The existing equipment had reached the end of its service life.

The goal of project PRJ-222 is to improve data quality and reduce maintenance effort for Nessby Kraft GmbH.

## Financials

The initial estimate was 357000 EUR. After the scope review the steering committee approved a budget of 367500 EUR.

## Scope

Project PRJ-222 includes training for the customer's maintenance staff and a documentation package for audits.

The scope of project PRJ-222 covers delivery, installation and commissioning of Kestrel KP5 devices and their integration into the customer's control system.

Civil works and cabling outside the measuring points are not part of project PRJ-222 and are provided by Nessby Kraft GmbH.

Optional services in project PRJ-222, such as extended support, are offered separately.
