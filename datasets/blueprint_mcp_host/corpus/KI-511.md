# Incident Report INC-2026-080

Kestrel Instruments, internal document.

## Summary

Incident INC-2026-080 interrupted measurements at Holmen Kraft Oy and was handled by customer support together with engineering.

This report documents incident INC-2026-080 at Holmen Kraft Oy. It follows the blameless postmortem format and focuses on causes and improvements, not on individuals.

Incident INC-2026-080 occurred on 18 January 2026 at Holmen Kraft Oy. The affected product was the Kestrel KP8 and the incident was classified as severity 2.

## Impact

The customer had to fall back on manual readings while incident INC-2026-080 was open.

Data gaps caused by incident INC-2026-080 were documented and shared with the customer.

Measurements at Holmen Kraft Oy were unavailable or unreliable during the incident. No safety functions were affected.

The final total downtime was 59 minutes.

## How the incident was detected

A technician of Holmen Kraft Oy reported unusual readings during a routine inspection, which started incident INC-2026-080.

Monitoring at Holmen Kraft Oy raised a communication alarm, which led to the investigation of incident INC-2026-080.

## Root cause analysis

The root cause was a firmware memory leak.

## Contributing factors

- Documentation of the installation was incomplete
- The fault was first mistaken for a network problem
- Monitoring did not alert on the first symptoms
- The affected installation was not on the latest firmware

## Lessons learned

- Remote diagnostics access was essential
- Checklists need regular review after incidents
