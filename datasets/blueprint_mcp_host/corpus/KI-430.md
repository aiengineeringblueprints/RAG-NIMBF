# Project Brief PRJ-230: Glacier Diagnostics Expansion

Owner: Kestrel Instruments. Classification: internal.

## Introduction

Project PRJ-230, Glacier Diagnostics Expansion, is delivered for Falkberg Foods GmbH.

Project PRJ-230 replaces manual readings with continuous monitoring, so that Falkberg Foods GmbH can react to deviations faster.

The goal of project PRJ-230 is to improve data quality and reduce maintenance effort for Falkberg Foods GmbH.

Falkberg Foods GmbH commissioned project PRJ-230 to modernise measurement and monitoring at one of its sites. The existing equipment had reached the end of its service life.

## Scope

Civil works and cabling outside the measuring points are not part of project PRJ-230 and are provided by Falkberg Foods GmbH.

Optional services in project PRJ-230, such as extended support, are offered separately.

Project PRJ-230 includes training for the customer's maintenance staff and a documentation package for audits.

The scope of project PRJ-230 covers delivery, installation and commissioning of Kestrel KT4 devices and their integration into the customer's control system.

## Communication

Weekly calls between the project teams of Falkberg Foods GmbH and Kestrel keep project PRJ-230 on track between steering committee meetings.

Project PRJ-230 uses a shared workspace for documents, meeting minutes and open points.

Decisions in project PRJ-230 are documented in the decision log and confirmed by email.

## Objectives

- Provide remote diagnostics for the service team
- Reduce manual inspection rounds
- Hand over a validated configuration backup

## Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
