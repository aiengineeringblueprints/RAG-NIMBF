# Business Travel Policy 2022 (superseded)

Kestrel Instruments, internal document.

**Status: superseded.** This version is archived and no longer valid.

## Purpose of this policy

The Business Travel Policy 2022 turns legal requirements and company values into practical rules. Where local law sets stricter requirements, local law takes precedence.

The Business Travel Policy 2022 protects employees, customers and the company by setting clear and fair expectations. It applies equally at every Kestrel site.

## Definitions

- **Site**: any office, plant, lab or warehouse operated by Kestrel Instruments
- **Manager**: an employee with formal responsibility for a team or cost center
- **Intranet**: the internal information portal of Kestrel Instruments
- **Employee**: a person with an employment contract with Kestrel Instruments
- **Working day**: a weekday that is not a public holiday at the employee's site
- **Contractor**: a person working for Kestrel through a service agreement

## Background

Feedback from employees and managers shaped the current Business Travel Policy 2022; it aims to be simple to apply and fair for everybody.

Audits and legal requirements also influenced the Business Travel Policy 2022, which therefore uses clear and verifiable rules.

## Requirements

This policy was superseded in 2025 and is kept for audit purposes only. Under this superseded policy the daily per diem was 45 EUR. Hotel costs were reimbursed up to 130 EUR per night.

## Procedure

1. Keep the confirmation for your records
2. Submit the request through the HR portal
3. Wait for the confirmation before acting on the request
4. Attach the required documents
5. Inform your team about any absence or change that affects them

## FAQ

**Q:** Where can I find the current version of the Business Travel Policy 2022?
**A:** The current version is always published on the intranet; printed copies may be outdated.

**Q:** Who answers questions about the Business Travel Policy 2022?
**A:** Your manager is the first contact; HR helps with questions the manager cannot answer.

**Q:** Are earlier versions of the Business Travel Policy 2022 still valid?
**A:** No. Earlier versions are archived for audit purposes only and must not be applied.

## Roles and responsibilities

Employees follow the Business Travel Policy 2022 and ask their manager or HR when something is unclear.

HR maintains the Business Travel Policy 2022, answers questions about it and reports on its use to the management team.

Managers apply the Business Travel Policy 2022 consistently and document exceptions they approve.

The document owner of the Business Travel Policy 2022 reviews it regularly and publishes changes on the intranet.
