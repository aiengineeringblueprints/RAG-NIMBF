# Kestrel KD1 Datasheet

Kestrel Instruments. Internal use only.

## Product overview

Product overview: the Kestrel KD1 (data logger) is ordered with SKU KS-1029 and has been on the market since it was launched in 2017.

Typical applications of the Kestrel KD1 include process monitoring, building automation and environmental control. The device integrates into existing control systems and does not need proprietary software for basic operation.

The Kestrel KD1 belongs to the Kestrel data logger range and shares accessories, mounting kits and configuration tools with the other models of its family. Spare parts remain available for the whole support period of the product.

## Storage and transport

Store the Kestrel KD1 in its original packaging in a dry place, protected from dust, direct sunlight and condensation.

Transport the Kestrel KD1 only in packaging that protects it against shocks; damage caused by unsuitable packaging is not covered by the warranty.

## Application examples

In research laboratories the Kestrel KD1 documents environmental conditions for experiments that must be reproducible and traceable.

Data center operators install the Kestrel KD1 in server rooms to detect unfavourable conditions before they affect equipment availability.

## Compliance

Materials of the Kestrel KD1 comply with the RoHS directive, and a declaration of conformity is available on request.

Test reports for the Kestrel KD1 are kept for the whole support period and can be provided for customer audits.

## Mounting

Mount the Kestrel KD1 on a vibration-free surface away from direct sunlight and heat sources. Leave enough clearance around the housing for cable entry and later maintenance work.

## Wiring

Use shielded twisted-pair cable for the communication line of the Kestrel KD1 and terminate the bus at both ends according to the rules of the fieldbus in use.

Route signal cables to the Kestrel KD1 separately from power cables and connect the cable shield on the controller side only, to avoid ground loops.

## Service and maintenance

Check the cable glands and seals of the Kestrel KD1 for wear during each inspection and replace them if they have become brittle.

Recalibrate the Kestrel KD1 according to the calibration procedure of Kestrel Instruments or send it to an accredited calibration lab.

## Questions and answers

**Q:** Where do I order spare parts for the Kestrel KD1?
**A:** Spare parts are ordered through the Kestrel sales office responsible for your country.

**Q:** Do I need special software for the Kestrel KD1?
**A:** No. The device works with standard control systems; the Kestrel Configurator is only needed for advanced settings.

**Q:** Can I replace the sensing element of the Kestrel KD1 myself?
**A:** Only trained service staff should replace it, because the device must be recalibrated afterwards.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Possible cause | Remedy |
|---|---|---|
| Configuration rejected | User code active | Enter the user code and retry |
| No reading | No supply voltage | Check fuse, wiring and supply polarity |
| Alarm without cause | Alarm limits too tight | Review limits and hysteresis |
| Reading drifts slowly | Sensing element contaminated | Clean or recalibrate the device |
