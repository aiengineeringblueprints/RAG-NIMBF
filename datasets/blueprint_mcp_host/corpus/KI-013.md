# Kestrel KF2 Datasheet

Kestrel Instruments. Internal use only.

## Introduction

The Kestrel KF2 is a flow meter sold under SKU KS-1013. It was launched in 2015.

Typical applications of the Kestrel KF2 include process monitoring, building automation and environmental control. The device integrates into existing control systems and does not need proprietary software for basic operation.

## Technical data

Minimum operating temperature: -10 °C. Maximum operating temperature: 80 °C. Supply current: 58 mA. Communication interface: CANopen. Accuracy: ±2 % of reading. Enclosure rating: IP40. Weight (current revision): 300 g.

The Kestrel KF2 operates between -10 °C and 80 °C and communicates via CANopen.

## Storage and transport

Transport the Kestrel KF2 only in packaging that protects it against shocks; damage caused by unsuitable packaging is not covered by the warranty.

## Mounting

Mount the Kestrel KF2 on a vibration-free surface away from direct sunlight and heat sources. Leave enough clearance around the housing for cable entry and later maintenance work.

## Wiring

Use shielded twisted-pair cable for the communication line of the Kestrel KF2 and terminate the bus at both ends according to the rules of the fieldbus in use.

## Service and maintenance

Inspect the Kestrel KF2 during scheduled plant shutdowns, clean the housing with a dry cloth, and record every intervention in the asset log.
