# Training Course: Root Cause Analysis

Kestrel Instruments, internal document.

## About the course

The course Root Cause Analysis lasts 1 days and is taught by Paul Tangen.

Root Cause Analysis combines short theory blocks with hands-on exercises on real devices.

Participants of Root Cause Analysis work in small groups, so trainers can respond to individual questions.

It accepts at most 11 participants and costs 1700 EUR per person.

Participants must first complete Project Management Basics.

## Target audience

Root Cause Analysis is aimed at technicians and engineers who install, configure or maintain Kestrel products.

## Materials

Participants of Root Cause Analysis receive the course slides and exercise descriptions in digital form.

## Agenda

### Day 1

- Configuration workshop
- Review and questions
- Group work on case studies
