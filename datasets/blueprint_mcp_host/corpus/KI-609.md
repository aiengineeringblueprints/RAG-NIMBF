# Supplier Profile: Orion Technik

Kestrel Instruments, internal document.

## Overview

Orion Technik supplies ultrasonic transducers to Kestrel Instruments. Orion Technik is based in Czech Republic.

Orion Technik is a long-standing supplier of Kestrel Instruments and delivers ultrasonic transducers for several product families.

Kestrel sources ultrasonic transducers from Orion Technik because of consistent quality and good technical support.

Orion Technik produces at its own facilities in Czech Republic and holds the usual quality certifications for its industry.

## Key data

- Component: ultrasonic transducers
- Country: Czech Republic
- Current standard lead time: 11 weeks
- Framework contract until: 2027

## Lead time history

During the component shortage the lead time temporarily rose to 16 weeks. The current standard lead time is 11 weeks. The current framework contract runs until 2027.

## Sustainability

Packaging from Orion Technik is reusable or recyclable wherever possible.

Orion Technik has signed the Kestrel supplier code of conduct.

Orion Technik reports on energy use and waste reduction as part of the annual supplier review.

## Quality

Incoming inspection at Kestrel checks samples of every delivery from Orion Technik. Deviations are handled through supplier corrective action requests.

Supplier performance of Orion Technik is reviewed every quarter by purchasing and quality.

Orion Technik shares test reports with every delivery of ultrasonic transducers, which simplifies incoming inspection.

## Audits

Kestrel quality engineers audit Orion Technik regularly; the last audit found no major deviations.

Orion Technik has implemented all corrective actions from previous audits on time.

Audit reports for Orion Technik are stored in the quality system and shared with purchasing.

## Contract terms

Payment terms with Orion Technik follow the Kestrel standard terms unless the contract states otherwise.

The framework contract with Orion Technik defines prices, delivery terms and quality requirements for ultrasonic transducers.

Price changes from Orion Technik must be announced in advance and are reviewed by the purchasing board.

## Risks

- Transport disruptions
- Exchange rate fluctuations
- Capacity limits during demand peaks

## Deliveries

Deliveries from Orion Technik go to the Aarhus plant and the Stavanger warehouse. Delivery schedules and open purchase orders are tracked in the ERP system.

Forecasts for ultrasonic transducers are shared with Orion Technik monthly to support their capacity planning.

Orion Technik offers consignment stock for ultrasonic transducers, which reduces the risk of shortages.
