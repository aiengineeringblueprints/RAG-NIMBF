# Supplier Profile: Nordic Electronics

Kestrel Instruments. Internal use only.

## Introduction

Kestrel buys humidity sensing elements from Nordic Electronics. Nordic Electronics is based in Taiwan.

Nordic Electronics is a long-standing supplier of Kestrel Instruments and delivers humidity sensing elements for several product families.

Nordic Electronics produces at its own facilities in Taiwan and holds the usual quality certifications for its industry.

Kestrel sources humidity sensing elements from Nordic Electronics because of consistent quality and good technical support.

## Lead time history

During the component shortage the lead time temporarily rose to 16 weeks. The current standard lead time is 7 weeks. The current framework contract runs until 2028.

## Logistics

Forecasts for humidity sensing elements are shared with Nordic Electronics monthly to support their capacity planning.

Nordic Electronics offers consignment stock for humidity sensing elements, which reduces the risk of shortages.

Deliveries from Nordic Electronics go to the Aarhus plant and the Stavanger warehouse. Delivery schedules and open purchase orders are tracked in the ERP system.

## Responsible sourcing

Nordic Electronics reports on energy use and waste reduction as part of the annual supplier review.

Packaging from Nordic Electronics is reusable or recyclable wherever possible.

Nordic Electronics has signed the Kestrel supplier code of conduct.

## Audit history

Audit reports for Nordic Electronics are stored in the quality system and shared with purchasing.

Nordic Electronics has implemented all corrective actions from previous audits on time.

Kestrel quality engineers audit Nordic Electronics regularly; the last audit found no major deviations.

## Supply risks

- Exchange rate fluctuations
- Single-source dependency for this component

## Quality management

Nordic Electronics shares test reports with every delivery of humidity sensing elements, which simplifies incoming inspection.

Supplier performance of Nordic Electronics is reviewed every quarter by purchasing and quality.

Incoming inspection at Kestrel checks samples of every delivery from Nordic Electronics. Deviations are handled through supplier corrective action requests.

## Contract terms

Price changes from Nordic Electronics must be announced in advance and are reviewed by the purchasing board.

The framework contract with Nordic Electronics defines prices, delivery terms and quality requirements for humidity sensing elements.

Payment terms with Nordic Electronics follow the Kestrel standard terms unless the contract states otherwise.
