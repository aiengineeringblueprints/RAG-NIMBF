# Hamburg Service Center

Kestrel Instruments, internal document.

## Overview

The Hamburg Service Center in Hamburg, Germany, is headed by Ronja Fossum, Service Center Manager.

The Hamburg Service Center has grown steadily since it opened and is an important part of the company's presence in Germany.

The Hamburg Service Center is part of the Kestrel Instruments network of sites and works closely with the headquarters and the other locations.

## Facts and figures

- The Hamburg service center repairs devices for customers in Central Europe.
- Standard repairs in Hamburg are completed within 7 working days.
- The Hamburg service center opened in 2021.

## Teams on site

- Customer support
- IT support
- Facilities
- Management and administration

## Site history

The Hamburg Service Center was renovated recently to provide modern workplaces and better meeting rooms.

The Hamburg Service Center started as a small team and has expanded several times since then. The current building was chosen for its good transport connections in Hamburg.

Over the years the Hamburg Service Center has taken on additional responsibilities and now supports colleagues and customers across the region.

## Sustainability

The Hamburg Service Center uses renewable electricity and separates waste for recycling.

## Amenities

- First aid room and defibrillator
- Charging points for electric vehicles
- Meeting rooms with video conferencing equipment
- Visitor reception and badge office
- Staff canteen and coffee corners
- Quiet rooms for focused work

## Directions

Detailed directions to the Hamburg Service Center are sent with every meeting invitation. Taxis can be ordered at the reception.

The Hamburg Service Center can be reached by public transport from the city center of Hamburg. Parking for visitors is available next to the main entrance.
