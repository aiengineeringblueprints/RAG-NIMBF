## History

Over the years Kronborg Foods AB has standardised several measuring points on Kestrel products, which simplifies spare parts and training on their side.

Kronborg Foods AB values short response times and local support, which is why the account is served by a dedicated account manager.

## Support contract

In 2020 Kronborg Foods AB upgraded its support contract from the Bronze tier to the Silver tier. Before the upgrade, the SLA response time was 48 hours; it is now 24 hours.
