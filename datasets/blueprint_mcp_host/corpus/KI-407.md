# Project Brief PRJ-207: Harbor Metering Expansion

Owner: Kestrel Instruments. Classification: internal.

## Introduction

Project PRJ-207, Harbor Metering Expansion, is delivered for Haugland Logistics ApS.

Project PRJ-207 replaces manual readings with continuous monitoring, so that Haugland Logistics ApS can react to deviations faster.

The goal of project PRJ-207 is to improve data quality and reduce maintenance effort for Haugland Logistics ApS.

Haugland Logistics ApS commissioned project PRJ-207 to modernise measurement and monitoring at one of its sites. The existing equipment had reached the end of its service life.

## Financials

The initial estimate was 886500 EUR. After the scope review the steering committee approved a budget of 870000 EUR.

## Scope of delivery

The scope of project PRJ-207 covers delivery, installation and commissioning of Kestrel KP5 devices and their integration into the customer's control system.

Civil works and cabling outside the measuring points are not part of project PRJ-207 and are provided by Haugland Logistics ApS.

Project PRJ-207 includes training for the customer's maintenance staff and a documentation package for audits.

Optional services in project PRJ-207, such as extended support, are offered separately.

## Goals

- Hand over a validated configuration backup
- Document the installation for later audits
- Replace legacy instruments with current Kestrel devices
- Reduce manual inspection rounds
- Provide remote diagnostics for the service team
