# Supplier Profile: Pioneer Supply

Owner: Kestrel Instruments. Classification: internal.

## Supplier overview

Kestrel buys microcontrollers from Pioneer Supply. Pioneer Supply is based in Finland.

Pioneer Supply produces at its own facilities in Finland and holds the usual quality certifications for its industry.

Pioneer Supply is a long-standing supplier of Kestrel Instruments and delivers microcontrollers for several product families.

Kestrel sources microcontrollers from Pioneer Supply because of consistent quality and good technical support.

## At a glance

Component: microcontrollers. Country: Finland. Current standard lead time: 20 weeks. Framework contract until: 2028.

## Delivery performance

During the component shortage the lead time temporarily rose to 23 weeks. The current standard lead time is 20 weeks. The current framework contract runs until 2028.

## Quality management

Incoming inspection at Kestrel checks samples of every delivery from Pioneer Supply. Deviations are handled through supplier corrective action requests.

Pioneer Supply shares test reports with every delivery of microcontrollers, which simplifies incoming inspection.

Supplier performance of Pioneer Supply is reviewed every quarter by purchasing and quality.

## Responsible sourcing

Pioneer Supply reports on energy use and waste reduction as part of the annual supplier review.

Packaging from Pioneer Supply is reusable or recyclable wherever possible.

Pioneer Supply has signed the Kestrel supplier code of conduct.

## Commercial terms

Price changes from Pioneer Supply must be announced in advance and are reviewed by the purchasing board.

Payment terms with Pioneer Supply follow the Kestrel standard terms unless the contract states otherwise.

The framework contract with Pioneer Supply defines prices, delivery terms and quality requirements for microcontrollers.

## Audits

Kestrel quality engineers audit Pioneer Supply regularly; the last audit found no major deviations.

Pioneer Supply has implemented all corrective actions from previous audits on time.

Audit reports for Pioneer Supply are stored in the quality system and shared with purchasing.

## Risks

- Raw material price changes
- Transport disruptions

## Deliveries

Pioneer Supply offers consignment stock for microcontrollers, which reduces the risk of shortages.

Forecasts for microcontrollers are shared with Pioneer Supply monthly to support their capacity planning.

Deliveries from Pioneer Supply go to the Aarhus plant and the Stavanger warehouse. Delivery schedules and open purchase orders are tracked in the ERP system.
