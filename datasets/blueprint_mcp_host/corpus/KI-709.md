# Training Course: Gas Detection Essentials

Owner: Kestrel Instruments. Classification: internal.

## About the course

Gas Detection Essentials: 1 days, trainer Erik Kjeldsen.

Participants of Gas Detection Essentials work in small groups, so trainers can respond to individual questions.

The course Gas Detection Essentials gives participants the practical knowledge they need in daily work with Kestrel products.

It accepts at most 11 participants and costs 550 EUR per person.

Participants must first complete Flow Measurement in Practice.

## Programme

### Day 1

- Configuration workshop
- Introduction and safety briefing
- Final assessment
