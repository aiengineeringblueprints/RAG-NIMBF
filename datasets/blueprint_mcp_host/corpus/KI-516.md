# Incident Report INC-2026-114

Kestrel Instruments. Internal use only.

## Incident summary

This report documents incident INC-2026-114 at Sandnes Bygg Oy. It follows the blameless postmortem format and focuses on causes and improvements, not on individuals.

Incident INC-2026-114 occurred on 14 April 2026 at Sandnes Bygg Oy. The affected product was the Kestrel KP9 and the incident was classified as severity 2.

## Impact

The customer had to fall back on manual readings while incident INC-2026-114 was open.

The final total downtime was 163 minutes.

## Detection

Monitoring at Sandnes Bygg Oy raised a communication alarm, which led to the investigation of incident INC-2026-114.

## Cause

Root cause analysis identified water ingress through a damaged cable gland.

## Other factors

- The fault was first mistaken for a network problem
- Spare devices were not available on site
- The affected installation was not on the latest firmware
- Monitoring did not alert on the first symptoms

## Communication

A written summary of incident INC-2026-114 was sent to Sandnes Bygg Oy together with this report.

After incident INC-2026-114 the account manager held a review meeting with Sandnes Bygg Oy to explain causes and actions.

## Measures

| Action | Owner | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Ship the fix with firmware 4.3.2 | Engineering | done |
| Roll out the fix to all affected installations | Engineering | open |
| Share lessons learned in the engineering forum | Engineering | open |

The fix shipped with firmware 4.3.2.

## What we learned

- Clear customer communication reduced pressure on the team
- Remote diagnostics access was essential
