## Storage and transport

Transport the Kestrel KT7 only in packaging that protects it against shocks; damage caused by unsuitable packaging is not covered by the warranty.

Store the Kestrel KT7 in its original packaging in a dry place, protected from dust, direct sunlight and condensation.

After long storage, check the seals of the Kestrel KT7 before installation and replace them if they have hardened.

## Mounting

The Kestrel KT7 can be mounted on a wall bracket, a pipe clamp or a standard mounting rail. Choose the mounting kit that matches the installation and tighten the screws evenly.

Where the Kestrel KT7 is installed in a process line, use the supplied adapter and check the sealing surfaces for damage before tightening the process connection.

After mechanical installation, check that the Kestrel KT7 is firmly seated and that the housing cover closes without force.

Orient the cable entries of the Kestrel KT7 downwards when installing outdoors, so that condensation cannot collect in the terminal compartment.

Mount the Kestrel KT7 on a vibration-free surface away from direct sunlight and heat sources. Leave enough clearance around the housing for cable entry and later maintenance work.

## Electrical connection

Connect the protective earth of the Kestrel KT7 before connecting the supply. Check the polarity of all terminals against the wiring diagram on the inside of the cover.

Use shielded twisted-pair cable for the communication line of the Kestrel KT7 and terminate the bus at both ends according to the rules of the fieldbus in use.

Route signal cables to the Kestrel KT7 separately from power cables and connect the cable shield on the controller side only, to avoid ground loops.

Install a suitable fuse in the supply line of the Kestrel KT7 and protect the signal lines against surges where long cable runs leave the building.

The terminals of the Kestrel KT7 accept solid and stranded conductors. Use ferrules for stranded wire and tighten the terminal screws with a suitable screwdriver.

## Commissioning

Set the communication address of the Kestrel KT7 before connecting it to a bus with other devices, otherwise address conflicts can interrupt communication.

Compare the first readings of the Kestrel KT7 with a reference instrument and document the result in the commissioning report.

Once commissioning is complete, close the housing of the Kestrel KT7, seal unused cable entries and record the configuration in the asset management system.

Switch on the supply and wait until the status LED of the Kestrel KT7 indicates normal operation. The device runs a self-test after every start.

## Device configuration

The Kestrel KT7 is configured with the Kestrel Configurator, either locally through the service interface or remotely over the fieldbus.

Alarm limits, filter settings and engineering units of the Kestrel KT7 can be changed during operation. Changes take effect immediately and are logged in the device event list.

Access to the configuration of the Kestrel KT7 can be protected with a user code. Without the code, settings are visible but cannot be changed.

Configuration profiles of the Kestrel KT7 can be exported and imported, which speeds up the commissioning of many identical measuring points.

A factory reset restores the delivery configuration of the Kestrel KT7. The calibration data is kept, but communication settings must be entered again.

## Normal operation

Readings of the Kestrel KT7 can be logged in the control system or in a Kestrel data logger for later analysis.

During normal operation the Kestrel KT7 needs no user intervention. Measured values and diagnostic information are available continuously through the communication interface.

Diagnostic messages of the Kestrel KT7 follow the NAMUR recommendation and distinguish between failure, function check, out of specification and maintenance required.

The status LED of the Kestrel KT7 shows normal operation, maintenance requests and faults with different colours and blink patterns.

## Service and maintenance

Inspect the Kestrel KT7 during scheduled plant shutdowns, clean the housing with a dry cloth, and record every intervention in the asset log.

Firmware updates for the Kestrel KT7 are distributed through the Kestrel service portal. Read the release notes before updating devices in the field.

Recalibrate the Kestrel KT7 according to the calibration procedure of Kestrel Instruments or send it to an accredited calibration lab.

Check the cable glands and seals of the Kestrel KT7 for wear during each inspection and replace them if they have become brittle.

Do not use solvents or high-pressure cleaners on the Kestrel KT7; they can damage the seals and the sensing element.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Possible cause | Remedy |
|---|---|---|
| Condensation in housing | Cable entries facing upwards | Remount with entries facing down |
| Status LED shows a fault | Internal self-test failed | Restart; contact support if it persists |
| Alarm without cause | Alarm limits too tight | Review limits and hysteresis |
| Values frozen | Device in function-check mode | Leave service mode in the configurator |
| No reading | No supply voltage | Check fuse, wiring and supply polarity |
| Intermittent communication | Missing bus termination | Terminate the bus at both ends |
| Reading drifts slowly | Sensing element contaminated | Clean or recalibrate the device |
| Unstable reading | Electrical interference | Check shielding and cable routing |
| No communication | Wrong bus address | Set a unique address and restart |

## Questions and answers

**Q:** Can I replace the sensing element of the Kestrel KT7 myself?
**A:** Only trained service staff should replace it, because the device must be recalibrated afterwards.

**Q:** Do I need special software for the Kestrel KT7?
**A:** No. The device works with standard control systems; the Kestrel Configurator is only needed for advanced settings.

**Q:** Can the Kestrel KT7 be mounted next to a frequency converter?
**A:** Keep as much distance as possible and use shielded cables to limit electromagnetic interference.

**Q:** How is the Kestrel KT7 protected against unauthorised changes?
**A:** A user code can lock the configuration; without it settings can be read but not changed.

**Q:** How do I find the firmware version of my Kestrel KT7?
**A:** The configurator shows it on the device information page; the current release is listed in the firmware release notes.

**Q:** Where do I order spare parts for the Kestrel KT7?
**A:** Spare parts are ordered through the Kestrel sales office responsible for your country.

**Q:** Can the Kestrel KT7 be used outdoors?
**A:** Yes, provided the enclosure rating stated in the specification table matches the installation conditions and the cable entries face downwards.
