# Incident Report INC-2026-309

Kestrel Instruments. Internal use only.

## Overview

Incident INC-2026-309 interrupted measurements at Halland Energi AS and was handled by customer support together with engineering.

This report documents incident INC-2026-309 at Halland Energi AS. It follows the blameless postmortem format and focuses on causes and improvements, not on individuals.

Incident INC-2026-309 occurred on 5 May 2026 at Halland Energi AS. The affected product was the Kestrel KH9 and the incident was classified as severity 2.

## Customer impact

The customer had to fall back on manual readings while incident INC-2026-309 was open.

Data gaps caused by incident INC-2026-309 were documented and shared with the customer.

Measurements at Halland Energi AS were unavailable or unreliable during the incident. No safety functions were affected.

The final total downtime was 307 minutes.

## Root cause

The root cause was an incorrect calibration coefficient.

## Communication

A written summary of incident INC-2026-309 was sent to Halland Energi AS together with this report.

Halland Energi AS received status updates from customer support throughout incident INC-2026-309.

After incident INC-2026-309 the account manager held a review meeting with Halland Energi AS to explain causes and actions.

## Other factors

- Monitoring did not alert on the first symptoms
- Spare devices were not available on site
- The fault was first mistaken for a network problem

## Lessons learned

- Remote diagnostics access was essential
- Clear customer communication reduced pressure on the team
- Checklists need regular review after incidents
