# Aarhus Production Plant

Kestrel Instruments. Internal use only.

## Overview

The Aarhus Production Plant in Aarhus, Denmark, is headed by Ingrid Dahl, Plant Manager.

The Aarhus Production Plant has grown steadily since it opened and is an important part of the company's presence in Denmark.

The Aarhus Production Plant is part of the Kestrel Instruments network of sites and works closely with the headquarters and the other locations.

Teams at the Aarhus Production Plant in Aarhus collaborate daily with colleagues at other sites through shared tools and regular video meetings.

## Key facts

- The Aarhus plant runs two shifts on working days.
- The Aarhus plant assembles all pressure and flow products.
- The Aarhus plant is certified to ISO 14001.

## Who works here

- Facilities
- Customer support
- Management and administration

## Sustainability

The Aarhus Production Plant uses renewable electricity and separates waste for recycling.

Employees at the Aarhus Production Plant are encouraged to commute by public transport or bicycle.

Energy consumption of the Aarhus Production Plant is monitored with Kestrel devices and reviewed every year.

## Emergency information

Contractors working at the Aarhus Production Plant receive a site-specific safety briefing before they start.

Emergency exits and assembly points of the Aarhus Production Plant are marked on the floor plans next to every staircase.

First aiders and fire wardens are appointed at the Aarhus Production Plant; their names are posted on the notice boards.

## Facilities

- Secure storage for test equipment
- Bicycle parking and showers
- Charging points for electric vehicles

## History

Over the years the Aarhus Production Plant has taken on additional responsibilities and now supports colleagues and customers across the region.

The Aarhus Production Plant was renovated recently to provide modern workplaces and better meeting rooms.

The Aarhus Production Plant started as a small team and has expanded several times since then. The current building was chosen for its good transport connections in Aarhus.

## Getting there

Detailed directions to the Aarhus Production Plant are sent with every meeting invitation. Taxis can be ordered at the reception.

The Aarhus Production Plant can be reached by public transport from the city center of Aarhus. Parking for visitors is available next to the main entrance.

## Visiting the site

Deliveries for the Aarhus Production Plant are accepted at the goods entrance and registered by the facilities team.

Visitors to the Aarhus Production Plant register at the reception and receive a badge that must be worn at all times. Safety instructions are handed out during registration.

Guests of the Aarhus Production Plant are collected at the reception by their host and accompanied in production and laboratory areas.
