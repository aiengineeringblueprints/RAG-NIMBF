# Supplier Profile: Rhine Components

Owner: Kestrel Instruments. Classification: internal.

## Supplier overview

Rhine Components supplies stainless steel housings to Kestrel Instruments. Rhine Components is based in Japan.

Rhine Components is a long-standing supplier of Kestrel Instruments and delivers stainless steel housings for several product families.

Rhine Components produces at its own facilities in Japan and holds the usual quality certifications for its industry.
