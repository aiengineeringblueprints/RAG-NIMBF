# Incident Report INC-2026-269

Owner: Kestrel Instruments. Classification: internal.

## Overview

Incident INC-2026-269 interrupted measurements at Vikholm Data ApS and was handled by customer support together with engineering.

This report documents incident INC-2026-269 at Vikholm Data ApS. It follows the blameless postmortem format and focuses on causes and improvements, not on individuals.

Incident INC-2026-269 occurred on 9 July 2026 at Vikholm Data ApS. The affected product was the Kestrel KH3 and the incident was classified as severity 2.

## Impact

Measurements at Vikholm Data ApS were unavailable or unreliable during the incident. No safety functions were affected.

Data gaps caused by incident INC-2026-269 were documented and shared with the customer.

The customer had to fall back on manual readings while incident INC-2026-269 was open.

The final total downtime was 669 minutes.

## Detection

Incident INC-2026-269 was detected when Vikholm Data ApS noticed missing values in the control system and opened a support case.

A technician of Vikholm Data ApS reported unusual readings during a routine inspection, which started incident INC-2026-269.

## Root cause analysis

The root cause was lightning damage to the field wiring.

## Customer communication

After incident INC-2026-269 the account manager held a review meeting with Vikholm Data ApS to explain causes and actions.

A written summary of incident INC-2026-269 was sent to Vikholm Data ApS together with this report.

Vikholm Data ApS received status updates from customer support throughout incident INC-2026-269.

## Other factors

- Documentation of the installation was incomplete
- The affected installation was not on the latest firmware
- The fault was first mistaken for a network problem
- Spare devices were not available on site
