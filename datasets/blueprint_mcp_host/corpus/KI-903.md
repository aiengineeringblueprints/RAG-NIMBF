# Expense Policy 2020 (superseded)

Kestrel Instruments. Internal use only.

**Status: superseded.** This version is archived and no longer valid.

## Purpose

The Expense Policy 2020 protects employees, customers and the company by setting clear and fair expectations. It applies equally at every Kestrel site.

The Expense Policy 2020 turns legal requirements and company values into practical rules. Where local law sets stricter requirements, local law takes precedence.

This document describes the rules of the Expense Policy 2020 at Kestrel Instruments and explains why they exist. It gives employees and managers a common reference for daily decisions.

## Definitions

- **Contractor**: a person working for Kestrel through a service agreement
- **Intranet**: the internal information portal of Kestrel Instruments
- **Manager**: an employee with formal responsibility for a team or cost center
- **Employee**: a person with an employment contract with Kestrel Instruments
- **Site**: any office, plant, lab or warehouse operated by Kestrel Instruments
- **Working day**: a weekday that is not a public holiday at the employee's site

## Context

The Expense Policy 2020 was introduced to make expectations consistent across all Kestrel sites after the company grew beyond its original headquarters.

Feedback from employees and managers shaped the current Expense Policy 2020; it aims to be simple to apply and fair for everybody.

The Expense Policy 2020 is reviewed together with employee representatives, so that changes are understood and accepted.

## Applicability

Temporary staff and interns follow the Expense Policy 2020 in the same way as permanent employees.

The Expense Policy 2020 applies to all employees and contractors of Kestrel Instruments unless a local works agreement states otherwise.

Managers are responsible for making sure that their teams know the Expense Policy 2020 and apply it.

## Roles and responsibilities

The document owner of the Expense Policy 2020 reviews it regularly and publishes changes on the intranet.

Employees follow the Expense Policy 2020 and ask their manager or HR when something is unclear.

Managers apply the Expense Policy 2020 consistently and document exceptions they approve.

HR maintains the Expense Policy 2020, answers questions about it and reports on its use to the management team.

## FAQ

**Q:** Are earlier versions of the Expense Policy 2020 still valid?
**A:** No. Earlier versions are archived for audit purposes only and must not be applied.

**Q:** What happens if the Expense Policy 2020 conflicts with local law?
**A:** Local law always takes precedence, and HR documents the deviation.

**Q:** Where can I find the current version of the Expense Policy 2020?
**A:** The current version is always published on the intranet; printed copies may be outdated.

**Q:** Who answers questions about the Expense Policy 2020?
**A:** Your manager is the first contact; HR helps with questions the manager cannot answer.

## Exceptions

Exceptions to the Expense Policy 2020 require written approval from the responsible manager and HR.

Repeated or deliberate violations of the Expense Policy 2020 can lead to disciplinary measures.

Approved exceptions to the Expense Policy 2020 are documented and reviewed when the policy is updated.

## Questions and contacts

For urgent questions about the Expense Policy 2020 outside office hours, contact the duty manager of your site.

Questions about the Expense Policy 2020 go to your manager first. HR business partners support managers and employees with the interpretation.
