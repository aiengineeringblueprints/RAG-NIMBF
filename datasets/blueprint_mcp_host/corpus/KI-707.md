# Training Course: Hazardous Area Installation

Kestrel Instruments. Internal use only.

## About the course

The course Hazardous Area Installation lasts 3 days and is taught by Rasmus Furu.

The course Hazardous Area Installation gives participants the practical knowledge they need in daily work with Kestrel products.

Participants of Hazardous Area Installation work in small groups, so trainers can respond to individual questions.

Hazardous Area Installation combines short theory blocks with hands-on exercises on real devices.

It accepts at most 16 participants and costs 1250 EUR per person.

The course has no prerequisites.

## Assessment

Participants who do not pass the assessment of Hazardous Area Installation can repeat it at a later date free of charge.

Hazardous Area Installation ends with a short practical assessment on a demo device.

## Target audience

Hazardous Area Installation is aimed at technicians and engineers who install, configure or maintain Kestrel products.

Customers, partners and Kestrel employees can register for Hazardous Area Installation.

Participants of Hazardous Area Installation should be familiar with basic measurement technology.

## What you will learn

- Apply the relevant safety rules
- Document work according to company standards
- Use the service portal efficiently
- Plan maintenance tasks
- Understand the principles behind the topic

## Materials

After Hazardous Area Installation participants keep access to the recorded theory blocks for later reference.

Demo devices and tools for Hazardous Area Installation are provided by Kestrel; participants do not need to bring equipment apart from a laptop.

Participants of Hazardous Area Installation receive the course slides and exercise descriptions in digital form.
