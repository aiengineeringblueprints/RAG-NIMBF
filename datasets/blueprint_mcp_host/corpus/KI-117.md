# Parental Leave Policy

Kestrel Instruments, internal document.

## Purpose

The Parental Leave Policy protects employees, customers and the company by setting clear and fair expectations. It applies equally at every Kestrel site.

## Terms used

- **Contractor**: a person working for Kestrel through a service agreement
- **Manager**: an employee with formal responsibility for a team or cost center
- **Site**: any office, plant, lab or warehouse operated by Kestrel Instruments
- **Intranet**: the internal information portal of Kestrel Instruments
- **Employee**: a person with an employment contract with Kestrel Instruments
- **Working day**: a weekday that is not a public holiday at the employee's site

## Who this applies to

Temporary staff and interns follow the Parental Leave Policy in the same way as permanent employees.

## Policy statements

Kestrel tops up statutory parental benefits to full salary for 20 weeks. Parental leave must be announced at least 12 weeks in advance. Employees returning from parental leave may work 80 % for up to one year.

## Questions and contacts

The intranet page of the Parental Leave Policy lists the responsible HR contact for each site.

## Exceptions

Exceptions to the Parental Leave Policy require written approval from the responsible manager and HR.
