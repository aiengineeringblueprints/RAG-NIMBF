# Supplier Profile: Orion Supply

Owner: Kestrel Instruments. Classification: internal.

## Supplier overview

Kestrel buys power supply units from Orion Supply. Orion Supply is based in Japan.

Kestrel sources power supply units from Orion Supply because of consistent quality and good technical support.

Orion Supply produces at its own facilities in Japan and holds the usual quality certifications for its industry.

Orion Supply is a long-standing supplier of Kestrel Instruments and delivers power supply units for several product families.

## At a glance

Component: power supply units. Country: Japan. Current standard lead time: 14 weeks. Framework contract until: 2027.

## Delivery performance

During the component shortage the lead time temporarily rose to 17 weeks. The current standard lead time is 14 weeks. The current framework contract runs until 2027.

## Sustainability

Orion Supply reports on energy use and waste reduction as part of the annual supplier review.

Packaging from Orion Supply is reusable or recyclable wherever possible.

Orion Supply has signed the Kestrel supplier code of conduct.

## Deliveries

Deliveries from Orion Supply go to the Aarhus plant and the Stavanger warehouse. Delivery schedules and open purchase orders are tracked in the ERP system.

Orion Supply offers consignment stock for power supply units, which reduces the risk of shortages.

Forecasts for power supply units are shared with Orion Supply monthly to support their capacity planning.

## Supply risks

- Capacity limits during demand peaks
- Exchange rate fluctuations

## Contract terms

The framework contract with Orion Supply defines prices, delivery terms and quality requirements for power supply units.

Payment terms with Orion Supply follow the Kestrel standard terms unless the contract states otherwise.

Price changes from Orion Supply must be announced in advance and are reviewed by the purchasing board.

## Quality

Incoming inspection at Kestrel checks samples of every delivery from Orion Supply. Deviations are handled through supplier corrective action requests.

Supplier performance of Orion Supply is reviewed every quarter by purchasing and quality.

Orion Supply shares test reports with every delivery of power supply units, which simplifies incoming inspection.

## Audit history

Audit reports for Orion Supply are stored in the quality system and shared with purchasing.

Kestrel quality engineers audit Orion Supply regularly; the last audit found no major deviations.

Orion Supply has implemented all corrective actions from previous audits on time.
