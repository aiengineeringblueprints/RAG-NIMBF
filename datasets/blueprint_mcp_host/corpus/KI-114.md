# Company Vehicle Policy

Kestrel Instruments, internal document.

## Why this policy exists

The Company Vehicle Policy protects employees, customers and the company by setting clear and fair expectations. It applies equally at every Kestrel site.

## Scope

Managers are responsible for making sure that their teams know the Company Vehicle Policy and apply it.

## Rules

1. Company cars are replaced after 5 years or 150000 km, whichever comes first.
2. Fuel cards may only be used for company vehicles.
3. Vehicle damage must be reported to the Fleet Coordinator within 48 hours.

## Responsibilities

- Employees follow the Company Vehicle Policy and ask their manager or HR when something is unclear.
- HR maintains the Company Vehicle Policy, answers questions about it and reports on its use to the management team.
- The document owner of the Company Vehicle Policy reviews it regularly and publishes changes on the intranet.
