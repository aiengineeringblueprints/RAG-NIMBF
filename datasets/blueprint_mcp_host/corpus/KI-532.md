# Incident Report INC-2026-290

Kestrel Instruments. Internal use only.

## Summary

Incident INC-2026-290 interrupted measurements at Rosen Marine GmbH and was handled by customer support together with engineering.

This report documents incident INC-2026-290 at Rosen Marine GmbH. It follows the blameless postmortem format and focuses on causes and improvements, not on individuals.

Incident INC-2026-290 occurred on 22 May 2026 at Rosen Marine GmbH. The affected product was the Kestrel KG2 and the incident was classified as severity 3.

## Detection

Monitoring at Rosen Marine GmbH raised a communication alarm, which led to the investigation of incident INC-2026-290.

A technician of Rosen Marine GmbH reported unusual readings during a routine inspection, which started incident INC-2026-290.

## Impact

Data gaps caused by incident INC-2026-290 were documented and shared with the customer.

Measurements at Rosen Marine GmbH were unavailable or unreliable during the incident. No safety functions were affected.

The customer had to fall back on manual readings while incident INC-2026-290 was open.

The final total downtime was 672 minutes.

## Cause

Root cause analysis identified an expired TLS certificate on the gateway.

## Communication

Rosen Marine GmbH received status updates from customer support throughout incident INC-2026-290.

After incident INC-2026-290 the account manager held a review meeting with Rosen Marine GmbH to explain causes and actions.

A written summary of incident INC-2026-290 was sent to Rosen Marine GmbH together with this report.

## Other factors

- Spare devices were not available on site
- Monitoring did not alert on the first symptoms

## Corrective actions

| Action | Owner | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Ship the fix with firmware 3.2.6 | Engineering | done |
| Add an alert for the observed symptom | Customer Support | open |
| Roll out the fix to all affected installations | Engineering | in progress |
| Update the installation checklist | Quality | open |
| Share lessons learned in the engineering forum | Engineering | done |

The fix shipped with firmware 3.2.6.

## Follow-up

The effectiveness of the corrective actions for incident INC-2026-290 will be checked in the next quality review.

Similar installations at other customers were checked for the conditions that led to incident INC-2026-290.

The knowledge base was updated with the diagnosis steps used during incident INC-2026-290.

## Lessons learned

- Remote diagnostics access was essential
- Early escalation shortened the investigation
- Checklists need regular review after incidents
