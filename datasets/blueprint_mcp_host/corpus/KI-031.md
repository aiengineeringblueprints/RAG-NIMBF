## Mounting

Orient the cable entries of the Kestrel KD4 downwards when installing outdoors, so that condensation cannot collect in the terminal compartment.

Where the Kestrel KD4 is installed in a process line, use the supplied adapter and check the sealing surfaces for damage before tightening the process connection.

## Wiring

The terminals of the Kestrel KD4 accept solid and stranded conductors. Use ferrules for stranded wire and tighten the terminal screws with a suitable screwdriver.

Route signal cables to the Kestrel KD4 separately from power cables and connect the cable shield on the controller side only, to avoid ground loops.

## Service and maintenance

Inspect the Kestrel KD4 during scheduled plant shutdowns, clean the housing with a dry cloth, and record every intervention in the asset log.

Do not use solvents or high-pressure cleaners on the Kestrel KD4; they can damage the seals and the sensing element.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Possible cause | Remedy |
|---|---|---|
| No reading | No supply voltage | Check fuse, wiring and supply polarity |
| Intermittent communication | Missing bus termination | Terminate the bus at both ends |
| Condensation in housing | Cable entries facing upwards | Remount with entries facing down |
| Alarm without cause | Alarm limits too tight | Review limits and hysteresis |
