# Kestrel KP8 Datasheet

Kestrel Instruments, internal document.

## Product overview

Product overview: the Kestrel KP8 (pressure transmitter) is ordered with SKU KS-1011 and has been on the market since it was launched in 2021.

The Kestrel KP8 is designed for continuous operation in demanding industrial environments. It combines a robust enclosure with a measurement core that Kestrel Instruments develops and calibrates in-house, so readings stay stable over long service intervals.

## Mechanical installation

After mechanical installation, check that the Kestrel KP8 is firmly seated and that the housing cover closes without force.

## Wiring

Route signal cables to the Kestrel KP8 separately from power cables and connect the cable shield on the controller side only, to avoid ground loops.

## Maintenance

Firmware updates for the Kestrel KP8 are distributed through the Kestrel service portal. Read the release notes before updating devices in the field.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Possible cause | Remedy |
|---|---|---|
| Values frozen | Device in function-check mode | Leave service mode in the configurator |
| Condensation in housing | Cable entries facing upwards | Remount with entries facing down |
| Unstable reading | Electrical interference | Check shielding and cable routing |
