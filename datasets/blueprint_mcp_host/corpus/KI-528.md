## Overview

This report documents incident INC-2026-235 at Sandnes Mining Inc. It follows the blameless postmortem format and focuses on causes and improvements, not on individuals.

Incident INC-2026-235 interrupted measurements at Sandnes Mining Inc. and was handled by customer support together with engineering.

## Customer impact

Data gaps caused by incident INC-2026-235 were documented and shared with the customer.

The customer had to fall back on manual readings while incident INC-2026-235 was open.

Measurements at Sandnes Mining Inc. were unavailable or unreliable during the incident. No safety functions were affected.

The final total downtime was 292 minutes.

## How the incident was detected

A technician of Sandnes Mining Inc. reported unusual readings during a routine inspection, which started incident INC-2026-235.

Monitoring at Sandnes Mining Inc. raised a communication alarm, which led to the investigation of incident INC-2026-235.

## Cause

Root cause analysis identified a network switch failure at the customer site.

## Customer communication

Sandnes Mining Inc. received status updates from customer support throughout incident INC-2026-235.

After incident INC-2026-235 the account manager held a review meeting with Sandnes Mining Inc. to explain causes and actions.

A written summary of incident INC-2026-235 was sent to Sandnes Mining Inc. together with this report.

## What we learned

- Checklists need regular review after incidents
- Early escalation shortened the investigation
- Clear customer communication reduced pressure on the team
- Remote diagnostics access was essential
