# Incident Report INC-2026-310

Kestrel Instruments, internal document.

## Summary

Incident INC-2026-310 interrupted measurements at Ahlberg Data AS and was handled by customer support together with engineering.

This report documents incident INC-2026-310 at Ahlberg Data AS. It follows the blameless postmortem format and focuses on causes and improvements, not on individuals.

Incident INC-2026-310 occurred on 28 October 2025 at Ahlberg Data AS. The affected product was the Kestrel KT8 and the incident was classified as severity 2.

## How the incident was detected

A technician of Ahlberg Data AS reported unusual readings during a routine inspection, which started incident INC-2026-310.

Incident INC-2026-310 was detected when Ahlberg Data AS noticed missing values in the control system and opened a support case.

## Impact

Measurements at Ahlberg Data AS were unavailable or unreliable during the incident. No safety functions were affected.

Data gaps caused by incident INC-2026-310 were documented and shared with the customer.

The customer had to fall back on manual readings while incident INC-2026-310 was open.

The final total downtime was 577 minutes.

## Root cause analysis

Root cause analysis identified lightning damage to the field wiring.

## Other factors

- The fault was first mistaken for a network problem
- The affected installation was not on the latest firmware

## Customer communication

A written summary of incident INC-2026-310 was sent to Ahlberg Data AS together with this report.

After incident INC-2026-310 the account manager held a review meeting with Ahlberg Data AS to explain causes and actions.

Ahlberg Data AS received status updates from customer support throughout incident INC-2026-310.

## Follow-up

The knowledge base was updated with the diagnosis steps used during incident INC-2026-310.

Similar installations at other customers were checked for the conditions that led to incident INC-2026-310.

The effectiveness of the corrective actions for incident INC-2026-310 will be checked in the next quality review.

## What we learned

- Checklists need regular review after incidents
- Early escalation shortened the investigation
- Remote diagnostics access was essential
