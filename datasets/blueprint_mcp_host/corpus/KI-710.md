# Training Course: Vibration Analysis

Kestrel Instruments. Internal use only.

## Description

The course Vibration Analysis lasts 4 days and is taught by Sindre Eriksen.

Participants of Vibration Analysis work in small groups, so trainers can respond to individual questions.

Vibration Analysis combines short theory blocks with hands-on exercises on real devices.

The course Vibration Analysis gives participants the practical knowledge they need in daily work with Kestrel products.

It accepts at most 18 participants and costs 1850 EUR per person.

The course has no prerequisites.

## Assessment

Vibration Analysis ends with a short practical assessment on a demo device.

Participants who do not pass the assessment of Vibration Analysis can repeat it at a later date free of charge.

## Target audience

Vibration Analysis is aimed at technicians and engineers who install, configure or maintain Kestrel products.

Customers, partners and Kestrel employees can register for Vibration Analysis.

Participants of Vibration Analysis should be familiar with basic measurement technology.

## Materials

After Vibration Analysis participants keep access to the recorded theory blocks for later reference.

Demo devices and tools for Vibration Analysis are provided by Kestrel; participants do not need to bring equipment apart from a laptop.

Participants of Vibration Analysis receive the course slides and exercise descriptions in digital form.

## Learning objectives

- Plan maintenance tasks
- Apply the relevant safety rules
- Configure devices with the Kestrel Configurator
- Diagnose typical faults
