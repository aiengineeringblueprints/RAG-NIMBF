# Project Brief PRJ-203: Fjord Leak Detection Retrofit

Kestrel Instruments. Internal use only.

## Context

Project PRJ-203, Fjord Leak Detection Retrofit, is delivered for Aasen Mining Oy.

Aasen Mining Oy commissioned project PRJ-203 to modernise measurement and monitoring at one of its sites. The existing equipment had reached the end of its service life.

Project PRJ-203 replaces manual readings with continuous monitoring, so that Aasen Mining Oy can react to deviations faster.

The goal of project PRJ-203 is to improve data quality and reduce maintenance effort for Aasen Mining Oy.

## Scope of delivery

Optional services in project PRJ-203, such as extended support, are offered separately.

The scope of project PRJ-203 covers delivery, installation and commissioning of Kestrel KW8 devices and their integration into the customer's control system.

Project PRJ-203 includes training for the customer's maintenance staff and a documentation package for audits.

Civil works and cabling outside the measuring points are not part of project PRJ-203 and are provided by Aasen Mining Oy.

## Governance

Status reports are shared with Aasen Mining Oy every two weeks. Change requests follow the standard change process and need written approval.

Live figures of project PRJ-203 such as budget spent and percent complete are tracked in the project system, not in this brief.

A steering committee with representatives of Aasen Mining Oy and Kestrel meets at every milestone of project PRJ-203.
