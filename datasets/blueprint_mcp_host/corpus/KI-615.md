# Supplier Profile: Vega Industries

Owner: Kestrel Instruments. Classification: internal.

## Supplier overview

Vega Industries supplies gaskets to Kestrel Instruments. Vega Industries is based in Poland.

Kestrel sources gaskets from Vega Industries because of consistent quality and good technical support.

Vega Industries is a long-standing supplier of Kestrel Instruments and delivers gaskets for several product families.

Vega Industries produces at its own facilities in Poland and holds the usual quality certifications for its industry.

## Delivery performance

During the component shortage the lead time temporarily rose to 17 weeks. The current standard lead time is 11 weeks. The current framework contract runs until 2026.

## Commercial terms

The framework contract with Vega Industries defines prices, delivery terms and quality requirements for gaskets.

Price changes from Vega Industries must be announced in advance and are reviewed by the purchasing board.

Payment terms with Vega Industries follow the Kestrel standard terms unless the contract states otherwise.

## Risks

- Single-source dependency for this component
- Capacity limits during demand peaks

## Sustainability

Vega Industries has signed the Kestrel supplier code of conduct.

Packaging from Vega Industries is reusable or recyclable wherever possible.

Vega Industries reports on energy use and waste reduction as part of the annual supplier review.

## Quality management

Incoming inspection at Kestrel checks samples of every delivery from Vega Industries. Deviations are handled through supplier corrective action requests.

Supplier performance of Vega Industries is reviewed every quarter by purchasing and quality.

Vega Industries shares test reports with every delivery of gaskets, which simplifies incoming inspection.

## Logistics

Forecasts for gaskets are shared with Vega Industries monthly to support their capacity planning.

Vega Industries offers consignment stock for gaskets, which reduces the risk of shortages.

Deliveries from Vega Industries go to the Aarhus plant and the Stavanger warehouse. Delivery schedules and open purchase orders are tracked in the ERP system.

## Audit history

Audit reports for Vega Industries are stored in the quality system and shared with purchasing.

Kestrel quality engineers audit Vega Industries regularly; the last audit found no major deviations.

Vega Industries has implemented all corrective actions from previous audits on time.
