# Houston Office

Kestrel Instruments, internal document.

## Introduction

The Houston Office in Houston, United States, is headed by Astrid Ostby, General Manager Americas.

Teams at the Houston Office in Houston collaborate daily with colleagues at other sites through shared tools and regular video meetings.

The Houston Office is part of the Kestrel Instruments network of sites and works closely with the headquarters and the other locations.

The Houston Office has grown steadily since it opened and is an important part of the company's presence in United States.

## At a glance

- The Houston office handles sales and support for the Americas.
- Support in Houston is available from 07:00 to 18:00 Central Time.
- The Houston office opened in 2022.

## Getting there

The Houston Office can be reached by public transport from the city center of Houston. Parking for visitors is available next to the main entrance.

Detailed directions to the Houston Office are sent with every meeting invitation. Taxis can be ordered at the reception.

## Facilities

- Visitor reception and badge office
- Meeting rooms with video conferencing equipment
- Secure storage for test equipment
- Charging points for electric vehicles
- Quiet rooms for focused work

## Safety and emergencies

Contractors working at the Houston Office receive a site-specific safety briefing before they start.

Emergency exits and assembly points of the Houston Office are marked on the floor plans next to every staircase.

First aiders and fire wardens are appointed at the Houston Office; their names are posted on the notice boards.

## Teams on site

- Sales and application engineering
- Logistics
- Facilities
- Management and administration

## Environment

Employees at the Houston Office are encouraged to commute by public transport or bicycle.

Energy consumption of the Houston Office is monitored with Kestrel devices and reviewed every year.

The Houston Office uses renewable electricity and separates waste for recycling.

## Visitors

Guests of the Houston Office are collected at the reception by their host and accompanied in production and laboratory areas.

Visitors to the Houston Office register at the reception and receive a badge that must be worn at all times. Safety instructions are handed out during registration.

Deliveries for the Houston Office are accepted at the goods entrance and registered by the facilities team.

## History

The Houston Office started as a small team and has expanded several times since then. The current building was chosen for its good transport connections in Houston.

Over the years the Houston Office has taken on additional responsibilities and now supports colleagues and customers across the region.

The Houston Office was renovated recently to provide modern workplaces and better meeting rooms.
