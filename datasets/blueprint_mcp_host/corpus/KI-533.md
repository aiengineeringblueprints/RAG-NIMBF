# Incident Report INC-2026-307

Owner: Kestrel Instruments. Classification: internal.

## Incident summary

Incident INC-2026-307 interrupted measurements at Fjellheim Data Oy and was handled by customer support together with engineering.

This report documents incident INC-2026-307 at Fjellheim Data Oy. It follows the blameless postmortem format and focuses on causes and improvements, not on individuals.

Incident INC-2026-307 occurred on 7 October 2025 at Fjellheim Data Oy. The affected product was the Kestrel KQ2 and the incident was classified as severity 2.

## Customer impact

Data gaps caused by incident INC-2026-307 were documented and shared with the customer.

The customer had to fall back on manual readings while incident INC-2026-307 was open.

Measurements at Fjellheim Data Oy were unavailable or unreliable during the incident. No safety functions were affected.

The final total downtime was 160 minutes.

## Detection

A technician of Fjellheim Data Oy reported unusual readings during a routine inspection, which started incident INC-2026-307.

Monitoring at Fjellheim Data Oy raised a communication alarm, which led to the investigation of incident INC-2026-307.

## Cause

The root cause was a clock synchronisation error.

## Other factors

- The affected installation was not on the latest firmware
- The fault was first mistaken for a network problem
- Spare devices were not available on site

## Customer communication

Fjellheim Data Oy received status updates from customer support throughout incident INC-2026-307.

A written summary of incident INC-2026-307 was sent to Fjellheim Data Oy together with this report.

After incident INC-2026-307 the account manager held a review meeting with Fjellheim Data Oy to explain causes and actions.

## Corrective actions

| Action | Owner | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Ship the fix with firmware 2.0.5 | Engineering | done |
| Review spare part stock for key customers | Logistics | open |
| Share lessons learned in the engineering forum | Engineering | done |

The fix shipped with firmware 2.0.5.

## What we learned

- Remote diagnostics access was essential
- Early escalation shortened the investigation
