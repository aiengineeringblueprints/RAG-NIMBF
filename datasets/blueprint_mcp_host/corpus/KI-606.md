# Supplier Profile: Alpine Parts

Kestrel Instruments. Internal use only.

## Overview

Alpine Parts supplies radio modules to Kestrel Instruments. Alpine Parts is based in Finland.

Kestrel sources radio modules from Alpine Parts because of consistent quality and good technical support.

Alpine Parts is a long-standing supplier of Kestrel Instruments and delivers radio modules for several product families.

Alpine Parts produces at its own facilities in Finland and holds the usual quality certifications for its industry.

## Supplier data

- Component: radio modules
- Country: Finland
- Current standard lead time: 9 weeks
- Framework contract until: 2029

## Delivery performance

During the component shortage the lead time temporarily rose to 15 weeks. The current standard lead time is 9 weeks. The current framework contract runs until 2029.

## Responsible sourcing

Alpine Parts reports on energy use and waste reduction as part of the annual supplier review.

Packaging from Alpine Parts is reusable or recyclable wherever possible.

Alpine Parts has signed the Kestrel supplier code of conduct.

## Deliveries

Alpine Parts offers consignment stock for radio modules, which reduces the risk of shortages.

Forecasts for radio modules are shared with Alpine Parts monthly to support their capacity planning.

Deliveries from Alpine Parts go to the Aarhus plant and the Stavanger warehouse. Delivery schedules and open purchase orders are tracked in the ERP system.

## Supply risks

- Exchange rate fluctuations
- Single-source dependency for this component
- Transport disruptions

## Audit history

Alpine Parts has implemented all corrective actions from previous audits on time.

Audit reports for Alpine Parts are stored in the quality system and shared with purchasing.

Kestrel quality engineers audit Alpine Parts regularly; the last audit found no major deviations.

## Quality management

Alpine Parts shares test reports with every delivery of radio modules, which simplifies incoming inspection.

Supplier performance of Alpine Parts is reviewed every quarter by purchasing and quality.

Incoming inspection at Kestrel checks samples of every delivery from Alpine Parts. Deviations are handled through supplier corrective action requests.

## Contract terms

Price changes from Alpine Parts must be announced in advance and are reviewed by the purchasing board.

The framework contract with Alpine Parts defines prices, delivery terms and quality requirements for radio modules.

Payment terms with Alpine Parts follow the Kestrel standard terms unless the contract states otherwise.
