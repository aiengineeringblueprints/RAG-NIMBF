# Supplier Profile: Alpine Industries

Owner: Kestrel Instruments. Classification: internal.

## Overview

Alpine Industries supplies thermistors to Kestrel Instruments. Alpine Industries is based in Czech Republic.

Kestrel sources thermistors from Alpine Industries because of consistent quality and good technical support.

Alpine Industries is a long-standing supplier of Kestrel Instruments and delivers thermistors for several product families.

## At a glance

- Component: thermistors
- Country: Czech Republic
- Current standard lead time: 11 weeks
- Framework contract until: 2027

## Lead time history

During the component shortage the lead time temporarily rose to 15 weeks. The current standard lead time is 11 weeks. The current framework contract runs until 2027.

## Quality management

Supplier performance of Alpine Industries is reviewed every quarter by purchasing and quality.

## Audit history

Alpine Industries has implemented all corrective actions from previous audits on time.
