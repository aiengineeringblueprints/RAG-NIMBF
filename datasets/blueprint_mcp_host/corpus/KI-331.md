## History

Over the years Halland Marine GmbH has standardised several measuring points on Kestrel products, which simplifies spare parts and training on their side.

Halland Marine GmbH uses Kestrel equipment mainly for monitoring and quality assurance in its shipping operations.

Halland Marine GmbH took part in a beta programme for new firmware and gave valuable feedback to product management.

The installed base at Halland Marine GmbH is documented in the CRM, including installation sites and firmware versions.

## Contract history

In 2023 Halland Marine GmbH upgraded its support contract from the Gold tier to the Platinum tier. Before the upgrade, the SLA response time was 8 hours; it is now 4 hours.

## Communication preferences
