# Kestrel KV5 Datasheet

Kestrel Instruments, internal document.

## Introduction

The Kestrel KV5 is a vibration sensor sold under SKU KS-1023. It was launched in 2022.

Typical applications of the Kestrel KV5 include process monitoring, building automation and environmental control. The device integrates into existing control systems and does not need proprietary software for basic operation.

## Transport and storage

Transport the Kestrel KV5 only in packaging that protects it against shocks; damage caused by unsuitable packaging is not covered by the warranty.

Store the Kestrel KV5 in its original packaging in a dry place, protected from dust, direct sunlight and condensation.

After long storage, check the seals of the Kestrel KV5 before installation and replace them if they have hardened.

## Mechanical installation

The Kestrel KV5 can be mounted on a wall bracket, a pipe clamp or a standard mounting rail. Choose the mounting kit that matches the installation and tighten the screws evenly.

Orient the cable entries of the Kestrel KV5 downwards when installing outdoors, so that condensation cannot collect in the terminal compartment.

## Wiring

Connect the protective earth of the Kestrel KV5 before connecting the supply. Check the polarity of all terminals against the wiring diagram on the inside of the cover.

## Maintenance

Do not use solvents or high-pressure cleaners on the Kestrel KV5; they can damage the seals and the sensing element.

Recalibrate the Kestrel KV5 according to the calibration procedure of Kestrel Instruments or send it to an accredited calibration lab.

## FAQ

**Q:** Is the Kestrel KV5 suitable for hazardous areas?
**A:** Only variants with an explicit hazardous area certification may be used there; check the nameplate before installation.

**Q:** Can the Kestrel KV5 be used outdoors?
**A:** Yes, provided the enclosure rating stated in the specification table matches the installation conditions and the cable entries face downwards.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Possible cause | Remedy |
|---|---|---|
| Reading drifts slowly | Sensing element contaminated | Clean or recalibrate the device |
| Status LED shows a fault | Internal self-test failed | Restart; contact support if it persists |
| No reading | No supply voltage | Check fuse, wiring and supply polarity |
