# Incident Report INC-2026-094

Kestrel Instruments. Internal use only.

## Overview

Incident INC-2026-094 interrupted measurements at Falkberg Logistics Oy and was handled by customer support together with engineering.

This report documents incident INC-2026-094 at Falkberg Logistics Oy. It follows the blameless postmortem format and focuses on causes and improvements, not on individuals.

Incident INC-2026-094 occurred on 6 October 2025 at Falkberg Logistics Oy. The affected product was the Kestrel KF2 and the incident was classified as severity 1.

## Detection

Incident INC-2026-094 was detected when Falkberg Logistics Oy noticed missing values in the control system and opened a support case.

A technician of Falkberg Logistics Oy reported unusual readings during a routine inspection, which started incident INC-2026-094.

## Impact

The customer had to fall back on manual readings while incident INC-2026-094 was open.

Data gaps caused by incident INC-2026-094 were documented and shared with the customer.

The final total downtime was 571 minutes.

## Cause

The root cause was a faulty batch of sensing elements from a supplier.

## Customer communication

A written summary of incident INC-2026-094 was sent to Falkberg Logistics Oy together with this report.

Falkberg Logistics Oy received status updates from customer support throughout incident INC-2026-094.

## Contributing factors

- Documentation of the installation was incomplete
- Spare devices were not available on site
- Monitoring did not alert on the first symptoms

## Actions

| Action | Owner | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Ship the fix with firmware 1.4.9 | Engineering | done |
| Roll out the fix to all affected installations | Engineering | in progress |
| Update the installation checklist | Quality | open |
| Add an alert for the observed symptom | Customer Support | in progress |
| Review spare part stock for key customers | Logistics | open |

The fix shipped with firmware 1.4.9.
