# Remote Work Policy 2021 (superseded)

Owner: Kestrel Instruments. Classification: internal.

**Status: superseded.** This version is archived and no longer valid.

## Purpose

The Remote Work Policy 2021 protects employees, customers and the company by setting clear and fair expectations. It applies equally at every Kestrel site.

This document describes the rules of the Remote Work Policy 2021 at Kestrel Instruments and explains why they exist. It gives employees and managers a common reference for daily decisions.

## Who this applies to

The Remote Work Policy 2021 applies to all employees and contractors of Kestrel Instruments unless a local works agreement states otherwise.

Managers are responsible for making sure that their teams know the Remote Work Policy 2021 and apply it.

## Terms used

- **Intranet**: the internal information portal of Kestrel Instruments
- **Working day**: a weekday that is not a public holiday at the employee's site
- **Manager**: an employee with formal responsibility for a team or cost center
- **Contractor**: a person working for Kestrel through a service agreement
- **Employee**: a person with an employment contract with Kestrel Instruments

## Policy statements

This policy was replaced by the current remote work policy. It allowed remote work on up to 2 days per week. The equipment allowance was 200 EUR.

## FAQ

**Q:** What happens if the Remote Work Policy 2021 conflicts with local law?
**A:** Local law always takes precedence, and HR documents the deviation.

**Q:** Where can I find the current version of the Remote Work Policy 2021?
**A:** The current version is always published on the intranet; printed copies may be outdated.

**Q:** Are earlier versions of the Remote Work Policy 2021 still valid?
**A:** No. Earlier versions are archived for audit purposes only and must not be applied.

**Q:** Who answers questions about the Remote Work Policy 2021?
**A:** Your manager is the first contact; HR helps with questions the manager cannot answer.

## How to apply this policy

1. Discuss open questions with your manager
2. Keep the confirmation for your records
3. Wait for the confirmation before acting on the request

## Questions and contacts

For urgent questions about the Remote Work Policy 2021 outside office hours, contact the duty manager of your site.

## Roles and responsibilities

The document owner of the Remote Work Policy 2021 reviews it regularly and publishes changes on the intranet.

HR maintains the Remote Work Policy 2021, answers questions about it and reports on its use to the management team.

Managers apply the Remote Work Policy 2021 consistently and document exceptions they approve.

Employees follow the Remote Work Policy 2021 and ask their manager or HR when something is unclear.
