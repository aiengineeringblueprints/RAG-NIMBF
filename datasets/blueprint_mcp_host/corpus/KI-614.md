# Supplier Profile: Helix Technik

Owner: Kestrel Instruments. Classification: internal.

## Overview

Helix Technik supplies antennas to Kestrel Instruments. Helix Technik is based in Sweden.

Helix Technik produces at its own facilities in Sweden and holds the usual quality certifications for its industry.

Kestrel sources antennas from Helix Technik because of consistent quality and good technical support.
