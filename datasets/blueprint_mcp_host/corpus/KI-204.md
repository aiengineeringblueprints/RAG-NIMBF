# Trondheim R&D Center

Kestrel Instruments. Internal use only.

## About the site

The Trondheim R&D Center in Trondheim, Norway, is headed by Julie Hartmann, Director of Research.

The Trondheim R&D Center has grown steadily since it opened and is an important part of the company's presence in Norway.

Teams at the Trondheim R&D Center in Trondheim collaborate daily with colleagues at other sites through shared tools and regular video meetings.

The Trondheim R&D Center is part of the Kestrel Instruments network of sites and works closely with the headquarters and the other locations.

## Key facts

The Trondheim center employs 35 engineers. Trondheim focuses on wireless gateways and firmware development. The Trondheim center opened in 2016.

## Sustainability

Energy consumption of the Trondheim R&D Center is monitored with Kestrel devices and reviewed every year.

The Trondheim R&D Center uses renewable electricity and separates waste for recycling.

Employees at the Trondheim R&D Center are encouraged to commute by public transport or bicycle.

## Who works here

- Engineering and testing
- Management and administration
- IT support

## Getting there

The Trondheim R&D Center can be reached by public transport from the city center of Trondheim. Parking for visitors is available next to the main entrance.

Detailed directions to the Trondheim R&D Center are sent with every meeting invitation. Taxis can be ordered at the reception.

## Visiting the site

Guests of the Trondheim R&D Center are collected at the reception by their host and accompanied in production and laboratory areas.

Deliveries for the Trondheim R&D Center are accepted at the goods entrance and registered by the facilities team.
