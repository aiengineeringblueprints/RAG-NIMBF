# Incident Report INC-2026-074

Kestrel Instruments. Internal use only.

## Summary

Incident INC-2026-074 interrupted measurements at Ramstad Logistics Oy and was handled by customer support together with engineering.

Incident INC-2026-074 occurred on 30 September 2026 at Ramstad Logistics Oy. The affected product was the Kestrel KQ5 and the incident was classified as severity 3.

## Customer impact

Measurements at Ramstad Logistics Oy were unavailable or unreliable during the incident. No safety functions were affected.

The final total downtime was 402 minutes.

## Detection

Incident INC-2026-074 was detected when Ramstad Logistics Oy noticed missing values in the control system and opened a support case.

## Root cause

Root cause analysis identified an incorrect calibration coefficient.

## Other factors

- Spare devices were not available on site
- Documentation of the installation was incomplete

## Customer communication

After incident INC-2026-074 the account manager held a review meeting with Ramstad Logistics Oy to explain causes and actions.

## Actions

| Action | Owner | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Ship the fix with firmware 4.1.9 | Engineering | done |
| Share lessons learned in the engineering forum | Engineering | in progress |
| Add an alert for the observed symptom | Customer Support | done |
| Roll out the fix to all affected installations | Engineering | done |
| Review spare part stock for key customers | Logistics | open |

The fix shipped with firmware 4.1.9.

## What we learned

- Checklists need regular review after incidents
- Clear customer communication reduced pressure on the team

## Follow-up

The knowledge base was updated with the diagnosis steps used during incident INC-2026-074.

The effectiveness of the corrective actions for incident INC-2026-074 will be checked in the next quality review.
