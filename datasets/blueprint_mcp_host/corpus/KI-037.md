The Kestrel KQ1 belongs to the Kestrel power quality meter range and shares accessories, mounting kits and configuration tools with the other models of its family. Spare parts remain available for the whole support period of the product.

The Kestrel KQ1 is designed for continuous operation in demanding industrial environments. It combines a robust enclosure with a measurement core that Kestrel Instruments develops and calibrates in-house, so readings stay stable over long service intervals.

## Mechanical installation

Mount the Kestrel KQ1 on a vibration-free surface away from direct sunlight and heat sources. Leave enough clearance around the housing for cable entry and later maintenance work.

Where the Kestrel KQ1 is installed in a process line, use the supplied adapter and check the sealing surfaces for damage before tightening the process connection.

After mechanical installation, check that the Kestrel KQ1 is firmly seated and that the housing cover closes without force.

Orient the cable entries of the Kestrel KQ1 downwards when installing outdoors, so that condensation cannot collect in the terminal compartment.

The Kestrel KQ1 can be mounted on a wall bracket, a pipe clamp or a standard mounting rail. Choose the mounting kit that matches the installation and tighten the screws evenly.

## Wiring

The terminals of the Kestrel KQ1 accept solid and stranded conductors. Use ferrules for stranded wire and tighten the terminal screws with a suitable screwdriver.

Connect the protective earth of the Kestrel KQ1 before connecting the supply. Check the polarity of all terminals against the wiring diagram on the inside of the cover.

Route signal cables to the Kestrel KQ1 separately from power cables and connect the cable shield on the controller side only, to avoid ground loops.

Install a suitable fuse in the supply line of the Kestrel KQ1 and protect the signal lines against surges where long cable runs leave the building.

Use shielded twisted-pair cable for the communication line of the Kestrel KQ1 and terminate the bus at both ends according to the rules of the fieldbus in use.

## Commissioning

Once commissioning is complete, close the housing of the Kestrel KQ1, seal unused cable entries and record the configuration in the asset management system.

Switch on the supply and wait until the status LED of the Kestrel KQ1 indicates normal operation. The device runs a self-test after every start.

Set the communication address of the Kestrel KQ1 before connecting it to a bus with other devices, otherwise address conflicts can interrupt communication.

Compare the first readings of the Kestrel KQ1 with a reference instrument and document the result in the commissioning report.

## Device configuration

Access to the configuration of the Kestrel KQ1 can be protected with a user code. Without the code, settings are visible but cannot be changed.

Configuration profiles of the Kestrel KQ1 can be exported and imported, which speeds up the commissioning of many identical measuring points.

The Kestrel KQ1 is configured with the Kestrel Configurator, either locally through the service interface or remotely over the fieldbus.

Alarm limits, filter settings and engineering units of the Kestrel KQ1 can be changed during operation. Changes take effect immediately and are logged in the device event list.

A factory reset restores the delivery configuration of the Kestrel KQ1. The calibration data is kept, but communication settings must be entered again.

## Normal operation

The status LED of the Kestrel KQ1 shows normal operation, maintenance requests and faults with different colours and blink patterns.

During normal operation the Kestrel KQ1 needs no user intervention. Measured values and diagnostic information are available continuously through the communication interface.

Readings of the Kestrel KQ1 can be logged in the control system or in a Kestrel data logger for later analysis.

Diagnostic messages of the Kestrel KQ1 follow the NAMUR recommendation and distinguish between failure, function check, out of specification and maintenance required.

## Maintenance

Inspect the Kestrel KQ1 during scheduled plant shutdowns, clean the housing with a dry cloth, and record every intervention in the asset log.

Do not use solvents or high-pressure cleaners on the Kestrel KQ1; they can damage the seals and the sensing element.

Firmware updates for the Kestrel KQ1 are distributed through the Kestrel service portal. Read the release notes before updating devices in the field.

Recalibrate the Kestrel KQ1 according to the calibration procedure of Kestrel Instruments or send it to an accredited calibration lab.

Check the cable glands and seals of the Kestrel KQ1 for wear during each inspection and replace them if they have become brittle.

## Diagnostics and troubleshooting

| Symptom | Possible cause | Remedy |
|---|---|---|
| Configuration rejected | User code active | Enter the user code and retry |
| Status LED shows a fault | Internal self-test failed | Restart; contact support if it persists |
| Intermittent communication | Missing bus termination | Terminate the bus at both ends |
| Reading drifts slowly | Sensing element contaminated | Clean or recalibrate the device |
| Condensation in housing | Cable entries facing upwards | Remount with entries facing down |
| No reading | No supply voltage | Check fuse, wiring and supply polarity |

## Questions and answers

**Q:** Can the Kestrel KQ1 be mounted next to a frequency converter?
**A:** Keep as much distance as possible and use shielded cables to limit electromagnetic interference.

**Q:** Can I replace the sensing element of the Kestrel KQ1 myself?
**A:** Only trained service staff should replace it, because the device must be recalibrated afterwards.

**Q:** Is the Kestrel KQ1 suitable for hazardous areas?
**A:** Only variants with an explicit hazardous area certification may be used there; check the nameplate before installation.

**Q:** Do I need special software for the Kestrel KQ1?
**A:** No. The device works with standard control systems; the Kestrel Configurator is only needed for advanced settings.

**Q:** How is the Kestrel KQ1 protected against unauthorised changes?
**A:** A user code can lock the configuration; without it settings can be read but not changed.

**Q:** Can the Kestrel KQ1 be used outdoors?
**A:** Yes, provided the enclosure rating stated in the specification table matches the installation conditions and the cable entries face downwards.
