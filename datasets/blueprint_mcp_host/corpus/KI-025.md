Kestrel Instruments. Internal use only.

Product overview: the Kestrel KL2 (level sensor) is ordered with SKU KS-1025 and has been on the market since it was launched in 2024.

Every Kestrel KL2 leaves the factory with an individual test report. The report documents the final functional test and the configuration loaded before shipment, which simplifies acceptance testing on site.

## Typical use cases

In research laboratories the Kestrel KL2 documents environmental conditions for experiments that must be reproducible and traceable.

## Storage and transport

Store the Kestrel KL2 in its original packaging in a dry place, protected from dust, direct sunlight and condensation.

## Mechanical installation

Orient the cable entries of the Kestrel KL2 downwards when installing outdoors, so that condensation cannot collect in the terminal compartment.

## Wiring

Install a suitable fuse in the supply line of the Kestrel KL2 and protect the signal lines against surges where long cable runs leave the building.

## Service and maintenance

Check the cable glands and seals of the Kestrel KL2 for wear during each inspection and replace them if they have become brittle.
