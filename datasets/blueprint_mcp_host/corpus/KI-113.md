# Data Protection Policy

Kestrel Instruments. Internal use only.

## Why this policy exists

This document describes the rules of the Data Protection Policy at Kestrel Instruments and explains why they exist. It gives employees and managers a common reference for daily decisions.

The Data Protection Policy protects employees, customers and the company by setting clear and fair expectations. It applies equally at every Kestrel site.

The Data Protection Policy turns legal requirements and company values into practical rules. Where local law sets stricter requirements, local law takes precedence.

## Who this applies to

The Data Protection Policy applies to all employees and contractors of Kestrel Instruments unless a local works agreement states otherwise.

Temporary staff and interns follow the Data Protection Policy in the same way as permanent employees.

Managers are responsible for making sure that their teams know the Data Protection Policy and apply it.

## Definitions

- **Contractor**: a person working for Kestrel through a service agreement
- **Working day**: a weekday that is not a public holiday at the employee's site
- **Site**: any office, plant, lab or warehouse operated by Kestrel Instruments
- **Manager**: an employee with formal responsibility for a team or cost center
- **Employee**: a person with an employment contract with Kestrel Instruments
- **Intranet**: the internal information portal of Kestrel Instruments

## Background

The Data Protection Policy is reviewed together with employee representatives, so that changes are understood and accepted.

The Data Protection Policy was introduced to make expectations consistent across all Kestrel sites after the company grew beyond its original headquarters.

Feedback from employees and managers shaped the current Data Protection Policy; it aims to be simple to apply and fair for everybody.

## Policy

Personal customer data is deleted 24 months after the last contract ends. Data protection incidents are reported to the Data Protection Officer within 24 hours. Customer data is stored only in data centers within the European Economic Area.

## Responsibilities

- The document owner of the Data Protection Policy reviews it regularly and publishes changes on the intranet.
- Employees follow the Data Protection Policy and ask their manager or HR when something is unclear.

## Procedure

1. Keep the confirmation for your records
2. Wait for the confirmation before acting on the request
3. Discuss open questions with your manager

## Deviations

Repeated or deliberate violations of the Data Protection Policy can lead to disciplinary measures.

Approved exceptions to the Data Protection Policy are documented and reviewed when the policy is updated.

Exceptions to the Data Protection Policy require written approval from the responsible manager and HR.

## Contacts

For urgent questions about the Data Protection Policy outside office hours, contact the duty manager of your site.

The intranet page of the Data Protection Policy lists the responsible HR contact for each site.

## FAQ

**Q:** Where can I find the current version of the Data Protection Policy?
**A:** The current version is always published on the intranet; printed copies may be outdated.

**Q:** Are earlier versions of the Data Protection Policy still valid?
**A:** No. Earlier versions are archived for audit purposes only and must not be applied.

**Q:** What happens if the Data Protection Policy conflicts with local law?
**A:** Local law always takes precedence, and HR documents the deviation.

**Q:** Who answers questions about the Data Protection Policy?
**A:** Your manager is the first contact; HR helps with questions the manager cannot answer.
