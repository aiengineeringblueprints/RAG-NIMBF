# Procurement Policy

Owner: Kestrel Instruments. Classification: internal.

## Purpose of this policy

This document describes the rules of the Procurement Policy at Kestrel Instruments and explains why they exist. It gives employees and managers a common reference for daily decisions.

The Procurement Policy protects employees, customers and the company by setting clear and fair expectations. It applies equally at every Kestrel site.

The Procurement Policy turns legal requirements and company values into practical rules. Where local law sets stricter requirements, local law takes precedence.

## Terms used

- **Manager**: an employee with formal responsibility for a team or cost center
- **Contractor**: a person working for Kestrel through a service agreement
- **Employee**: a person with an employment contract with Kestrel Instruments
- **Working day**: a weekday that is not a public holiday at the employee's site

## Applicability

The Procurement Policy applies to all employees and contractors of Kestrel Instruments unless a local works agreement states otherwise.

Temporary staff and interns follow the Procurement Policy in the same way as permanent employees.

Managers are responsible for making sure that their teams know the Procurement Policy and apply it.

## Context

The Procurement Policy was introduced to make expectations consistent across all Kestrel sites after the company grew beyond its original headquarters.

The Procurement Policy is reviewed together with employee representatives, so that changes are understood and accepted.

Feedback from employees and managers shaped the current Procurement Policy; it aims to be simple to apply and fair for everybody.

## Policy

1. Purchases above 5000 EUR require three competing quotes.
2. New suppliers are approved by the purchasing board, which meets monthly.
3. Framework agreements with suppliers run for at most 3 years.

## FAQ

**Q:** What happens if the Procurement Policy conflicts with local law?
**A:** Local law always takes precedence, and HR documents the deviation.

**Q:** Are earlier versions of the Procurement Policy still valid?
**A:** No. Earlier versions are archived for audit purposes only and must not be applied.

## Contacts

Questions about the Procurement Policy go to your manager first. HR business partners support managers and employees with the interpretation.

The intranet page of the Procurement Policy lists the responsible HR contact for each site.

## Procedure

1. Read the relevant section of the Procurement Policy before starting the process
2. Wait for the confirmation before acting on the request
3. Attach the required documents
4. Keep the confirmation for your records
5. Submit the request through the HR portal

## Deviations

Repeated or deliberate violations of the Procurement Policy can lead to disciplinary measures.

Exceptions to the Procurement Policy require written approval from the responsible manager and HR.

Approved exceptions to the Procurement Policy are documented and reviewed when the policy is updated.

## Roles and responsibilities

- Managers apply the Procurement Policy consistently and document exceptions they approve.
- HR maintains the Procurement Policy, answers questions about it and reports on its use to the management team.
- Employees follow the Procurement Policy and ask their manager or HR when something is unclear.
