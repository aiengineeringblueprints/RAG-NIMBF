# Kestrel KV9 Datasheet

Kestrel Instruments. Internal use only.

## Product overview

The Kestrel KV9 is a vibration sensor sold under SKU KS-1024. It was launched in 2021.

Typical applications of the Kestrel KV9 include process monitoring, building automation and environmental control. The device integrates into existing control systems and does not need proprietary software for basic operation.

The Kestrel KV9 is designed for continuous operation in demanding industrial environments. It combines a robust enclosure with a measurement core that Kestrel Instruments develops and calibrates in-house, so readings stay stable over long service intervals.

## Specifications

Minimum operating temperature: -30 °C. Maximum operating temperature: 100 °C. Supply current: 17 mA. Communication interface: CANopen. Accuracy: ±2 % of range. Enclosure rating: IP65. Weight (current revision): 525 g.

The Kestrel KV9 operates between -30 °C and 100 °C and communicates via CANopen.

## Applications

In water treatment plants the Kestrel KV9 monitors process conditions continuously and feeds its readings into the plant control system, where operators set alarms for deviations.

Building operators use the Kestrel KV9 to keep indoor conditions within comfort and energy targets, often as part of a larger building management system.

## Storage and transport

Transport the Kestrel KV9 only in packaging that protects it against shocks; damage caused by unsuitable packaging is not covered by the warranty.

Store the Kestrel KV9 in its original packaging in a dry place, protected from dust, direct sunlight and condensation.

After long storage, check the seals of the Kestrel KV9 before installation and replace them if they have hardened.

## Mechanical installation

The Kestrel KV9 can be mounted on a wall bracket, a pipe clamp or a standard mounting rail. Choose the mounting kit that matches the installation and tighten the screws evenly.

Mount the Kestrel KV9 on a vibration-free surface away from direct sunlight and heat sources. Leave enough clearance around the housing for cable entry and later maintenance work.

## Electrical connection

Install a suitable fuse in the supply line of the Kestrel KV9 and protect the signal lines against surges where long cable runs leave the building.

Connect the protective earth of the Kestrel KV9 before connecting the supply. Check the polarity of all terminals against the wiring diagram on the inside of the cover.

## Care and maintenance

Do not use solvents or high-pressure cleaners on the Kestrel KV9; they can damage the seals and the sensing element.

Check the cable glands and seals of the Kestrel KV9 for wear during each inspection and replace them if they have become brittle.

## Diagnostics and troubleshooting

| Symptom | Possible cause | Remedy |
|---|---|---|
| Values frozen | Device in function-check mode | Leave service mode in the configurator |
| Alarm without cause | Alarm limits too tight | Review limits and hysteresis |
| Reading drifts slowly | Sensing element contaminated | Clean or recalibrate the device |
