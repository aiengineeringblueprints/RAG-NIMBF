# Incident Report INC-2026-107

Kestrel Instruments, internal document.

## Incident summary

This report documents incident INC-2026-107 at Vassdal Bygg ApS. It follows the blameless postmortem format and focuses on causes and improvements, not on individuals.

Incident INC-2026-107 interrupted measurements at Vassdal Bygg ApS and was handled by customer support together with engineering.

Incident INC-2026-107 occurred on 4 June 2026 at Vassdal Bygg ApS. The affected product was the Kestrel KF2 and the incident was classified as severity 2.

## How the incident was detected

Monitoring at Vassdal Bygg ApS raised a communication alarm, which led to the investigation of incident INC-2026-107.

Incident INC-2026-107 was detected when Vassdal Bygg ApS noticed missing values in the control system and opened a support case.

## Impact

The customer had to fall back on manual readings while incident INC-2026-107 was open.

Data gaps caused by incident INC-2026-107 were documented and shared with the customer.

Measurements at Vassdal Bygg ApS were unavailable or unreliable during the incident. No safety functions were affected.

The final total downtime was 670 minutes.

## Root cause analysis

Root cause analysis identified lightning damage to the field wiring.

## Other factors

- Spare devices were not available on site
- Monitoring did not alert on the first symptoms
- The fault was first mistaken for a network problem

## Lessons learned

- Early escalation shortened the investigation
- Remote diagnostics access was essential
- Clear customer communication reduced pressure on the team
- Checklists need regular review after incidents
