# Kestrel KT9 Installation and Operating Manual

Owner: Kestrel Instruments. Classification: internal.

## Product overview

The Kestrel KT9 is a temperature probe sold under SKU KS-1008. It was launched in 2017.

Every Kestrel KT9 leaves the factory with an individual test report. The report documents the final functional test and the configuration loaded before shipment, which simplifies acceptance testing on site.

The Kestrel KT9 is designed for continuous operation in demanding industrial environments. It combines a robust enclosure with a measurement core that Kestrel Instruments develops and calibrates in-house, so readings stay stable over long service intervals.

The Kestrel KT9 belongs to the Kestrel temperature probe range and shares accessories, mounting kits and configuration tools with the other models of its family. Spare parts remain available for the whole support period of the product.

Typical applications of the Kestrel KT9 include process monitoring, building automation and environmental control. The device integrates into existing control systems and does not need proprietary software for basic operation.

## Safety

Disconnect the supply voltage before opening the terminal compartment of the Kestrel KT9. Work on live circuits is permitted only where national regulations allow it and the necessary protective measures are in place.

If the housing of the Kestrel KT9 is visibly damaged, take the device out of service and contact customer support before reconnecting it.

Only qualified personnel may install, commission or service the Kestrel KT9. Read this document completely before working on the device and keep it available at the place of installation.

Modifications to the Kestrel KT9 that are not described in this document are not permitted. Use only original spare parts and accessories supplied by Kestrel Instruments.

Do not operate the Kestrel KT9 outside the conditions stated in the specification table. Operation outside these limits can damage the device and void the warranty.

## Transport and storage

After long storage, check the seals of the Kestrel KT9 before installation and replace them if they have hardened.

Store the Kestrel KT9 in its original packaging in a dry place, protected from dust, direct sunlight and condensation.

Transport the Kestrel KT9 only in packaging that protects it against shocks; damage caused by unsuitable packaging is not covered by the warranty.

## Mounting

Where the Kestrel KT9 is installed in a process line, use the supplied adapter and check the sealing surfaces for damage before tightening the process connection.

Orient the cable entries of the Kestrel KT9 downwards when installing outdoors, so that condensation cannot collect in the terminal compartment.

After mechanical installation, check that the Kestrel KT9 is firmly seated and that the housing cover closes without force.

The Kestrel KT9 can be mounted on a wall bracket, a pipe clamp or a standard mounting rail. Choose the mounting kit that matches the installation and tighten the screws evenly.

Mount the Kestrel KT9 on a vibration-free surface away from direct sunlight and heat sources. Leave enough clearance around the housing for cable entry and later maintenance work.

## Wiring

Connect the protective earth of the Kestrel KT9 before connecting the supply. Check the polarity of all terminals against the wiring diagram on the inside of the cover.

The terminals of the Kestrel KT9 accept solid and stranded conductors. Use ferrules for stranded wire and tighten the terminal screws with a suitable screwdriver.

Use shielded twisted-pair cable for the communication line of the Kestrel KT9 and terminate the bus at both ends according to the rules of the fieldbus in use.

Route signal cables to the Kestrel KT9 separately from power cables and connect the cable shield on the controller side only, to avoid ground loops.

Install a suitable fuse in the supply line of the Kestrel KT9 and protect the signal lines against surges where long cable runs leave the building.

## Commissioning

Switch on the supply and wait until the status LED of the Kestrel KT9 indicates normal operation. The device runs a self-test after every start.

Once commissioning is complete, close the housing of the Kestrel KT9, seal unused cable entries and record the configuration in the asset management system.

Set the communication address of the Kestrel KT9 before connecting it to a bus with other devices, otherwise address conflicts can interrupt communication.

Compare the first readings of the Kestrel KT9 with a reference instrument and document the result in the commissioning report.

## Device configuration

The Kestrel KT9 is configured with the Kestrel Configurator, either locally through the service interface or remotely over the fieldbus.

A factory reset restores the delivery configuration of the Kestrel KT9. The calibration data is kept, but communication settings must be entered again.

Configuration profiles of the Kestrel KT9 can be exported and imported, which speeds up the commissioning of many identical measuring points.

Alarm limits, filter settings and engineering units of the Kestrel KT9 can be changed during operation. Changes take effect immediately and are logged in the device event list.

Access to the configuration of the Kestrel KT9 can be protected with a user code. Without the code, settings are visible but cannot be changed.

## Normal operation

Readings of the Kestrel KT9 can be logged in the control system or in a Kestrel data logger for later analysis.

Diagnostic messages of the Kestrel KT9 follow the NAMUR recommendation and distinguish between failure, function check, out of specification and maintenance required.

The status LED of the Kestrel KT9 shows normal operation, maintenance requests and faults with different colours and blink patterns.

During normal operation the Kestrel KT9 needs no user intervention. Measured values and diagnostic information are available continuously through the communication interface.

## Maintenance

Check the cable glands and seals of the Kestrel KT9 for wear during each inspection and replace them if they have become brittle.

Inspect the Kestrel KT9 during scheduled plant shutdowns, clean the housing with a dry cloth, and record every intervention in the asset log.

Firmware updates for the Kestrel KT9 are distributed through the Kestrel service portal. Read the release notes before updating devices in the field.

Recalibrate the Kestrel KT9 according to the calibration procedure of Kestrel Instruments or send it to an accredited calibration lab.

Do not use solvents or high-pressure cleaners on the Kestrel KT9; they can damage the seals and the sensing element.

## Diagnostics and troubleshooting

| Symptom | Possible cause | Remedy |
|---|---|---|
| Unstable reading | Electrical interference | Check shielding and cable routing |
| Values frozen | Device in function-check mode | Leave service mode in the configurator |
| Reading drifts slowly | Sensing element contaminated | Clean or recalibrate the device |
| No reading | No supply voltage | Check fuse, wiring and supply polarity |
| No communication | Wrong bus address | Set a unique address and restart |
| Status LED shows a fault | Internal self-test failed | Restart; contact support if it persists |

## FAQ

**Q:** Is the Kestrel KT9 suitable for hazardous areas?
**A:** Only variants with an explicit hazardous area certification may be used there; check the nameplate before installation.

**Q:** How do I find the firmware version of my Kestrel KT9?
**A:** The configurator shows it on the device information page; the current release is listed in the firmware release notes.

**Q:** Can the Kestrel KT9 be used outdoors?
**A:** Yes, provided the enclosure rating stated in the specification table matches the installation conditions and the cable entries face downwards.

**Q:** Can I replace the sensing element of the Kestrel KT9 myself?
**A:** Only trained service staff should replace it, because the device must be recalibrated afterwards.

**Q:** Can the Kestrel KT9 be mounted next to a frequency converter?
**A:** Keep as much distance as possible and use shielded cables to limit electromagnetic interference.

**Q:** Do I need special software for the Kestrel KT9?
**A:** No. The device works with standard control systems; the Kestrel Configurator is only needed for advanced settings.
