# Workplace Safety Policy

Kestrel Instruments. Internal use only.

## Purpose of this policy

The Workplace Safety Policy turns legal requirements and company values into practical rules. Where local law sets stricter requirements, local law takes precedence.

## Definitions

- **Contractor**: a person working for Kestrel through a service agreement
- **Working day**: a weekday that is not a public holiday at the employee's site
- **Manager**: an employee with formal responsibility for a team or cost center

## Background

The Workplace Safety Policy was introduced to make expectations consistent across all Kestrel sites after the company grew beyond its original headquarters.

## Policy statements

1. Safety shoes are mandatory in the Aarhus plant and the Stavanger warehouse.
2. Near misses are reported to the Health and Safety Officer within 24 hours.
3. Fire drills take place twice a year at every site.

## Exceptions

Approved exceptions to the Workplace Safety Policy are documented and reviewed when the policy is updated.

## Roles and responsibilities

The document owner of the Workplace Safety Policy reviews it regularly and publishes changes on the intranet.

Employees follow the Workplace Safety Policy and ask their manager or HR when something is unclear.

Managers apply the Workplace Safety Policy consistently and document exceptions they approve.

## FAQ

**Q:** What happens if the Workplace Safety Policy conflicts with local law?
**A:** Local law always takes precedence, and HR documents the deviation.

**Q:** Who answers questions about the Workplace Safety Policy?
**A:** Your manager is the first contact; HR helps with questions the manager cannot answer.

**Q:** Are earlier versions of the Workplace Safety Policy still valid?
**A:** No. Earlier versions are archived for audit purposes only and must not be applied.
