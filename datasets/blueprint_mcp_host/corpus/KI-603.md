# Supplier Profile: Alpine Supply

Kestrel Instruments, internal document.

## Overview

Alpine Supply supplies printed circuit boards to Kestrel Instruments. Alpine Supply is based in Italy.

Kestrel sources printed circuit boards from Alpine Supply because of consistent quality and good technical support.

Alpine Supply produces at its own facilities in Italy and holds the usual quality certifications for its industry.

Alpine Supply is a long-standing supplier of Kestrel Instruments and delivers printed circuit boards for several product families.

## Lead time history

During the component shortage the lead time temporarily rose to 15 weeks. The current standard lead time is 7 weeks. The current framework contract runs until 2026.

## Sustainability

Alpine Supply has signed the Kestrel supplier code of conduct.

Alpine Supply reports on energy use and waste reduction as part of the annual supplier review.

Packaging from Alpine Supply is reusable or recyclable wherever possible.

## Audits

Kestrel quality engineers audit Alpine Supply regularly; the last audit found no major deviations.

Audit reports for Alpine Supply are stored in the quality system and shared with purchasing.

Alpine Supply has implemented all corrective actions from previous audits on time.

## Commercial terms

Price changes from Alpine Supply must be announced in advance and are reviewed by the purchasing board.

The framework contract with Alpine Supply defines prices, delivery terms and quality requirements for printed circuit boards.

Payment terms with Alpine Supply follow the Kestrel standard terms unless the contract states otherwise.

## Logistics

Deliveries from Alpine Supply go to the Aarhus plant and the Stavanger warehouse. Delivery schedules and open purchase orders are tracked in the ERP system.

Forecasts for printed circuit boards are shared with Alpine Supply monthly to support their capacity planning.

Alpine Supply offers consignment stock for printed circuit boards, which reduces the risk of shortages.

## Supply risks

- Transport disruptions
- Exchange rate fluctuations
- Raw material price changes

## Quality

Incoming inspection at Kestrel checks samples of every delivery from Alpine Supply. Deviations are handled through supplier corrective action requests.

Supplier performance of Alpine Supply is reviewed every quarter by purchasing and quality.

Alpine Supply shares test reports with every delivery of printed circuit boards, which simplifies incoming inspection.
