# Supplier Profile: Danube Technik

Kestrel Instruments, internal document.

## Overview

Kestrel buys flash memory chips from Danube Technik. Danube Technik is based in Germany.

Danube Technik produces at its own facilities in Germany and holds the usual quality certifications for its industry.

Danube Technik is a long-standing supplier of Kestrel Instruments and delivers flash memory chips for several product families.

## At a glance

Component: flash memory chips. Country: Germany. Current standard lead time: 5 weeks. Framework contract until: 2027.

## Delivery performance

During the component shortage the lead time temporarily rose to 12 weeks. The current standard lead time is 5 weeks. The current framework contract runs until 2027.

## Commercial terms

Price changes from Danube Technik must be announced in advance and are reviewed by the purchasing board.

## Supply risks

- Exchange rate fluctuations
- Single-source dependency for this component
- Capacity limits during demand peaks
- Transport disruptions
