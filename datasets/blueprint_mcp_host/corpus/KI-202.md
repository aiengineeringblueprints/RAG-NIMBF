# Bergen Calibration Lab

Owner: Kestrel Instruments. Classification: internal.

## Introduction

The Bergen Calibration Lab in Bergen, Norway, is headed by Vegard Holm, Head of Calibration Lab.

The Bergen Calibration Lab is part of the Kestrel Instruments network of sites and works closely with the headquarters and the other locations.

Teams at the Bergen Calibration Lab in Bergen collaborate daily with colleagues at other sites through shared tools and regular video meetings.

The Bergen Calibration Lab has grown steadily since it opened and is an important part of the company's presence in Norway.

## Facts and figures

- The Bergen lab employs 12 calibration technicians.
- The Bergen lab is accredited to ISO/IEC 17025.
- The Bergen lab also hosts the customer support team.

## Sustainability

Energy consumption of the Bergen Calibration Lab is monitored with Kestrel devices and reviewed every year.

The Bergen Calibration Lab uses renewable electricity and separates waste for recycling.

Employees at the Bergen Calibration Lab are encouraged to commute by public transport or bicycle.

## Emergency information

Contractors working at the Bergen Calibration Lab receive a site-specific safety briefing before they start.

First aiders and fire wardens are appointed at the Bergen Calibration Lab; their names are posted on the notice boards.

Emergency exits and assembly points of the Bergen Calibration Lab are marked on the floor plans next to every staircase.

## History

The Bergen Calibration Lab started as a small team and has expanded several times since then. The current building was chosen for its good transport connections in Bergen.

Over the years the Bergen Calibration Lab has taken on additional responsibilities and now supports colleagues and customers across the region.

The Bergen Calibration Lab was renovated recently to provide modern workplaces and better meeting rooms.

## Visitors

Visitors to the Bergen Calibration Lab register at the reception and receive a badge that must be worn at all times. Safety instructions are handed out during registration.

Deliveries for the Bergen Calibration Lab are accepted at the goods entrance and registered by the facilities team.

Guests of the Bergen Calibration Lab are collected at the reception by their host and accompanied in production and laboratory areas.

## Facilities

- Bicycle parking and showers
- Charging points for electric vehicles
- First aid room and defibrillator
- Secure storage for test equipment

## Directions

The Bergen Calibration Lab can be reached by public transport from the city center of Bergen. Parking for visitors is available next to the main entrance.

Detailed directions to the Bergen Calibration Lab are sent with every meeting invitation. Taxis can be ordered at the reception.
