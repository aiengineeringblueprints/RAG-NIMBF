# Expense Policy

Kestrel Instruments, internal document.

## Purpose of this policy

The Expense Policy protects employees, customers and the company by setting clear and fair expectations. It applies equally at every Kestrel site.

This document describes the rules of the Expense Policy at Kestrel Instruments and explains why they exist. It gives employees and managers a common reference for daily decisions.

The Expense Policy turns legal requirements and company values into practical rules. Where local law sets stricter requirements, local law takes precedence.

## Context

Audits and legal requirements also influenced the Expense Policy, which therefore uses clear and verifiable rules.

Feedback from employees and managers shaped the current Expense Policy; it aims to be simple to apply and fair for everybody.

The Expense Policy is reviewed together with employee representatives, so that changes are understood and accepted.

## Scope

Temporary staff and interns follow the Expense Policy in the same way as permanent employees.

The Expense Policy applies to all employees and contractors of Kestrel Instruments unless a local works agreement states otherwise.

Managers are responsible for making sure that their teams know the Expense Policy and apply it.

## Definitions

- **Intranet**: the internal information portal of Kestrel Instruments
- **Manager**: an employee with formal responsibility for a team or cost center
- **Contractor**: a person working for Kestrel through a service agreement
- **Working day**: a weekday that is not a public holiday at the employee's site
- **Site**: any office, plant, lab or warehouse operated by Kestrel Instruments
- **Employee**: a person with an employment contract with Kestrel Instruments

## Policy

1. Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days after the expense was incurred.
2. Receipts are required for every expense above 25 EUR.
3. Expense reports are approved by the cost center owner.

## Questions and contacts

For urgent questions about the Expense Policy outside office hours, contact the duty manager of your site.

The intranet page of the Expense Policy lists the responsible HR contact for each site.

## How to apply this policy

1. Attach the required documents
2. Wait for the confirmation before acting on the request
3. Inform your team about any absence or change that affects them
4. Discuss open questions with your manager
5. Submit the request through the HR portal
6. Keep the confirmation for your records

## Exceptions

Repeated or deliberate violations of the Expense Policy can lead to disciplinary measures.

Exceptions to the Expense Policy require written approval from the responsible manager and HR.

Approved exceptions to the Expense Policy are documented and reviewed when the policy is updated.

## Frequently asked questions

**Q:** Where can I find the current version of the Expense Policy?
**A:** The current version is always published on the intranet; printed copies may be outdated.

**Q:** Who answers questions about the Expense Policy?
**A:** Your manager is the first contact; HR helps with questions the manager cannot answer.

**Q:** Are earlier versions of the Expense Policy still valid?
**A:** No. Earlier versions are archived for audit purposes only and must not be applied.

**Q:** What happens if the Expense Policy conflicts with local law?
**A:** Local law always takes precedence, and HR documents the deviation.

## Roles and responsibilities

HR maintains the Expense Policy, answers questions about it and reports on its use to the management team.

The document owner of the Expense Policy reviews it regularly and publishes changes on the intranet.

Employees follow the Expense Policy and ask their manager or HR when something is unclear.

Managers apply the Expense Policy consistently and document exceptions they approve.
