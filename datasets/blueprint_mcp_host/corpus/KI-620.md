# Supplier Profile: Pioneer Electronics

Kestrel Instruments. Internal use only.

## Supplier overview

Pioneer Electronics supplies calibration gases to Kestrel Instruments. Pioneer Electronics is based in Poland.

Pioneer Electronics is a long-standing supplier of Kestrel Instruments and delivers calibration gases for several product families.

Pioneer Electronics produces at its own facilities in Poland and holds the usual quality certifications for its industry.

## Lead time history

During the component shortage the lead time temporarily rose to 16 weeks. The current standard lead time is 9 weeks. The current framework contract runs until 2027.

## Deliveries

Deliveries from Pioneer Electronics go to the Aarhus plant and the Stavanger warehouse. Delivery schedules and open purchase orders are tracked in the ERP system.

## Sustainability

Packaging from Pioneer Electronics is reusable or recyclable wherever possible.

## Audits

Pioneer Electronics has implemented all corrective actions from previous audits on time.
