# Incident Report INC-2026-167

Owner: Kestrel Instruments. Classification: internal.

## Incident summary

Incident INC-2026-167 interrupted measurements at Sandnes Mining Inc. and was handled by customer support together with engineering.

Incident INC-2026-167 occurred on 19 October 2025 at Sandnes Mining Inc. The affected product was the Kestrel KG7 and the incident was classified as severity 1.

## Detection

A technician of Sandnes Mining Inc. reported unusual readings during a routine inspection, which started incident INC-2026-167.

## Customer impact

Data gaps caused by incident INC-2026-167 were documented and shared with the customer.

Measurements at Sandnes Mining Inc. were unavailable or unreliable during the incident. No safety functions were affected.

The customer had to fall back on manual readings while incident INC-2026-167 was open.

The final total downtime was 160 minutes.

## Root cause analysis

The root cause was a corroded connector.

## Contributing factors

- Documentation of the installation was incomplete
- Monitoring did not alert on the first symptoms
- Spare devices were not available on site
- The fault was first mistaken for a network problem

## Actions

| Action | Owner | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Ship the fix with firmware 5.5.6 | Engineering | done |
| Add an alert for the observed symptom | Customer Support | done |
| Roll out the fix to all affected installations | Engineering | done |
| Update the installation checklist | Quality | done |
| Share lessons learned in the engineering forum | Engineering | in progress |

The fix shipped with firmware 5.5.6.

## What we learned

- Early escalation shortened the investigation
- Clear customer communication reduced pressure on the team
- Remote diagnostics access was essential
- Checklists need regular review after incidents

## Follow-up

The knowledge base was updated with the diagnosis steps used during incident INC-2026-167.

Similar installations at other customers were checked for the conditions that led to incident INC-2026-167.
