# Software Release Process

Owner: Kestrel Instruments. Classification: internal.

## Why this policy exists

This document describes the rules of the Software Release Process at Kestrel Instruments and explains why they exist. It gives employees and managers a common reference for daily decisions.

## Terms used

- **Employee**: a person with an employment contract with Kestrel Instruments
- **Manager**: an employee with formal responsibility for a team or cost center
- **Working day**: a weekday that is not a public holiday at the employee's site
- **Site**: any office, plant, lab or warehouse operated by Kestrel Instruments
- **Contractor**: a person working for Kestrel through a service agreement
- **Intranet**: the internal information portal of Kestrel Instruments

## Scope

The Software Release Process applies to all employees and contractors of Kestrel Instruments unless a local works agreement states otherwise.

## Policy statements

1. Every release requires two approvals from code owners.
2. The weekly code freeze starts on Thursday at 12:00.
3. Hotfixes during the freeze need approval from the on-call engineer.

## Questions and contacts

Questions about the Software Release Process go to your manager first. HR business partners support managers and employees with the interpretation.

## Exceptions

Repeated or deliberate violations of the Software Release Process can lead to disciplinary measures.

## Roles and responsibilities

- The document owner of the Software Release Process reviews it regularly and publishes changes on the intranet.
- HR maintains the Software Release Process, answers questions about it and reports on its use to the management team.
- Employees follow the Software Release Process and ask their manager or HR when something is unclear.
