# Training Course: Data Logger Programming

Kestrel Instruments, internal document.

## Course overview

The course Data Logger Programming lasts 4 days and is taught by Eirik Rognes.

The course Data Logger Programming gives participants the practical knowledge they need in daily work with Kestrel products.

Participants of Data Logger Programming work in small groups, so trainers can respond to individual questions.

It accepts at most 10 participants and costs 550 EUR per person.

The course has no prerequisites.
