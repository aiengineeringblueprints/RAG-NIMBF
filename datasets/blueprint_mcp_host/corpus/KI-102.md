# Business Travel Policy (current, effective 2025)

Owner: Kestrel Instruments. Classification: internal.

## Purpose of this policy

This document describes the rules of the Business Travel Policy at Kestrel Instruments and explains why they exist. It gives employees and managers a common reference for daily decisions.

The Business Travel Policy turns legal requirements and company values into practical rules. Where local law sets stricter requirements, local law takes precedence.

The Business Travel Policy protects employees, customers and the company by setting clear and fair expectations. It applies equally at every Kestrel site.

## Definitions

- **Manager**: an employee with formal responsibility for a team or cost center
- **Working day**: a weekday that is not a public holiday at the employee's site
- **Employee**: a person with an employment contract with Kestrel Instruments
- **Contractor**: a person working for Kestrel through a service agreement
- **Intranet**: the internal information portal of Kestrel Instruments

## Scope

Managers are responsible for making sure that their teams know the Business Travel Policy and apply it.

The Business Travel Policy applies to all employees and contractors of Kestrel Instruments unless a local works agreement states otherwise.

Temporary staff and interns follow the Business Travel Policy in the same way as permanent employees.

## Background

Audits and legal requirements also influenced the Business Travel Policy, which therefore uses clear and verifiable rules.

The Business Travel Policy is reviewed together with employee representatives, so that changes are understood and accepted.

The Business Travel Policy was introduced to make expectations consistent across all Kestrel sites after the company grew beyond its original headquarters.

## Rules

1. Flights shorter than 6 hours are booked in economy class.
2. The daily per diem for business travel is 55 EUR.
3. Hotel costs are reimbursed up to 160 EUR per night.

## Frequently asked questions

**Q:** Where can I find the current version of the Business Travel Policy?
**A:** The current version is always published on the intranet; printed copies may be outdated.

**Q:** Who answers questions about the Business Travel Policy?
**A:** Your manager is the first contact; HR helps with questions the manager cannot answer.

**Q:** Are earlier versions of the Business Travel Policy still valid?
**A:** No. Earlier versions are archived for audit purposes only and must not be applied.

**Q:** What happens if the Business Travel Policy conflicts with local law?
**A:** Local law always takes precedence, and HR documents the deviation.

## Responsibilities

HR maintains the Business Travel Policy, answers questions about it and reports on its use to the management team.

Managers apply the Business Travel Policy consistently and document exceptions they approve.

Employees follow the Business Travel Policy and ask their manager or HR when something is unclear.

The document owner of the Business Travel Policy reviews it regularly and publishes changes on the intranet.

## Exceptions

Repeated or deliberate violations of the Business Travel Policy can lead to disciplinary measures.

Approved exceptions to the Business Travel Policy are documented and reviewed when the policy is updated.

Exceptions to the Business Travel Policy require written approval from the responsible manager and HR.

## Procedure

1. Keep the confirmation for your records
2. Inform your team about any absence or change that affects them
3. Attach the required documents
4. Submit the request through the HR portal

## Contacts

For urgent questions about the Business Travel Policy outside office hours, contact the duty manager of your site.

Questions about the Business Travel Policy go to your manager first. HR business partners support managers and employees with the interpretation.
