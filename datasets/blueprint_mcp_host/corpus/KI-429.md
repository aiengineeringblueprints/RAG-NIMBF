# Project Brief PRJ-229: Pine Climate Control Upgrade

Owner: Kestrel Instruments. Classification: internal.

## Background

Project PRJ-229, Pine Climate Control Upgrade, is delivered for Nessby Kraft GmbH.

Nessby Kraft GmbH commissioned project PRJ-229 to modernise measurement and monitoring at one of its sites. The existing equipment had reached the end of its service life.

The goal of project PRJ-229 is to improve data quality and reduce maintenance effort for Nessby Kraft GmbH.

Project PRJ-229 replaces manual readings with continuous monitoring, so that Nessby Kraft GmbH can react to deviations faster.

Status reports are shared with Nessby Kraft GmbH every two weeks. Change requests follow the standard change process and need written approval.

A steering committee with representatives of Nessby Kraft GmbH and Kestrel meets at every milestone of project PRJ-229.

Live figures of project PRJ-229 such as budget spent and percent complete are tracked in the project system, not in this brief.

## Scope of delivery

Civil works and cabling outside the measuring points are not part of project PRJ-229 and are provided by Nessby Kraft GmbH.

Optional services in project PRJ-229, such as extended support, are offered separately.

The scope of project PRJ-229 covers delivery, installation and commissioning of Kestrel KQ5 devices and their integration into the customer's control system.

Project PRJ-229 includes training for the customer's maintenance staff and a documentation package for audits.

## Goals

- Train the customer's technicians
- Reduce manual inspection rounds
- Document the installation for later audits
