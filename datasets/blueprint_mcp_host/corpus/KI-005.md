# Kestrel KT4 Datasheet

Owner: Kestrel Instruments. Classification: internal.

## Overview

Product overview: the Kestrel KT4 (temperature probe) is ordered with SKU KS-1005 and has been on the market since it was launched in 2022.

Every Kestrel KT4 leaves the factory with an individual test report. The report documents the final functional test and the configuration loaded before shipment, which simplifies acceptance testing on site.

## Applications

In research laboratories the Kestrel KT4 documents environmental conditions for experiments that must be reproducible and traceable.

## Mounting

Mount the Kestrel KT4 on a vibration-free surface away from direct sunlight and heat sources. Leave enough clearance around the housing for cable entry and later maintenance work.

## Electrical connection

The terminals of the Kestrel KT4 accept solid and stranded conductors. Use ferrules for stranded wire and tighten the terminal screws with a suitable screwdriver.
