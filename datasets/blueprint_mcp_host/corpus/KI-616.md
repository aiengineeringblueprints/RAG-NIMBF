# Supplier Profile: Helix Supply

Kestrel Instruments, internal document.

## Overview

Kestrel buys aluminium enclosures from Helix Supply. Helix Supply is based in Finland.

Helix Supply produces at its own facilities in Finland and holds the usual quality certifications for its industry.

Helix Supply is a long-standing supplier of Kestrel Instruments and delivers aluminium enclosures for several product families.

Kestrel sources aluminium enclosures from Helix Supply because of consistent quality and good technical support.

## Delivery performance

During the component shortage the lead time temporarily rose to 14 weeks. The current standard lead time is 5 weeks. The current framework contract runs until 2029.

## Supply risks

- Raw material price changes
- Transport disruptions
- Single-source dependency for this component
- Capacity limits during demand peaks

## Commercial terms

Price changes from Helix Supply must be announced in advance and are reviewed by the purchasing board.

Payment terms with Helix Supply follow the Kestrel standard terms unless the contract states otherwise.

The framework contract with Helix Supply defines prices, delivery terms and quality requirements for aluminium enclosures.

## Responsible sourcing

Helix Supply reports on energy use and waste reduction as part of the annual supplier review.

Helix Supply has signed the Kestrel supplier code of conduct.

Packaging from Helix Supply is reusable or recyclable wherever possible.

## Audit history

Helix Supply has implemented all corrective actions from previous audits on time.

Audit reports for Helix Supply are stored in the quality system and shared with purchasing.

Kestrel quality engineers audit Helix Supply regularly; the last audit found no major deviations.

## Deliveries

Helix Supply offers consignment stock for aluminium enclosures, which reduces the risk of shortages.

Forecasts for aluminium enclosures are shared with Helix Supply monthly to support their capacity planning.

Deliveries from Helix Supply go to the Aarhus plant and the Stavanger warehouse. Delivery schedules and open purchase orders are tracked in the ERP system.

## Quality

Supplier performance of Helix Supply is reviewed every quarter by purchasing and quality.

Helix Supply shares test reports with every delivery of aluminium enclosures, which simplifies incoming inspection.

Incoming inspection at Kestrel checks samples of every delivery from Helix Supply. Deviations are handled through supplier corrective action requests.
