# Training Course: Sensor Calibration Basics

Kestrel Instruments, internal document.

## Description

The course Sensor Calibration Basics lasts 3 days and is taught by Erik Kjeldsen.

Participants of Sensor Calibration Basics work in small groups, so trainers can respond to individual questions.

The course Sensor Calibration Basics gives participants the practical knowledge they need in daily work with Kestrel products.

Sensor Calibration Basics combines short theory blocks with hands-on exercises on real devices.

It accepts at most 14 participants and costs 1950 EUR per person.

The course has no prerequisites.

## Who should attend

Participants of Sensor Calibration Basics should be familiar with basic measurement technology.

## Materials

Demo devices and tools for Sensor Calibration Basics are provided by Kestrel; participants do not need to bring equipment apart from a laptop.

After Sensor Calibration Basics participants keep access to the recorded theory blocks for later reference.

Participants of Sensor Calibration Basics receive the course slides and exercise descriptions in digital form.

## Certification

Participants who do not pass the assessment of Sensor Calibration Basics can repeat it at a later date free of charge.

Sensor Calibration Basics ends with a short practical assessment on a demo device.
