# Kestrel KG2 Datasheet

Owner: Kestrel Instruments. Classification: internal.

## Overview

Product overview: the Kestrel KG2 (gas sensor) is ordered with SKU KS-1017 and has been on the market since it was launched in 2025.

The Kestrel KG2 is designed for continuous operation in demanding industrial environments. It combines a robust enclosure with a measurement core that Kestrel Instruments develops and calibrates in-house, so readings stay stable over long service intervals.

The Kestrel KG2 belongs to the Kestrel gas sensor range and shares accessories, mounting kits and configuration tools with the other models of its family. Spare parts remain available for the whole support period of the product.

- Minimum operating temperature: -10 °C

- Accuracy: ±20 ppm

The Kestrel KG2 operates between -10 °C and 105 °C and communicates via Modbus TCP.

## Transport and storage

After long storage, check the seals of the Kestrel KG2 before installation and replace them if they have hardened.

Store the Kestrel KG2 in its original packaging in a dry place, protected from dust, direct sunlight and condensation.

Transport the Kestrel KG2 only in packaging that protects it against shocks; damage caused by unsuitable packaging is not covered by the warranty.

## Typical use cases

In water treatment plants the Kestrel KG2 monitors process conditions continuously and feeds its readings into the plant control system, where operators set alarms for deviations.

Data center operators install the Kestrel KG2 in server rooms to detect unfavourable conditions before they affect equipment availability.

## Mounting

Mount the Kestrel KG2 on a vibration-free surface away from direct sunlight and heat sources. Leave enough clearance around the housing for cable entry and later maintenance work.

Where the Kestrel KG2 is installed in a process line, use the supplied adapter and check the sealing surfaces for damage before tightening the process connection.

## Electrical connection

Use shielded twisted-pair cable for the communication line of the Kestrel KG2 and terminate the bus at both ends according to the rules of the fieldbus in use.

Install a suitable fuse in the supply line of the Kestrel KG2 and protect the signal lines against surges where long cable runs leave the building.

## Frequently asked questions

**Q:** Can the Kestrel KG2 be mounted next to a frequency converter?
**A:** Keep as much distance as possible and use shielded cables to limit electromagnetic interference.

**Q:** How is the Kestrel KG2 protected against unauthorised changes?
**A:** A user code can lock the configuration; without it settings can be read but not changed.

**Q:** How do I find the firmware version of my Kestrel KG2?
**A:** The configurator shows it on the device information page; the current release is listed in the firmware release notes.
