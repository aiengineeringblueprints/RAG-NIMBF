# Onboarding Guide

Kestrel Instruments. Internal use only.

## Purpose

This document describes the rules of the Onboarding Guide at Kestrel Instruments and explains why they exist. It gives employees and managers a common reference for daily decisions.

## Rules

Every new employee is assigned an onboarding buddy. The probation review takes place after 90 days. New employees complete the safety induction within their first 2 weeks.
