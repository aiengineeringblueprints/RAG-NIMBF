# Project Brief PRJ-206: Harbor Metering Pilot

Owner: Kestrel Instruments. Classification: internal.

## Introduction

Falkberg Foods GmbH commissioned project PRJ-206 (Harbor Metering Pilot).

Project PRJ-206 replaces manual readings with continuous monitoring, so that Falkberg Foods GmbH can react to deviations faster.

Falkberg Foods GmbH commissioned project PRJ-206 to modernise measurement and monitoring at one of its sites. The existing equipment had reached the end of its service life.

## Goals

- Hand over a validated configuration backup
- Integrate all measuring points into the customer's control system
- Replace legacy instruments with current Kestrel devices

## Scope

Civil works and cabling outside the measuring points are not part of project PRJ-206 and are provided by Falkberg Foods GmbH.

The scope of project PRJ-206 covers delivery, installation and commissioning of Kestrel KW7 devices and their integration into the customer's control system.
