# Supplier Profile: Atlas Parts

Kestrel Instruments. Internal use only.

## Introduction

Kestrel buys pressure diaphragms from Atlas Parts. Atlas Parts is based in Poland.

Kestrel sources pressure diaphragms from Atlas Parts because of consistent quality and good technical support.

Atlas Parts is a long-standing supplier of Kestrel Instruments and delivers pressure diaphragms for several product families.

Atlas Parts produces at its own facilities in Poland and holds the usual quality certifications for its industry.

## Delivery performance

During the component shortage the lead time temporarily rose to 19 weeks. The current standard lead time is 9 weeks. The current framework contract runs until 2029.

## Audits

Atlas Parts has implemented all corrective actions from previous audits on time.

Audit reports for Atlas Parts are stored in the quality system and shared with purchasing.

Kestrel quality engineers audit Atlas Parts regularly; the last audit found no major deviations.

## Logistics

Atlas Parts offers consignment stock for pressure diaphragms, which reduces the risk of shortages.

Deliveries from Atlas Parts go to the Aarhus plant and the Stavanger warehouse. Delivery schedules and open purchase orders are tracked in the ERP system.

Forecasts for pressure diaphragms are shared with Atlas Parts monthly to support their capacity planning.

## Responsible sourcing

Atlas Parts has signed the Kestrel supplier code of conduct.

Packaging from Atlas Parts is reusable or recyclable wherever possible.

Atlas Parts reports on energy use and waste reduction as part of the annual supplier review.

## Risks

- Transport disruptions
- Single-source dependency for this component

## Contract terms

The framework contract with Atlas Parts defines prices, delivery terms and quality requirements for pressure diaphragms.

Payment terms with Atlas Parts follow the Kestrel standard terms unless the contract states otherwise.

Price changes from Atlas Parts must be announced in advance and are reviewed by the purchasing board.

## Quality management

Atlas Parts shares test reports with every delivery of pressure diaphragms, which simplifies incoming inspection.

Supplier performance of Atlas Parts is reviewed every quarter by purchasing and quality.

Incoming inspection at Kestrel checks samples of every delivery from Atlas Parts. Deviations are handled through supplier corrective action requests.
