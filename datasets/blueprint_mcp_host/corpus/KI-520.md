# Incident Report INC-2026-187

Owner: Kestrel Instruments. Classification: internal.

## Incident summary

Incident INC-2026-187 interrupted measurements at Ahlberg Data AS and was handled by customer support together with engineering.

This report documents incident INC-2026-187 at Ahlberg Data AS. It follows the blameless postmortem format and focuses on causes and improvements, not on individuals.

Incident INC-2026-187 occurred on 1 August 2026 at Ahlberg Data AS. The affected product was the Kestrel KP5 and the incident was classified as severity 3.

## Impact

The customer had to fall back on manual readings while incident INC-2026-187 was open.

Data gaps caused by incident INC-2026-187 were documented and shared with the customer.

Measurements at Ahlberg Data AS were unavailable or unreliable during the incident. No safety functions were affected.

The final total downtime was 372 minutes.

## Detection

Monitoring at Ahlberg Data AS raised a communication alarm, which led to the investigation of incident INC-2026-187.

Incident INC-2026-187 was detected when Ahlberg Data AS noticed missing values in the control system and opened a support case.

## Cause

Root cause analysis identified a misconfigured Modbus address.

## Customer communication

Ahlberg Data AS received status updates from customer support throughout incident INC-2026-187.

A written summary of incident INC-2026-187 was sent to Ahlberg Data AS together with this report.

After incident INC-2026-187 the account manager held a review meeting with Ahlberg Data AS to explain causes and actions.

## Contributing factors

- Documentation of the installation was incomplete
- The affected installation was not on the latest firmware
- Monitoring did not alert on the first symptoms
- The fault was first mistaken for a network problem

## Corrective actions

| Action | Owner | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Ship the fix with firmware 4.9.4 | Engineering | done |
| Update the installation checklist | Quality | done |
| Review spare part stock for key customers | Logistics | open |

The fix shipped with firmware 4.9.4.

## Follow-up

The effectiveness of the corrective actions for incident INC-2026-187 will be checked in the next quality review.

Similar installations at other customers were checked for the conditions that led to incident INC-2026-187.

The knowledge base was updated with the diagnosis steps used during incident INC-2026-187.

## Lessons learned

- Checklists need regular review after incidents
- Remote diagnostics access was essential
