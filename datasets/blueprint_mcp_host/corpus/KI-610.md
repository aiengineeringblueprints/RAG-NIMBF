# Supplier Profile: Nordic Components

Kestrel Instruments. Internal use only.

## Overview

Nordic Components supplies gas sensing cells to Kestrel Instruments. Nordic Components is based in Germany.

Nordic Components is a long-standing supplier of Kestrel Instruments and delivers gas sensing cells for several product families.

Nordic Components produces at its own facilities in Germany and holds the usual quality certifications for its industry.

Kestrel sources gas sensing cells from Nordic Components because of consistent quality and good technical support.

## At a glance

- Component: gas sensing cells
- Country: Germany
- Current standard lead time: 9 weeks
- Framework contract until: 2028

## Delivery performance

During the component shortage the lead time temporarily rose to 15 weeks. The current standard lead time is 9 weeks. The current framework contract runs until 2028.

## Contract terms

Payment terms with Nordic Components follow the Kestrel standard terms unless the contract states otherwise.

Price changes from Nordic Components must be announced in advance and are reviewed by the purchasing board.

The framework contract with Nordic Components defines prices, delivery terms and quality requirements for gas sensing cells.

## Quality

Supplier performance of Nordic Components is reviewed every quarter by purchasing and quality.

Incoming inspection at Kestrel checks samples of every delivery from Nordic Components. Deviations are handled through supplier corrective action requests.

Nordic Components shares test reports with every delivery of gas sensing cells, which simplifies incoming inspection.

## Logistics

Nordic Components offers consignment stock for gas sensing cells, which reduces the risk of shortages.

Forecasts for gas sensing cells are shared with Nordic Components monthly to support their capacity planning.

Deliveries from Nordic Components go to the Aarhus plant and the Stavanger warehouse. Delivery schedules and open purchase orders are tracked in the ERP system.

## Sustainability

Packaging from Nordic Components is reusable or recyclable wherever possible.

Nordic Components reports on energy use and waste reduction as part of the annual supplier review.

Nordic Components has signed the Kestrel supplier code of conduct.

## Supply risks

- Exchange rate fluctuations
- Single-source dependency for this component
- Capacity limits during demand peaks

## Audits

Audit reports for Nordic Components are stored in the quality system and shared with purchasing.

Nordic Components has implemented all corrective actions from previous audits on time.

Kestrel quality engineers audit Nordic Components regularly; the last audit found no major deviations.
