# Kestrel KP4 Datasheet

Owner: Kestrel Instruments. Classification: internal.

## Introduction

The Kestrel KP4 is a pressure transmitter sold under SKU KS-1009. It was launched in 2017.

Typical applications of the Kestrel KP4 include process monitoring, building automation and environmental control. The device integrates into existing control systems and does not need proprietary software for basic operation.

## Mechanical installation

Mount the Kestrel KP4 on a vibration-free surface away from direct sunlight and heat sources. Leave enough clearance around the housing for cable entry and later maintenance work.

## Electrical connection

Connect the protective earth of the Kestrel KP4 before connecting the supply. Check the polarity of all terminals against the wiring diagram on the inside of the cover.

## Care and maintenance

Check the cable glands and seals of the Kestrel KP4 for wear during each inspection and replace them if they have become brittle.

## Questions and answers

**Q:** How do I find the firmware version of my Kestrel KP4?
**A:** The configurator shows it on the device information page; the current release is listed in the firmware release notes.

**Q:** Can I replace the sensing element of the Kestrel KP4 myself?
**A:** Only trained service staff should replace it, because the device must be recalibrated afterwards.

**Q:** Can the Kestrel KP4 be mounted next to a frequency converter?
**A:** Keep as much distance as possible and use shielded cables to limit electromagnetic interference.

**Q:** Where do I order spare parts for the Kestrel KP4?
**A:** Spare parts are ordered through the Kestrel sales office responsible for your country.
