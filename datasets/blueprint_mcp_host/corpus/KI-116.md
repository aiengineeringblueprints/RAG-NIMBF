# On-Call Policy

Kestrel Instruments, internal document.

## Purpose of this policy

The On-Call Policy turns legal requirements and company values into practical rules. Where local law sets stricter requirements, local law takes precedence.

The On-Call Policy protects employees, customers and the company by setting clear and fair expectations. It applies equally at every Kestrel site.

## Definitions

- **Manager**: an employee with formal responsibility for a team or cost center
- **Site**: any office, plant, lab or warehouse operated by Kestrel Instruments
- **Employee**: a person with an employment contract with Kestrel Instruments

## Applicability

Temporary staff and interns follow the On-Call Policy in the same way as permanent employees.

Managers are responsible for making sure that their teams know the On-Call Policy and apply it.

## Policy

1. On-call engineers receive a weekly allowance of 450 EUR.
2. An on-call shift lasts 7 days and starts on Monday at 08:00.
3. On-call engineers must acknowledge a page within 10 minutes.

## Roles and responsibilities

The document owner of the On-Call Policy reviews it regularly and publishes changes on the intranet.

Managers apply the On-Call Policy consistently and document exceptions they approve.

## Process

1. Attach the required documents
2. Keep the confirmation for your records
3. Inform your team about any absence or change that affects them
4. Wait for the confirmation before acting on the request
5. Read the relevant section of the On-Call Policy before starting the process

## Deviations

Exceptions to the On-Call Policy require written approval from the responsible manager and HR.

Repeated or deliberate violations of the On-Call Policy can lead to disciplinary measures.

Approved exceptions to the On-Call Policy are documented and reviewed when the policy is updated.
