## History

Kvist Bygg AB values short response times and local support, which is why the account is served by a dedicated account manager.

Kvist Bygg AB is a construction company based in Sweden. The relationship started with a small pilot installation and has expanded to several plants since then.

The installed base at Kvist Bygg AB is documented in the CRM, including installation sites and firmware versions.

Over the years Kvist Bygg AB has standardised several measuring points on Kestrel products, which simplifies spare parts and training on their side.
