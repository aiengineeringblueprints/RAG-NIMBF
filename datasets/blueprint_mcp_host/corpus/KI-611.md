# Supplier Profile: Danube Supply

Kestrel Instruments. Internal use only.

## Overview

Danube Supply supplies M12 connectors to Kestrel Instruments. Danube Supply is based in Germany.

Danube Supply produces at its own facilities in Germany and holds the usual quality certifications for its industry.

Danube Supply is a long-standing supplier of Kestrel Instruments and delivers M12 connectors for several product families.

Kestrel sources M12 connectors from Danube Supply because of consistent quality and good technical support.

## Delivery performance

During the component shortage the lead time temporarily rose to 19 weeks. The current standard lead time is 16 weeks. The current framework contract runs until 2029.

## Logistics

Forecasts for M12 connectors are shared with Danube Supply monthly to support their capacity planning.

Danube Supply offers consignment stock for M12 connectors, which reduces the risk of shortages.

Deliveries from Danube Supply go to the Aarhus plant and the Stavanger warehouse. Delivery schedules and open purchase orders are tracked in the ERP system.

## Sustainability

Packaging from Danube Supply is reusable or recyclable wherever possible.

Danube Supply has signed the Kestrel supplier code of conduct.

Danube Supply reports on energy use and waste reduction as part of the annual supplier review.

## Commercial terms

The framework contract with Danube Supply defines prices, delivery terms and quality requirements for M12 connectors.

Payment terms with Danube Supply follow the Kestrel standard terms unless the contract states otherwise.

Price changes from Danube Supply must be announced in advance and are reviewed by the purchasing board.

## Supply risks

- Raw material price changes
- Exchange rate fluctuations

## Audits

Danube Supply has implemented all corrective actions from previous audits on time.

Kestrel quality engineers audit Danube Supply regularly; the last audit found no major deviations.

Audit reports for Danube Supply are stored in the quality system and shared with purchasing.

## Quality

Supplier performance of Danube Supply is reviewed every quarter by purchasing and quality.

Incoming inspection at Kestrel checks samples of every delivery from Danube Supply. Deviations are handled through supplier corrective action requests.

Danube Supply shares test reports with every delivery of M12 connectors, which simplifies incoming inspection.
