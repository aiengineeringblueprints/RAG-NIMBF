# Kestrel KQ4 Installation and Operating Manual

Kestrel Instruments, internal document.

## Introduction

The Kestrel KQ4 is a power quality meter sold under SKU KS-1039. It was launched in 2015.

The Kestrel KQ4 is designed for continuous operation in demanding industrial environments. It combines a robust enclosure with a measurement core that Kestrel Instruments develops and calibrates in-house, so readings stay stable over long service intervals.

Typical applications of the Kestrel KQ4 include process monitoring, building automation and environmental control. The device integrates into existing control systems and does not need proprietary software for basic operation.

Every Kestrel KQ4 leaves the factory with an individual test report. The report documents the final functional test and the configuration loaded before shipment, which simplifies acceptance testing on site.

The Kestrel KQ4 belongs to the Kestrel power quality meter range and shares accessories, mounting kits and configuration tools with the other models of its family. Spare parts remain available for the whole support period of the product.

## Safety instructions

Only qualified personnel may install, commission or service the Kestrel KQ4. Read this document completely before working on the device and keep it available at the place of installation.

If the housing of the Kestrel KQ4 is visibly damaged, take the device out of service and contact customer support before reconnecting it.

Disconnect the supply voltage before opening the terminal compartment of the Kestrel KQ4. Work on live circuits is permitted only where national regulations allow it and the necessary protective measures are in place.

Modifications to the Kestrel KQ4 that are not described in this document are not permitted. Use only original spare parts and accessories supplied by Kestrel Instruments.

Do not operate the Kestrel KQ4 outside the conditions stated in the specification table. Operation outside these limits can damage the device and void the warranty.

## Mechanical installation

Mount the Kestrel KQ4 on a vibration-free surface away from direct sunlight and heat sources. Leave enough clearance around the housing for cable entry and later maintenance work.

Where the Kestrel KQ4 is installed in a process line, use the supplied adapter and check the sealing surfaces for damage before tightening the process connection.

The Kestrel KQ4 can be mounted on a wall bracket, a pipe clamp or a standard mounting rail. Choose the mounting kit that matches the installation and tighten the screws evenly.

After mechanical installation, check that the Kestrel KQ4 is firmly seated and that the housing cover closes without force.

Orient the cable entries of the Kestrel KQ4 downwards when installing outdoors, so that condensation cannot collect in the terminal compartment.

## Electrical connection

Route signal cables to the Kestrel KQ4 separately from power cables and connect the cable shield on the controller side only, to avoid ground loops.

The terminals of the Kestrel KQ4 accept solid and stranded conductors. Use ferrules for stranded wire and tighten the terminal screws with a suitable screwdriver.

Use shielded twisted-pair cable for the communication line of the Kestrel KQ4 and terminate the bus at both ends according to the rules of the fieldbus in use.

Install a suitable fuse in the supply line of the Kestrel KQ4 and protect the signal lines against surges where long cable runs leave the building.

Connect the protective earth of the Kestrel KQ4 before connecting the supply. Check the polarity of all terminals against the wiring diagram on the inside of the cover.

## Commissioning

Once commissioning is complete, close the housing of the Kestrel KQ4, seal unused cable entries and record the configuration in the asset management system.

Set the communication address of the Kestrel KQ4 before connecting it to a bus with other devices, otherwise address conflicts can interrupt communication.

Switch on the supply and wait until the status LED of the Kestrel KQ4 indicates normal operation. The device runs a self-test after every start.

Compare the first readings of the Kestrel KQ4 with a reference instrument and document the result in the commissioning report.

## Device configuration

Access to the configuration of the Kestrel KQ4 can be protected with a user code. Without the code, settings are visible but cannot be changed.

A factory reset restores the delivery configuration of the Kestrel KQ4. The calibration data is kept, but communication settings must be entered again.

The Kestrel KQ4 is configured with the Kestrel Configurator, either locally through the service interface or remotely over the fieldbus.

Configuration profiles of the Kestrel KQ4 can be exported and imported, which speeds up the commissioning of many identical measuring points.

Alarm limits, filter settings and engineering units of the Kestrel KQ4 can be changed during operation. Changes take effect immediately and are logged in the device event list.

## Normal operation

Readings of the Kestrel KQ4 can be logged in the control system or in a Kestrel data logger for later analysis.

Diagnostic messages of the Kestrel KQ4 follow the NAMUR recommendation and distinguish between failure, function check, out of specification and maintenance required.

The status LED of the Kestrel KQ4 shows normal operation, maintenance requests and faults with different colours and blink patterns.

During normal operation the Kestrel KQ4 needs no user intervention. Measured values and diagnostic information are available continuously through the communication interface.

## Service and maintenance

Inspect the Kestrel KQ4 during scheduled plant shutdowns, clean the housing with a dry cloth, and record every intervention in the asset log.

Firmware updates for the Kestrel KQ4 are distributed through the Kestrel service portal. Read the release notes before updating devices in the field.

Recalibrate the Kestrel KQ4 according to the calibration procedure of Kestrel Instruments or send it to an accredited calibration lab.

Do not use solvents or high-pressure cleaners on the Kestrel KQ4; they can damage the seals and the sensing element.

Check the cable glands and seals of the Kestrel KQ4 for wear during each inspection and replace them if they have become brittle.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Possible cause | Remedy |
|---|---|---|
| Unstable reading | Electrical interference | Check shielding and cable routing |
| Status LED shows a fault | Internal self-test failed | Restart; contact support if it persists |
| Values frozen | Device in function-check mode | Leave service mode in the configurator |
| Alarm without cause | Alarm limits too tight | Review limits and hysteresis |
| Reading drifts slowly | Sensing element contaminated | Clean or recalibrate the device |
| No communication | Wrong bus address | Set a unique address and restart |
| Condensation in housing | Cable entries facing upwards | Remount with entries facing down |

## Questions and answers

**Q:** Can I replace the sensing element of the Kestrel KQ4 myself?
**A:** Only trained service staff should replace it, because the device must be recalibrated afterwards.

**Q:** Can the Kestrel KQ4 be used outdoors?
**A:** Yes, provided the enclosure rating stated in the specification table matches the installation conditions and the cable entries face downwards.

**Q:** Is the Kestrel KQ4 suitable for hazardous areas?
**A:** Only variants with an explicit hazardous area certification may be used there; check the nameplate before installation.

**Q:** Do I need special software for the Kestrel KQ4?
**A:** No. The device works with standard control systems; the Kestrel Configurator is only needed for advanced settings.

**Q:** How do I find the firmware version of my Kestrel KQ4?
**A:** The configurator shows it on the device information page; the current release is listed in the firmware release notes.

**Q:** Can the Kestrel KQ4 be mounted next to a frequency converter?
**A:** Keep as much distance as possible and use shielded cables to limit electromagnetic interference.
