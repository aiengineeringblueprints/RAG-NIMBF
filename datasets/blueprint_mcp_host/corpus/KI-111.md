# Calibration Procedure

Kestrel Instruments. Internal use only.

## Why this policy exists

The Calibration Procedure turns legal requirements and company values into practical rules. Where local law sets stricter requirements, local law takes precedence.

This document describes the rules of the Calibration Procedure at Kestrel Instruments and explains why they exist. It gives employees and managers a common reference for daily decisions.

The Calibration Procedure protects employees, customers and the company by setting clear and fair expectations. It applies equally at every Kestrel site.

## Who this applies to

Managers are responsible for making sure that their teams know the Calibration Procedure and apply it.

Temporary staff and interns follow the Calibration Procedure in the same way as permanent employees.

The Calibration Procedure applies to all employees and contractors of Kestrel Instruments unless a local works agreement states otherwise.

## Background

The Calibration Procedure was introduced to make expectations consistent across all Kestrel sites after the company grew beyond its original headquarters.

Audits and legal requirements also influenced the Calibration Procedure, which therefore uses clear and verifiable rules.

The Calibration Procedure is reviewed together with employee representatives, so that changes are understood and accepted.

## Definitions

- **Contractor**: a person working for Kestrel through a service agreement
- **Site**: any office, plant, lab or warehouse operated by Kestrel Instruments
- **Intranet**: the internal information portal of Kestrel Instruments

## Requirements

1. Reference instruments are recalibrated every 6 months.
2. Calibration certificates are issued within 3 working days after a device is received.
3. Each calibration tolerance report is archived for 10 years.

## Deviations

Repeated or deliberate violations of the Calibration Procedure can lead to disciplinary measures.

Approved exceptions to the Calibration Procedure are documented and reviewed when the policy is updated.

Exceptions to the Calibration Procedure require written approval from the responsible manager and HR.

## Frequently asked questions

**Q:** Who answers questions about the Calibration Procedure?
**A:** Your manager is the first contact; HR helps with questions the manager cannot answer.

**Q:** Are earlier versions of the Calibration Procedure still valid?
**A:** No. Earlier versions are archived for audit purposes only and must not be applied.

**Q:** What happens if the Calibration Procedure conflicts with local law?
**A:** Local law always takes precedence, and HR documents the deviation.

**Q:** Where can I find the current version of the Calibration Procedure?
**A:** The current version is always published on the intranet; printed copies may be outdated.

## Procedure

1. Keep the confirmation for your records
2. Discuss open questions with your manager
3. Wait for the confirmation before acting on the request

## Questions and contacts

The intranet page of the Calibration Procedure lists the responsible HR contact for each site.

Questions about the Calibration Procedure go to your manager first. HR business partners support managers and employees with the interpretation.

## Responsibilities

Employees follow the Calibration Procedure and ask their manager or HR when something is unclear.

HR maintains the Calibration Procedure, answers questions about it and reports on its use to the management team.

The document owner of the Calibration Procedure reviews it regularly and publishes changes on the intranet.

Managers apply the Calibration Procedure consistently and document exceptions they approve.
