# Training Course: Industrial Networking

Owner: Kestrel Instruments. Classification: internal.

## About the course

Industrial Networking: 4 days, trainer Lukas Engstrom.

Industrial Networking combines short theory blocks with hands-on exercises on real devices.

The course Industrial Networking gives participants the practical knowledge they need in daily work with Kestrel products.

It accepts at most 13 participants and costs 300 EUR per person.

Participants must first complete Firmware Update Workshop.

## Assessment

Industrial Networking ends with a short practical assessment on a demo device.

## Materials

Demo devices and tools for Industrial Networking are provided by Kestrel; participants do not need to bring equipment apart from a laptop.
