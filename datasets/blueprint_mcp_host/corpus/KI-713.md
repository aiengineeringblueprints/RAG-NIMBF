# Training Course: Customer Support Excellence

Kestrel Instruments, internal document.

## Description

Customer Support Excellence: 1 days, trainer Sindre Eriksen.

Participants of Customer Support Excellence work in small groups, so trainers can respond to individual questions.

Customer Support Excellence combines short theory blocks with hands-on exercises on real devices.

It accepts at most 20 participants and costs 250 EUR per person.

The course has no prerequisites.

## Target audience

Participants of Customer Support Excellence should be familiar with basic measurement technology.

Customer Support Excellence is aimed at technicians and engineers who install, configure or maintain Kestrel products.

Customers, partners and Kestrel employees can register for Customer Support Excellence.

## Learning objectives

- Understand the principles behind the topic
- Configure devices with the Kestrel Configurator
- Diagnose typical faults
- Apply the relevant safety rules
- Use the service portal efficiently

## Course materials

Participants of Customer Support Excellence receive the course slides and exercise descriptions in digital form.

After Customer Support Excellence participants keep access to the recorded theory blocks for later reference.

## Course agenda

### Day 1

- Review and questions
- Theory block
- Group work on case studies
- Troubleshooting lab
- Introduction and safety briefing
