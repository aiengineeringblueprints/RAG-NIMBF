# Incident Report INC-2026-228

Kestrel Instruments, internal document.

## Summary

This report documents incident INC-2026-228 at Torvik Kraft AS. It follows the blameless postmortem format and focuses on causes and improvements, not on individuals.

Incident INC-2026-228 interrupted measurements at Torvik Kraft AS and was handled by customer support together with engineering.

Incident INC-2026-228 occurred on 8 March 2026 at Torvik Kraft AS. The affected product was the Kestrel KH5 and the incident was classified as severity 2.

## How the incident was detected

Incident INC-2026-228 was detected when Torvik Kraft AS noticed missing values in the control system and opened a support case.

Monitoring at Torvik Kraft AS raised a communication alarm, which led to the investigation of incident INC-2026-228.

## Impact

The customer had to fall back on manual readings while incident INC-2026-228 was open.

Data gaps caused by incident INC-2026-228 were documented and shared with the customer.

Measurements at Torvik Kraft AS were unavailable or unreliable during the incident. No safety functions were affected.

The final total downtime was 473 minutes.

## Root cause analysis

The root cause was a clock synchronisation error.

## Customer communication

Torvik Kraft AS received status updates from customer support throughout incident INC-2026-228.

A written summary of incident INC-2026-228 was sent to Torvik Kraft AS together with this report.

After incident INC-2026-228 the account manager held a review meeting with Torvik Kraft AS to explain causes and actions.

## Contributing factors

- Documentation of the installation was incomplete
- Monitoring did not alert on the first symptoms
- The affected installation was not on the latest firmware
- Spare devices were not available on site

## Corrective actions

| Action | Owner | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Ship the fix with firmware 5.8.4 | Engineering | done |
| Add an alert for the observed symptom | Customer Support | open |
| Share lessons learned in the engineering forum | Engineering | done |
| Update the installation checklist | Quality | done |

The fix shipped with firmware 5.8.4.

## What we learned

- Clear customer communication reduced pressure on the team
- Remote diagnostics access was essential
- Early escalation shortened the investigation
