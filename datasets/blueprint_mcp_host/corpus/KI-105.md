# Information Security Policy

Kestrel Instruments, internal document.

## Why this policy exists

The Information Security Policy protects employees, customers and the company by setting clear and fair expectations. It applies equally at every Kestrel site.

The Information Security Policy turns legal requirements and company values into practical rules. Where local law sets stricter requirements, local law takes precedence.

This document describes the rules of the Information Security Policy at Kestrel Instruments and explains why they exist. It gives employees and managers a common reference for daily decisions.

## Who this applies to

The Information Security Policy applies to all employees and contractors of Kestrel Instruments unless a local works agreement states otherwise.

Managers are responsible for making sure that their teams know the Information Security Policy and apply it.

Temporary staff and interns follow the Information Security Policy in the same way as permanent employees.

## Definitions

- **Employee**: a person with an employment contract with Kestrel Instruments
- **Site**: any office, plant, lab or warehouse operated by Kestrel Instruments
- **Contractor**: a person working for Kestrel through a service agreement

## Background

Audits and legal requirements also influenced the Information Security Policy, which therefore uses clear and verifiable rules.

The Information Security Policy was introduced to make expectations consistent across all Kestrel sites after the company grew beyond its original headquarters.

The Information Security Policy is reviewed together with employee representatives, so that changes are understood and accepted.

## Requirements

Passwords must be changed every 180 days. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all company accounts. A lost or stolen device must be reported to the Information Security Officer within 1 hour.

## Roles and responsibilities

- Employees follow the Information Security Policy and ask their manager or HR when something is unclear.
- Managers apply the Information Security Policy consistently and document exceptions they approve.
- HR maintains the Information Security Policy, answers questions about it and reports on its use to the management team.

## Questions and contacts

The intranet page of the Information Security Policy lists the responsible HR contact for each site.

For urgent questions about the Information Security Policy outside office hours, contact the duty manager of your site.

## How to apply this policy

1. Wait for the confirmation before acting on the request
2. Attach the required documents
3. Read the relevant section of the Information Security Policy before starting the process
4. Submit the request through the HR portal

## Deviations

Exceptions to the Information Security Policy require written approval from the responsible manager and HR.

Approved exceptions to the Information Security Policy are documented and reviewed when the policy is updated.

Repeated or deliberate violations of the Information Security Policy can lead to disciplinary measures.

## FAQ

**Q:** Are earlier versions of the Information Security Policy still valid?
**A:** No. Earlier versions are archived for audit purposes only and must not be applied.

**Q:** What happens if the Information Security Policy conflicts with local law?
**A:** Local law always takes precedence, and HR documents the deviation.

**Q:** Who answers questions about the Information Security Policy?
**A:** Your manager is the first contact; HR helps with questions the manager cannot answer.

**Q:** Where can I find the current version of the Information Security Policy?
**A:** The current version is always published on the intranet; printed copies may be outdated.
