# Gothenburg Sales Office

Owner: Kestrel Instruments. Classification: internal.

## Introduction

The Gothenburg Sales Office in Gothenburg, Sweden, is headed by Martin Andersen, Sales Director Nordics.

Teams at the Gothenburg Sales Office in Gothenburg collaborate daily with colleagues at other sites through shared tools and regular video meetings.

The Gothenburg Sales Office has grown steadily since it opened and is an important part of the company's presence in Sweden.

The Gothenburg Sales Office is part of the Kestrel Instruments network of sites and works closely with the headquarters and the other locations.

## Key facts

The Gothenburg office serves customers in Sweden and Finland. Eighteen sales and application engineers work in Gothenburg. The Gothenburg office opened in 2019.

## History

The Gothenburg Sales Office was renovated recently to provide modern workplaces and better meeting rooms.

Over the years the Gothenburg Sales Office has taken on additional responsibilities and now supports colleagues and customers across the region.

## Directions

The Gothenburg Sales Office can be reached by public transport from the city center of Gothenburg. Parking for visitors is available next to the main entrance.

Detailed directions to the Gothenburg Sales Office are sent with every meeting invitation. Taxis can be ordered at the reception.
