# Training Course: Power Quality Analysis

Owner: Kestrel Instruments. Classification: internal.

## About the course

The course Power Quality Analysis lasts 1 days and is taught by Lena Aune.

Power Quality Analysis combines short theory blocks with hands-on exercises on real devices.

The course Power Quality Analysis gives participants the practical knowledge they need in daily work with Kestrel products.

Participants of Power Quality Analysis work in small groups, so trainers can respond to individual questions.

It accepts at most 6 participants and costs 750 EUR per person.

Participants must first complete Data Logger Programming.

## Learning objectives

- Use the service portal efficiently
- Configure devices with the Kestrel Configurator
- Apply the relevant safety rules

## Certification

Power Quality Analysis ends with a short practical assessment on a demo device.

Participants who do not pass the assessment of Power Quality Analysis can repeat it at a later date free of charge.

## Course materials

Participants of Power Quality Analysis receive the course slides and exercise descriptions in digital form.

After Power Quality Analysis participants keep access to the recorded theory blocks for later reference.

Demo devices and tools for Power Quality Analysis are provided by Kestrel; participants do not need to bring equipment apart from a laptop.

## Who should attend

Customers, partners and Kestrel employees can register for Power Quality Analysis.

Participants of Power Quality Analysis should be familiar with basic measurement technology.

Power Quality Analysis is aimed at technicians and engineers who install, configure or maintain Kestrel products.

## Course agenda

- Troubleshooting lab
- Group work on case studies
- Final assessment

## FAQ

**Q:** Is Power Quality Analysis also offered online?
**A:** Selected dates are offered online; hands-on exercises then use remote demo devices.

**A:** Yes, after completing the final assessment.

**Q:** Do I need to bring a laptop to Power Quality Analysis?
**A:** Yes, please bring a laptop with the Kestrel Configurator installed.
