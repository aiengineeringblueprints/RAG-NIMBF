# Incident Report INC-2026-196

Kestrel Instruments, internal document.

## Incident summary

This report documents incident INC-2026-196 at Strandli Bygg AS. It follows the blameless postmortem format and focuses on causes and improvements, not on individuals.

Incident INC-2026-196 interrupted measurements at Strandli Bygg AS and was handled by customer support together with engineering.

Incident INC-2026-196 occurred on 29 April 2026 at Strandli Bygg AS. The affected product was the Kestrel KV5 and the incident was classified as severity 3.

## Detection

A technician of Strandli Bygg AS reported unusual readings during a routine inspection, which started incident INC-2026-196.

Incident INC-2026-196 was detected when Strandli Bygg AS noticed missing values in the control system and opened a support case.

## Impact

Measurements at Strandli Bygg AS were unavailable or unreliable during the incident. No safety functions were affected.

Data gaps caused by incident INC-2026-196 were documented and shared with the customer.

The customer had to fall back on manual readings while incident INC-2026-196 was open.

The final total downtime was 620 minutes.

## Root cause

Root cause analysis identified a faulty batch of sensing elements from a supplier.

## Contributing factors

- Documentation of the installation was incomplete
- Spare devices were not available on site

## Communication

A written summary of incident INC-2026-196 was sent to Strandli Bygg AS together with this report.

Strandli Bygg AS received status updates from customer support throughout incident INC-2026-196.

After incident INC-2026-196 the account manager held a review meeting with Strandli Bygg AS to explain causes and actions.

## Corrective actions

| Action | Owner | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Ship the fix with firmware 2.2.0 | Engineering | done |
| Update the installation checklist | Quality | open |
| Add an alert for the observed symptom | Customer Support | open |
| Roll out the fix to all affected installations | Engineering | open |

The fix shipped with firmware 2.2.0.

## Next steps

Similar installations at other customers were checked for the conditions that led to incident INC-2026-196.

The effectiveness of the corrective actions for incident INC-2026-196 will be checked in the next quality review.
