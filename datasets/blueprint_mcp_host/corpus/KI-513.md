# Incident Report INC-2026-098

Kestrel Instruments, internal document.

## Overview

Incident INC-2026-098 interrupted measurements at Ramstad Logistics Oy and was handled by customer support together with engineering.

Incident INC-2026-098 occurred on 28 April 2026 at Ramstad Logistics Oy. The affected product was the Kestrel KP4 and the incident was classified as severity 2.

## Customer impact

The customer had to fall back on manual readings while incident INC-2026-098 was open.

The final total downtime was 713 minutes.

## Root cause

Root cause analysis identified a firmware memory leak.

## Contributing factors

- Documentation of the installation was incomplete
- The fault was first mistaken for a network problem
- Spare devices were not available on site

## Measures

| Action | Owner | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Ship the fix with firmware 3.1.5 | Engineering | done |
| Share lessons learned in the engineering forum | Engineering | in progress |
| Review spare part stock for key customers | Logistics | in progress |
| Update the installation checklist | Quality | open |

The fix shipped with firmware 3.1.5.

## Lessons learned

- Early escalation shortened the investigation
- Checklists need regular review after incidents
