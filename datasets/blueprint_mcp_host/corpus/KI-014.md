## Typical use cases

In water treatment plants the Kestrel KF7 monitors process conditions continuously and feeds its readings into the plant control system, where operators set alarms for deviations.

Data center operators install the Kestrel KF7 in server rooms to detect unfavourable conditions before they affect equipment availability.

## Transport and storage

After long storage, check the seals of the Kestrel KF7 before installation and replace them if they have hardened.

Store the Kestrel KF7 in its original packaging in a dry place, protected from dust, direct sunlight and condensation.

Transport the Kestrel KF7 only in packaging that protects it against shocks; damage caused by unsuitable packaging is not covered by the warranty.

## Mechanical installation

Mount the Kestrel KF7 on a vibration-free surface away from direct sunlight and heat sources. Leave enough clearance around the housing for cable entry and later maintenance work.

## Electrical connection

Use shielded twisted-pair cable for the communication line of the Kestrel KF7 and terminate the bus at both ends according to the rules of the fieldbus in use.

Install a suitable fuse in the supply line of the Kestrel KF7 and protect the signal lines against surges where long cable runs leave the building.

## Frequently asked questions

**Q:** Can the Kestrel KF7 be mounted next to a frequency converter?
**A:** Keep as much distance as possible and use shielded cables to limit electromagnetic interference.

**Q:** Can the Kestrel KF7 be used outdoors?
**A:** Yes, provided the enclosure rating stated in the specification table matches the installation conditions and the cable entries face downwards.

**Q:** How is the Kestrel KF7 protected against unauthorised changes?
**A:** A user code can lock the configuration; without it settings can be read but not changed.

**Q:** Where do I order spare parts for the Kestrel KF7?
**A:** Spare parts are ordered through the Kestrel sales office responsible for your country.
