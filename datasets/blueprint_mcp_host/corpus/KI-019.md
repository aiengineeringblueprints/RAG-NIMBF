# Kestrel KG8 Datasheet

Kestrel Instruments. Internal use only.

## Product overview

The Kestrel KG8 is a gas sensor sold under SKU KS-1019. It was launched in 2019.

Typical applications of the Kestrel KG8 include process monitoring, building automation and environmental control. The device integrates into existing control systems and does not need proprietary software for basic operation.

The Kestrel KG8 is designed for continuous operation in demanding industrial environments. It combines a robust enclosure with a measurement core that Kestrel Instruments develops and calibrates in-house, so readings stay stable over long service intervals.

## Transport and storage

Transport the Kestrel KG8 only in packaging that protects it against shocks; damage caused by unsuitable packaging is not covered by the warranty.

After long storage, check the seals of the Kestrel KG8 before installation and replace them if they have hardened.

Store the Kestrel KG8 in its original packaging in a dry place, protected from dust, direct sunlight and condensation.

## Typical use cases

Building operators use the Kestrel KG8 to keep indoor conditions within comfort and energy targets, often as part of a larger building management system.

## Mounting

Mount the Kestrel KG8 on a vibration-free surface away from direct sunlight and heat sources. Leave enough clearance around the housing for cable entry and later maintenance work.

The Kestrel KG8 can be mounted on a wall bracket, a pipe clamp or a standard mounting rail. Choose the mounting kit that matches the installation and tighten the screws evenly.

## Electrical connection

The terminals of the Kestrel KG8 accept solid and stranded conductors. Use ferrules for stranded wire and tighten the terminal screws with a suitable screwdriver.

Install a suitable fuse in the supply line of the Kestrel KG8 and protect the signal lines against surges where long cable runs leave the building.

## Care and maintenance

Recalibrate the Kestrel KG8 according to the calibration procedure of Kestrel Instruments or send it to an accredited calibration lab.

Inspect the Kestrel KG8 during scheduled plant shutdowns, clean the housing with a dry cloth, and record every intervention in the asset log.

## Diagnostics and troubleshooting

| Symptom | Possible cause | Remedy |
|---|---|---|
| Unstable reading | Electrical interference | Check shielding and cable routing |
| Alarm without cause | Alarm limits too tight | Review limits and hysteresis |
| Reading drifts slowly | Sensing element contaminated | Clean or recalibrate the device |
| No reading | No supply voltage | Check fuse, wiring and supply polarity |
