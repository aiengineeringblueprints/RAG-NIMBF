# Incident Report INC-2026-376

Kestrel Instruments. Internal use only.

## Summary

Incident INC-2026-376 interrupted measurements at Bergvik Marine Oy and was handled by customer support together with engineering.

Incident INC-2026-376 occurred on 25 August 2026 at Bergvik Marine Oy. The affected product was the Kestrel KT7 and the incident was classified as severity 3.
