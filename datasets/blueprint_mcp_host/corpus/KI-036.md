# Kestrel KW9 Datasheet

Kestrel Instruments. Internal use only.

## Product overview

The Kestrel KW9 is a wireless gateway sold under SKU KS-1036. It was launched in 2025.

Typical applications of the Kestrel KW9 include process monitoring, building automation and environmental control. The device integrates into existing control systems and does not need proprietary software for basic operation.

Every Kestrel KW9 leaves the factory with an individual test report. The report documents the final functional test and the configuration loaded before shipment, which simplifies acceptance testing on site.

## Storage and transport

Store the Kestrel KW9 in its original packaging in a dry place, protected from dust, direct sunlight and condensation.

Transport the Kestrel KW9 only in packaging that protects it against shocks; damage caused by unsuitable packaging is not covered by the warranty.

After long storage, check the seals of the Kestrel KW9 before installation and replace them if they have hardened.

## Mounting

The Kestrel KW9 can be mounted on a wall bracket, a pipe clamp or a standard mounting rail. Choose the mounting kit that matches the installation and tighten the screws evenly.

Mount the Kestrel KW9 on a vibration-free surface away from direct sunlight and heat sources. Leave enough clearance around the housing for cable entry and later maintenance work.

## Electrical connection

Route signal cables to the Kestrel KW9 separately from power cables and connect the cable shield on the controller side only, to avoid ground loops.

Connect the protective earth of the Kestrel KW9 before connecting the supply. Check the polarity of all terminals against the wiring diagram on the inside of the cover.

## Maintenance

Recalibrate the Kestrel KW9 according to the calibration procedure of Kestrel Instruments or send it to an accredited calibration lab.

Check the cable glands and seals of the Kestrel KW9 for wear during each inspection and replace them if they have become brittle.

## Frequently asked questions

**Q:** Is the Kestrel KW9 suitable for hazardous areas?
**A:** Only variants with an explicit hazardous area certification may be used there; check the nameplate before installation.

**Q:** Can the Kestrel KW9 be mounted next to a frequency converter?
**A:** Keep as much distance as possible and use shielded cables to limit electromagnetic interference.

**Q:** Do I need special software for the Kestrel KW9?
**A:** No. The device works with standard control systems; the Kestrel Configurator is only needed for advanced settings.

**Q:** How is the Kestrel KW9 protected against unauthorised changes?
**A:** A user code can lock the configuration; without it settings can be read but not changed.
