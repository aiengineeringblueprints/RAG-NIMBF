# Supplier Profile: Helix Industries

Owner: Kestrel Instruments. Classification: internal.

## Overview

Helix Industries supplies packaging materials to Kestrel Instruments. Helix Industries is based in Taiwan.

Kestrel sources packaging materials from Helix Industries because of consistent quality and good technical support.

Helix Industries is a long-standing supplier of Kestrel Instruments and delivers packaging materials for several product families.

Helix Industries produces at its own facilities in Taiwan and holds the usual quality certifications for its industry.

## Key data

- Component: packaging materials
- Country: Taiwan
- Current standard lead time: 16 weeks
- Framework contract until: 2030

## Delivery performance

During the component shortage the lead time temporarily rose to 20 weeks. The current standard lead time is 16 weeks. The current framework contract runs until 2030.

## Contract terms

The framework contract with Helix Industries defines prices, delivery terms and quality requirements for packaging materials.

Price changes from Helix Industries must be announced in advance and are reviewed by the purchasing board.

## Sustainability

Helix Industries has signed the Kestrel supplier code of conduct.

Helix Industries reports on energy use and waste reduction as part of the annual supplier review.

Packaging from Helix Industries is reusable or recyclable wherever possible.

## Quality

Incoming inspection at Kestrel checks samples of every delivery from Helix Industries. Deviations are handled through supplier corrective action requests.

Helix Industries shares test reports with every delivery of packaging materials, which simplifies incoming inspection.

## Audits

Audit reports for Helix Industries are stored in the quality system and shared with purchasing.

## Risks

- Transport disruptions
- Capacity limits during demand peaks
