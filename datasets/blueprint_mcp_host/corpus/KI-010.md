# Kestrel KP5 Datasheet

Owner: Kestrel Instruments. Classification: internal.

## Product overview

The Kestrel KP5 is a pressure transmitter sold under SKU KS-1010. It was launched in 2025.

The Kestrel KP5 belongs to the Kestrel pressure transmitter range and shares accessories, mounting kits and configuration tools with the other models of its family. Spare parts remain available for the whole support period of the product.

Typical applications of the Kestrel KP5 include process monitoring, building automation and environmental control. The device integrates into existing control systems and does not need proprietary software for basic operation.

## Transport and storage

After long storage, check the seals of the Kestrel KP5 before installation and replace them if they have hardened.

Store the Kestrel KP5 in its original packaging in a dry place, protected from dust, direct sunlight and condensation.

Transport the Kestrel KP5 only in packaging that protects it against shocks; damage caused by unsuitable packaging is not covered by the warranty.

## Mounting

Orient the cable entries of the Kestrel KP5 downwards when installing outdoors, so that condensation cannot collect in the terminal compartment.

Mount the Kestrel KP5 on a vibration-free surface away from direct sunlight and heat sources. Leave enough clearance around the housing for cable entry and later maintenance work.

## Electrical connection

Connect the protective earth of the Kestrel KP5 before connecting the supply. Check the polarity of all terminals against the wiring diagram on the inside of the cover.

The terminals of the Kestrel KP5 accept solid and stranded conductors. Use ferrules for stranded wire and tighten the terminal screws with a suitable screwdriver.

## Maintenance

Check the cable glands and seals of the Kestrel KP5 for wear during each inspection and replace them if they have become brittle.

Inspect the Kestrel KP5 during scheduled plant shutdowns, clean the housing with a dry cloth, and record every intervention in the asset log.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Possible cause | Remedy |
|---|---|---|
| No reading | No supply voltage | Check fuse, wiring and supply polarity |
| Condensation in housing | Cable entries facing upwards | Remount with entries facing down |
| Intermittent communication | Missing bus termination | Terminate the bus at both ends |
