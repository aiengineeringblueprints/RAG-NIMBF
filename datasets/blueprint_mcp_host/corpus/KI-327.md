## Background

Skogly Energi AB is an energy company based in Sweden. The relationship started with a small pilot installation and has expanded to several plants since then.

Over the years Skogly Energi AB has standardised several measuring points on Kestrel products, which simplifies spare parts and training on their side.

The installed base at Skogly Energi AB is documented in the CRM, including installation sites and firmware versions.

During the first years Skogly Energi AB mainly bought standard products. Later projects added integration services and customer training.
