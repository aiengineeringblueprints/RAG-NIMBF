# Kestrel KH9 Datasheet

Kestrel Instruments, internal document.

## Introduction

The Kestrel KH9 is a humidity sensor sold under SKU KS-1004. It was launched in 2019.

Typical applications of the Kestrel KH9 include process monitoring, building automation and environmental control. The device integrates into existing control systems and does not need proprietary software for basic operation.

The Kestrel KH9 is designed for continuous operation in demanding industrial environments. It combines a robust enclosure with a measurement core that Kestrel Instruments develops and calibrates in-house, so readings stay stable over long service intervals.

## Technical data

- Minimum operating temperature: -40 °C
- Maximum operating temperature: 80 °C
- Supply current: 7 mA
- Communication interface: BACnet MS/TP
- Accuracy: ±1.5 %RH
- Enclosure rating: IP67
- Weight (current revision): 665 g

The Kestrel KH9 operates between -40 °C and 80 °C and communicates via BACnet MS/TP.

## Storage and transport

Transport the Kestrel KH9 only in packaging that protects it against shocks; damage caused by unsuitable packaging is not covered by the warranty.

After long storage, check the seals of the Kestrel KH9 before installation and replace them if they have hardened.

Store the Kestrel KH9 in its original packaging in a dry place, protected from dust, direct sunlight and condensation.

## Compliance

The Kestrel KH9 carries the CE marking and complies with the applicable European directives for electromagnetic compatibility and electrical safety.

The Kestrel KH9 is manufactured under a quality management system certified to ISO 9001.

## Mechanical installation

Mount the Kestrel KH9 on a vibration-free surface away from direct sunlight and heat sources. Leave enough clearance around the housing for cable entry and later maintenance work.

Where the Kestrel KH9 is installed in a process line, use the supplied adapter and check the sealing surfaces for damage before tightening the process connection.

## Electrical connection

Use shielded twisted-pair cable for the communication line of the Kestrel KH9 and terminate the bus at both ends according to the rules of the fieldbus in use.

Route signal cables to the Kestrel KH9 separately from power cables and connect the cable shield on the controller side only, to avoid ground loops.

## Service and maintenance

Recalibrate the Kestrel KH9 according to the calibration procedure of Kestrel Instruments or send it to an accredited calibration lab.

Inspect the Kestrel KH9 during scheduled plant shutdowns, clean the housing with a dry cloth, and record every intervention in the asset log.

## Fault finding

| Symptom | Possible cause | Remedy |
|---|---|---|
| Condensation in housing | Cable entries facing upwards | Remount with entries facing down |
| Values frozen | Device in function-check mode | Leave service mode in the configurator |
| No communication | Wrong bus address | Set a unique address and restart |
