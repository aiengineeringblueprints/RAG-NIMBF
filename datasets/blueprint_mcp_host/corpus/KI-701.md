# Training Course: Modbus Fundamentals

Kestrel Instruments. Internal use only.

## About the course

Modbus Fundamentals: 2 days, trainer Lena Aune.

Modbus Fundamentals combines short theory blocks with hands-on exercises on real devices.

Participants of Modbus Fundamentals work in small groups, so trainers can respond to individual questions.

The course Modbus Fundamentals gives participants the practical knowledge they need in daily work with Kestrel products.

It accepts at most 10 participants and costs 1350 EUR per person.

The course has no prerequisites.

## Materials

Demo devices and tools for Modbus Fundamentals are provided by Kestrel; participants do not need to bring equipment apart from a laptop.

After Modbus Fundamentals participants keep access to the recorded theory blocks for later reference.

Participants of Modbus Fundamentals receive the course slides and exercise descriptions in digital form.

## Who should attend

Customers, partners and Kestrel employees can register for Modbus Fundamentals.

Participants of Modbus Fundamentals should be familiar with basic measurement technology.

Modbus Fundamentals is aimed at technicians and engineers who install, configure or maintain Kestrel products.

## What you will learn

- Apply the relevant safety rules
- Diagnose typical faults
- Document work according to company standards
- Understand the principles behind the topic
- Configure devices with the Kestrel Configurator

## Certification

Modbus Fundamentals ends with a short practical assessment on a demo device.

Participants who do not pass the assessment of Modbus Fundamentals can repeat it at a later date free of charge.

## Course agenda

### Day 1

- Configuration workshop
- Theory block
- Introduction and safety briefing

### Day 2

- Review and questions
- Hands-on exercise on demo devices
- Troubleshooting lab
- Final assessment
