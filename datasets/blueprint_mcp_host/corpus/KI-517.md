# Incident Report INC-2026-125

Kestrel Instruments. Internal use only.

## Incident summary

Incident INC-2026-125 interrupted measurements at Ahlberg Marine Oy and was handled by customer support together with engineering.

Incident INC-2026-125 occurred on 15 September 2025 at Ahlberg Marine Oy. The affected product was the Kestrel KF7 and the incident was classified as severity 1.

## Impact

Data gaps caused by incident INC-2026-125 were documented and shared with the customer.

The final total downtime was 684 minutes.

## Root cause analysis

The root cause was a misconfigured Modbus address.
