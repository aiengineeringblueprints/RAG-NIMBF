# Project Brief PRJ-216: Delta Metering Retrofit

Kestrel Instruments, internal document.

## Context

Ahlberg Marine Oy commissioned project PRJ-216 (Delta Metering Retrofit).

Ahlberg Marine Oy commissioned project PRJ-216 to modernise measurement and monitoring at one of its sites. The existing equipment had reached the end of its service life.

The goal of project PRJ-216 is to improve data quality and reduce maintenance effort for Ahlberg Marine Oy.

## Budget

The initial estimate was 577000 EUR. After the scope review the steering committee approved a budget of 564000 EUR.

## Risk register

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Interfaces to legacy systems | Run an interface test in the first project phase |
| Staff availability during holidays | Plan deputies for all key roles |
| Delayed site access | Agree access windows early with the customer |

## Kickoff notes

- The project plan was reviewed and accepted
- The next steering committee was scheduled
- Open questions were assigned to owners
- The customer presented site access rules
- Reporting format and frequency were agreed
- Interfaces to the control system were discussed

## Scope

Optional services in project PRJ-216, such as extended support, are offered separately.

Civil works and cabling outside the measuring points are not part of project PRJ-216 and are provided by Ahlberg Marine Oy.

## Governance

A steering committee with representatives of Ahlberg Marine Oy and Kestrel meets at every milestone of project PRJ-216.

## Objectives

- Document the installation for later audits
- Reduce manual inspection rounds
- Train the customer's technicians
- Provide remote diagnostics for the service team
- Replace legacy instruments with current Kestrel devices
- Integrate all measuring points into the customer's control system
