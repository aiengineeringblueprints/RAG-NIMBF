# Project Brief PRJ-228: Summit Monitoring Pilot

Kestrel Instruments, internal document.

## Introduction

Rosen Pharma ApS commissioned project PRJ-228 (Summit Monitoring Pilot).

Project PRJ-228 replaces manual readings with continuous monitoring, so that Rosen Pharma ApS can react to deviations faster.

The goal of project PRJ-228 is to improve data quality and reduce maintenance effort for Rosen Pharma ApS.

Rosen Pharma ApS commissioned project PRJ-228 to modernise measurement and monitoring at one of its sites. The existing equipment had reached the end of its service life.

## Budget

The initial estimate was 272500 EUR. After the scope review the steering committee approved a budget of 259500 EUR.

## Objectives

- Integrate all measuring points into the customer's control system
- Reduce manual inspection rounds
- Provide remote diagnostics for the service team
- Document the installation for later audits

## Kickoff notes

- The next steering committee was scheduled
- Open questions were assigned to owners
- The customer presented site access rules

## Communication

Weekly calls between the project teams of Rosen Pharma ApS and Kestrel keep project PRJ-228 on track between steering committee meetings.

Project PRJ-228 uses a shared workspace for documents, meeting minutes and open points.

Decisions in project PRJ-228 are documented in the decision log and confirmed by email.

## Governance

Live figures of project PRJ-228 such as budget spent and percent complete are tracked in the project system, not in this brief.

A steering committee with representatives of Rosen Pharma ApS and Kestrel meets at every milestone of project PRJ-228.

Status reports are shared with Rosen Pharma ApS every two weeks. Change requests follow the standard change process and need written approval.

## Scope of delivery

The scope of project PRJ-228 covers delivery, installation and commissioning of Kestrel KV4 devices and their integration into the customer's control system.

Optional services in project PRJ-228, such as extended support, are offered separately.

Project PRJ-228 includes training for the customer's maintenance staff and a documentation package for audits.

Civil works and cabling outside the measuring points are not part of project PRJ-228 and are provided by Rosen Pharma ApS.
