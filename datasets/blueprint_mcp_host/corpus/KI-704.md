# Training Course: Gateway Configuration

Kestrel Instruments. Internal use only.

## Course overview

The course Gateway Configuration lasts 5 days and is taught by Selma Nilsen.

The course Gateway Configuration gives participants the practical knowledge they need in daily work with Kestrel products.

Participants of Gateway Configuration work in small groups, so trainers can respond to individual questions.

It accepts at most 20 participants and costs 950 EUR per person.

The course has no prerequisites.

## Materials

After Gateway Configuration participants keep access to the recorded theory blocks for later reference.

Demo devices and tools for Gateway Configuration are provided by Kestrel; participants do not need to bring equipment apart from a laptop.

Participants of Gateway Configuration receive the course slides and exercise descriptions in digital form.

## Target audience

Customers, partners and Kestrel employees can register for Gateway Configuration.

Participants of Gateway Configuration should be familiar with basic measurement technology.

Gateway Configuration is aimed at technicians and engineers who install, configure or maintain Kestrel products.

## Learning objectives

- Understand the principles behind the topic
- Document work according to company standards
- Configure devices with the Kestrel Configurator
- Use the service portal efficiently
- Apply the relevant safety rules
- Plan maintenance tasks

## Assessment

Participants who do not pass the assessment of Gateway Configuration can repeat it at a later date free of charge.
